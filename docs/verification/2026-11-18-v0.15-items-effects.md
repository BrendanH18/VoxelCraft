## Implemented

**Effects.** Glowing, Levitation, Health Boost, Wind Charged, Weaving,
Oozing and Infested now act, for players (host, CLI agents, pads and LAN
players through the shared session) and mobs:

- *Glowing* outlines the entity in white through walls. Glowing mobs' and
  players' geometry is copied while the entity mesh is built
  (`EntityMesh::glow_start`/`glow_end`); on frames with any, the renderer
  draws it into a one-channel mask before the main pass and a full-screen
  pass turns pixels within two of the silhouette white
  (`render/outline.rs`, `shaders/outline.wgsl`, `fs_mask` in
  `shaders/entity.wgsl`). Frames without a glowing entity do no extra work.
  LAN clients get a mob's Glowing with its look.
- *Levitation*: `travelInAir` eases vertical motion 0.2 of the way to
  `0.05 * (amplifier + 1)` a tick before the 0.98 drag, for the player
  (exactly) and mobs (per-second form); falls are reset while levitating.
- *Health Boost* adds 4 maximum health per level to players and mobs; the
  HUD shows the extra hearts; when it ends, health above the new maximum
  goes; health above 20 survives a save while the effect does.
- *Wind Charged*: a killed body bursts with strength `3 + nextFloat() * 2`
  at half its height, through the shared wind burst (triggers blocks,
  pushes, hurts nothing).
- *Weaving*: a killed player always, and a mob with `mob_griefing`, leaves
  2–3 cobwebs among 15 random cells of the 3×3×3 cube around it (replaceable
  cells over a sturdy top). Players under Weaving are held less by cobwebs
  (0.5, 0.25, 0.5).
- *Oozing*: a killed body leaves two size-2 slimes half a block above its
  feet, limited by `max_entity_cramming` against slimes within 2 blocks
  (`OozingMobEffect.numberOfSlimesToSpawn`, including Java's `Mth.clamp`
  argument order). The gamerule is passed to the entities each tick.
- *Infested*: each non-lethal hit rolls 0.1 for 1–2 silverfish thrown out
  along the look direction.

Death effects run once, when a body starts dying, and clear its effects as
Java does; a body discarded rather than killed (an exploding creeper, an
expired trap horse) keeps them.

**Potions.** Wind charged, weaving, oozing and infested potions (indices
41–44) as drinkable (items 1650–1653) and splash (1654–1657) potions,
lingering potions and tipped arrows (the existing stack component), with
Java's names and colours. Awkward potion plus breeze rod, cobweb, slime
block or stone brews them; gunpowder and dragon's breath convert them.
`tests/java_brewing.rs` now matches all 279 recipes both ways.

**Spectral arrows** (item 1555): the recipe (four glowstone dust round an
arrow, two arrows), shooting from bows (Infinity doesn't spare them, as
Java's applies only to plain arrows), crossbows (the charged crossbow keeps
a spectral bolt) and dispensers (dispensers also now shoot tipped arrows
with their potion), trial-chamber item spawners, 200 ticks of Glowing on
the mob or player hit, pickup as a spectral arrow, a gold head on the model,
piglin bartering (40 weight, 6–12) and the bastion chest entries.

**Coloured eggs and farm variants.** Chickens, cows and pigs have Java's
temperate, warm and cold variants: natural and generation spawns pick by
biome (the expanded warm and cold farm-animal tags), babies take a random
parent's variant, and the variant is saved by name (`farm_variant`,
absent for temperate and older saves) and sent to LAN clients. Warm
chickens lay brown eggs (1560) and cold ones blue (1559); blue and brown
eggs stack to 16, are thrown (new thrown kinds) and hatch chicks of their
variant, count as `#eggs` in the cake and pumpkin pie, and draw with their
own shell colours. The variants' look is an original recolour: warm
animals ruddier, cold ones paler.

**Frosted ice and Frost Walker.** Block states 12256–12259 (ages 0–3,
original textures at layers 4000–4003): slippery (0.98), hardness 0.5,
no drops or item, glass sounds, a polar bear spawn floor, beside sugar cane
like ice, melting to water (to air in the Nether) when broken over support.
Each change of state schedules a persisted tick 60–120 ticks out; a tick
ages the block (one time in three, or with fewer than four frosted
neighbours) when the light exceeds `11 - age - 1` (block light alone in the
End), an aged-out block melts and pushes its frosted neighbours to age, and
frosted ice left with fewer than two frosted neighbours when one melts or
is removed melts at once. Frost Walker boots (the existing enchantment)
freeze still water with air above into age-0 frosted ice in a disk of
radius `2 + level` below a player who moves into another block on the
ground and not riding, skipping cells a mob overlaps. Chunks loaded without
a saved tick reschedule their frosted ice.

**Armor trims.** The 18 templates (1601–1618, 64 a stack, Java's rarities),
their copy recipes (seven diamonds, the template and its block), the
smithing table's trim recipe (`content::trim::apply`: one armor piece with
the trim, every other component kept, no result for the same trim again)
on the desktop and pad screens and the CLI agent's `smithing` command, the
11 trim materials and their items (`#trim_materials`), the trim stored in
the stack's existing `trim` component and saved by name, original pattern
masks drawn on armor worn by players, zombies, skeletons and piglins (36
new armor-sheet layers, tinted by material, darker on armor of the same
material), bands in the material's colour on armor-stand shells, and the
tooltips (`Upgrade:`, pattern and material in the material's colour; the
template's applies-to and ingredients lines, its name in its rarity's
colour). Templates now fill their chest entries in bastions, desert and
jungle pyramids, pillager outposts, shipwrecks, nether fortresses,
strongholds, ancient cities and trial chamber vaults; elder guardians drop
the tide template one time in five.

## Differences from Java

- The Glowing outline is a two-pixel ring drawn in one pass from a mask of
  the glowing entities' geometry; Java blurs a team-coloured silhouette
  through its `entity_outline` post chain. Teams don't exist, so the colour
  is always white. A player's own outline shows in third person only on the
  first view; split-screen views other than the host's don't capture their
  own player.
- Mob physics are per second, so a levitating mob's rise follows Java's
  per-tick easing in closed form rather than tick by tick; mobs aren't
  slowed by cobwebs at all yet, so Weaving's cobweb speed applies to
  players only. Mobs wearing Frost Walker boots don't freeze water.
- Frost Walker runs when the player's block position changes, like Java's
  `location_changed`; Java also runs it when the boots are put on.
  Unobstructed checks mobs' boxes, not other players or vehicles.
- Infested's roll for players happens once a tick on the hits since the
  previous one, and poison and wither damage (applied inside the effect
  tick) don't roll it.
- Farm variants are chosen for natural and generation spawns only; spawns
  by command or spawn egg (not yet in the game) stay temperate. Cold
  chickens and cold and warm cows and pigs use Java's normal model shape
  recoloured; Java's cold and warm model types differ in shape.
- Trim pattern masks are original procedural line motifs, not Java's
  textures; armor stands show trims as bands on their simplified shells.
  The trim layer has no enchantment glint of its own.
- The template's tooltip omits Java's two slot descriptions (shown in the
  smithing screen's empty slots, which this screen doesn't draw).
