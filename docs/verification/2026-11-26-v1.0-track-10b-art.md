## What changed

1. **93 tiles redrawn in place, in Track 10's approved style**, by a new
   child of Track 10's generator,
   [`src/render/final_art_b_textures.rs`](../../src/render/final_art_b_textures.rs).
   It uses Track 10's primitives (sprites lit at the upper left and shaded
   at the lower right, leaves, blobs, stalks, stamps), so its tiles share
   the material cache, the pinned-checksum test and the memory budget.
   Integer maths, plus IEEE square roots for the fan ribs, which every
   platform computes exactly. No layer was added and none of the reserved
   layers 4066–4079 was used.
   - **Flat ground plants**, seen from above: the **lily pad** (a round
     pad with a notch, a darker rim and raised veins); **pink petals**
     (four-petalled blooms joined by thin stems, a few fallen petals);
     **wildflowers** with one to four segments (stems, leaves and small
     yellow, white and pale violet blooms) and **leaf litter** with one to
     four segments (fallen leaves in browns and ambers, each with a
     midrib). Each segment fills one quadrant, and a higher count keeps
     every texel of the lower ones.
   - **Vines**: three swaying stems with lobed leaves on alternate sides,
     seamless both ways so walls of vine join up. **Glow lichen**:
     irregular grey-green patches lit at their upper left, with a glowing
     knot in each; seamless.
   - **Corals**, alive and dead (dead ones keep the living shape in one
     bleached grey): the **coral blocks** each with their own relief
     (packed tubes, meandering brain ridges, bubbles, leaning flame
     streaks, rippling horn plates); the **coral plants** (tube: tubes
     with dark mouths; brain: rounded lobes; bubble: bubbles on thin
     stalks; fire: forked flame branches; horn: thick antlers with blunt
     tips); the **coral fans** (a pie-slice of laced webbing braced by lit
     ribs, the rim scalloped between rib tips, each kind opening to its
     own width); and the **coral wall fans**, which are the same fan
     turned to their wall, within Java's 11-texel outline.
   - **Sea pickles**, one to four: olive capsules lit on their left with
     bumps, each capped by a glowing tip.
   - **Bamboo**: the stalk (a cane lit down its left, with nodes at rows 2
     and 12, clear of the rows its top face samples) and the small and
     large leaves (leaves sprouting from the top, their sheaths wrapping
     the post). The post samples columns 6–9 only, and those stay opaque
     on every row.
   - **Potted torchflower** (its own layer, 3443): the pot exactly as every
     other potted plant's (`utility::potted_side`), with Track 10's
     torchflower squeezed onto the cross planes as the other pots do.
   - **Ender chest** (front, side, top): built like Track 10's chest,
     obsidian with violet glints in a teal trim, the lid's seam on the
     sides and front, and an eye for a latch.

   Tint behaviour: none of these layers is in `tex::FOLIAGE`, the only
   layers the biome tints recolour, so no tint applied before and none
   applies now (Java tints the lily pad, vines and leaf litter; this game
   doesn't, before or after this round).
2. **The old drawings are removed.** The material lookup comes first in
   `textures::pixel`, so the previous drawings of every layer Tracks 10
   and 10b redrew were unreachable. Removed:
   - whole generators that drew only redrawn layers:
     `crop_textures.rs`, `plant_textures.rs` (the biome plants) and
     `sniffer_plant_textures.rs`;
   - the redrawn layers' arms and their now unused helpers in
     `textures.rs` (doors, the oak trapdoor, the pale oak and bamboo doors
     and trapdoors, chests, mushrooms, sugar cane, fern, blue orchid,
     tall grass, dandelion and poppy, dead bush, wheat, carrots,
     potatoes, nether wart, the saplings, anvils, TNT, and the village
     range's dirt path, hay bale and bell), `overworld_textures.rs`,
     `nether_textures.rs`, `gen5_textures.rs`, `cave_textures.rs`,
     `utility_textures.rs` and `wood_textures.rs` (the seven trapdoors,
     a tenth file Track 10's record didn't name);
   - the wither rose and eyeblossom sprites in `src/world/wither_rose.rs`
     and `src/world/eyeblossom.rs`, reached only for those redrawn layers
     (their pots still draw through `potted_side`).

   Proof: the new ignored test `render::textures::tests::layer_digests`
   writes an FNV-1a checksum of every block layer (all 4,066, through
   `textures::pixel`). Between the art commit and the cleanup, every layer
   is identical except **layer 485**, an unused third face slot of the
   hay bale (the hay bale uses 483 and 484) that the old hay code drew; it
   now shows the missing texture like the village range's other unused
   slots. Between the base and the art commit exactly the 93 layers of
   this round changed. The pinned-checksum test still pins all 670
   earlier material tiles unchanged.
3. **Vines are drawn in the alpha-tested pass** (`src/mesh.rs`). They
   are a shaped plate with see-through texels, but the mesher put them in
   the opaque pass, so the gaps between the leaves showed black: in this
   round's hanging-plants capture, before and after the new art. One line
   in the mesher's see-through list, and a test.
4. **Capture scenes**, appended to `tools/capture_scenes.json`
   (`materials` group, 1920 × 1080, random ticks off):
   `material-ground-plants` (lily pads on a pool, pink petals,
   wildflowers and leaf litter with one to four segments, bamboo stalks
   of three heights with their leaves, a bamboo shoot, glow lichen on the
   ground, a torchflower beside the potted torchflower, an ender chest
   beside a chest), `material-hanging-plants` (under a stone overhang:
   cave vines with and without berries, a spore blossom, hanging roots,
   pale hanging moss and weeping vines hanging from the ceiling; a
   curtain of vines and patches of glow lichen on the back wall, lichen on
   the ceiling and floor, twisting vines) and `material-water-plants`
   (inside a glass tank of water: the five coral blocks alive and dead,
   each with its coral on top, its wall fan on the front face and its fan
   behind; one to four sea pickles, seagrass, tall seagrass and kelp).
   The hanging and water plants now show in a scene, which Track 10's
   record listed as a gap. `tests/capture_scenes.rs` now accepts raw
   state ids in fills as well as places.
5. **Review sheet:** the ignored test
   `render::textures::tests::final_art_b_sheet` writes this round's 93
   layers, 8× and as a 2 × 2 repeat, to `$VC_FINAL_ART_B_SHEET`.
6. **Asset manifest:** `procedural_resources.material_art` records the
   round: 762 tiles, within the unchanged 768 KiB cache budget.

## Sources

No Minecraft texture was opened, copied or traced. Every design, palette
and shape is original and follows [the art and UI guide](../art-and-ui.md).
Model layouts (which texels the bamboo post, the wall fan plate and the
pot sample) are VoxelCraft's own and unchanged. The wall fan's reach of
11 texels is the outline already recorded in `world::utilities`. No Java
behaviour changed, so no Java fixture was read.

## Tests added

In `src/render/final_art_b_textures.rs`:

- `the_round_replaces_existing_layers_only`: the 93 layers are distinct,
  none of them Track 10's, all below the block array's 4,066, which
  hasn't grown; each is drawn.
- `sprites_are_shaded_and_cut_out`: the flat plants, vines, lichen, coral
  plants, fans and wall fans, sea pickles and the potted torchflower are
  cut-out sprites with at least three shades.
- `segments_fill_one_quadrant_each_and_nest`: each wildflower and leaf
  litter segment adds texels only in its own quadrant and keeps every
  texel of the counts below it.
- `dead_coral_keeps_the_living_shape_in_grey`: coral blocks are opaque
  and differ by kind; every dead block, plant and fan has its living
  twin's alpha and is grey.
- `wall_fans_turn_with_their_wall`: each facing is the north one turned,
  reaching at most 10 texels out.
- `bamboo_keeps_a_solid_cane`: columns 6–9 opaque on every row of all
  three tiles; the leafy ones differ from the bare stalk.
- `each_sea_pickle_glows`: one glowing tip per pickle.
- `the_potted_torchflower_shares_the_pot`: the pot texels equal
  `potted_side`'s; the bloom shows above it.
- `the_ender_chest_has_an_eye_for_a_latch`.

In `src/mesh.rs`: `vines_are_cutout`. In `src/render/textures.rs`
(ignored tools): `final_art_b_sheet` and `layer_digests`.

Extended: `render::textures::material::tests::redrawn_tiles_are_deterministic_and_pinned`

## Gaps and open decisions

- **The owner's look approval** of this round and the owner's ordinary
  play.
- **Not redrawn:** amethyst buds (outside both rounds' lists) and the
  potted bamboo, whose pot layer draws its own flat cane (`utility`'s
  `POTTED_BAMBOO`) instead of following the bamboo tile. Open decision for
  the owner: whether either belongs in a later round.
- **Model limits, unchanged:** the bamboo post is a single 4 × 4 column,
  so its leaves can only be suggested on the post (Java draws them as
  crossed planes beside it); the coral wall fan is a flat plate out from
  its wall; pink petals have one state (no petal count or facing); the
  lily pad, vines and leaf litter take no biome tint in this game. Each
  is a model or behaviour gap for the roadmap, not an art one.
- **Vine icon:** the fix covers placed vines in the world. The vine's
  item icon and its look in the hand weren't checked.
- **Budget:** the material cache holds 762 tiles (762 KiB of 768 KiB
  budgeted), so a further round of more than six tiles must raise
  `cpu_cache_bytes` in the asset manifest.
- **Frame time:** the round adds no layers and no per-frame work: 93 more
  tiles are built once at startup (93 KiB more in the tile cache), and
  vines move from the opaque to the alpha-tested pass. Startup cost and
  the frame-time gate stay to be measured on the reference Mac.
- Captures are software-rendered and appearance only.
