## Implemented paths

Hearts retain axis, activity and natural state in chunks. Two pale oak log-tag
blocks with matching axes activate a heart; an existing protector retains its
binding when the logs are removed. The authoritative day clock uses the pinned
12600-inclusive / 23401-exclusive creaking-active interval. Both natural and
player-built hearts can spawn a non-persistent protector near a player at night;
Peaceful and the existing spawning switch prevent new protectors. There are five
spawn attempts, within 16 blocks horizontally and eight vertically, on solid
non-leaf support. Hearts retain one protector UID, compute comparator output
`15 - floor(clamp(distance, 0, 32) / 32 * 15)` and notify the circuit on changes.

The dimension saves `creaking_hearts`; the mob component saves its home position
and appearance state through the existing name-based mob store. Chunk registration
and edits maintain a loaded-heart set; unloaded records remain saved and do not
tick. Console/agent summons use the common name registry. Mob kind and component
appearance replicate through the existing LAN snapshots.

Creakings have one health, a 0.9 × 2.7 collision box, movement attribute 0.4,
attack damage 3, follow range 32 and navigation step 1.0625. A survival player's
look can activate one within 12 blocks; an active one freezes when any eligible
player within 32 looks through its fixed 60-degree cone at one of three heights.
Frozen creakings do not travel or attack and cannot be pushed by mob separation.
Bound creakings ignore ordinary damage and knockback; player-credited hits produce
an eight-tick twitch and notify the heart. They do not distance-despawn while
bound. Breaking the heart initiates a 45-tick twitch/teardown without mob loot.
Original cuboids, procedural bark and amber eyes render the creature; its ambient,
hurt and death calls use original synthesized wood resonances and crackles.

An awake hurt heart tries 2–3 resin placements, then waits 100 ticks before another
emission. A shuffled breadth-first search visits pale oak logs up to depth two,
with a 64-position bound, adding one face to a neighboring clump, air cell or
water source. Clumps reuse glow lichen's placement, support loss, selection,
non-collision and paired waterlogged-state code. Mining gives one clump per face.
A heart gives 1–3 clumps plus a uniform Fortune bonus (maximum nine), or the heart
under Silk Touch. Resin construction has creative entries, shared placement and
mining, recipes, brick smelting (0.3 XP), stonecutting recipe data, the complete
stairs/slab state families and the project's four-state wall representation.

## Unfinished work and differences

- No eyeblossom blocks, pots, dyes, propagation, stew/mooshroom/bee inputs or
  eyeblossom generation were added. Existing name-based hooks are retained.
- No natural heart generation was added. The current pale oak shape is a 2×2
  trunk with no log cell enclosed on all six sides. Java's decorator shuffles
  its logs and requires all six neighbors to be logs; the 10% selected creaking
  tree uses decorator probability 1.0. A faithful generation-3 tree port is needed
  before that decorator can provide the survival source. Generations 1/2 remain
  unchanged. No approximate replacement layout was introduced.
- Runtime choices use the engine's SplitMix64/entity and world random streams,
  not Java's exact random sequence. The sampled counts and bounds follow Java.
- The creaking brain is a bounded pursuit implementation using existing ground
  navigation. It does not port Java's complete brain activity scheduling, idle
  stroll/look selectors, home path evaluation or player-stuck sensor.
- The watch sensor currently clips the shared collision shapes rather than
  Java's VISUAL shape mode; transparent-block and disguise-item cases remain open.
  Player eye height is derived from the shared pose dimensions. Activation and
  watch sensing run each simulation step rather than Java sensor cadence.
- Heart state refresh/day/range removal currently run each simulation step;
  spawn retries use a 20–24-tick delay. Spawn placement tests three air cells
  instead of Java's complete SpawnUtil collision strategy. Day/range removal
  shares teardown with heart breaking rather than Java's immediate discard path.
  Teardown keeps health until removal rather than setting zero health at its start.
- Heart unresolved-UID grace is represented as loaded time. Cross-chunk protector
  unload/reload, replacement hearts, naming persistence and dimension transitions
  still need broader lifecycle evidence.
- Resin trails are original transient particle requests, not Java's exact
  bidirectional trail/heart-call timings; raw particle requests are not LAN
  replicated. Specialized spawn/attack/step/heart-call sounds and emissive eye
  rendering remain unfinished. Only ambient/hurt/death calls exist.
- Natural-heart mining awards the reference 20–24 XP, including Silk Touch.
  Player-credited explosion XP is not yet wired.
- Damage-source distinctions (projectile owner, pet credit, invulnerability bypass,
  potion effects and attacks lacking a knockback source) need further parity work.
- The stonecutter's interactive screen is already deferred to v0.17; this slice
  provides its resin recipe data.
- Owner play, graphical/sound inspection, process-restart and representative
  simulation performance evidence remain unverified. No feature is marked Verified.
