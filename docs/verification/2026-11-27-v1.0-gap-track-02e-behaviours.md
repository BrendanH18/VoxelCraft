## What changed

### 1. Brain idle movement (`src/entity/mob/brain_walk.rs`)

- **`WALK_TARGET` and `MoveToTargetSink`** for brain mobs
  (`Mob::walking`, never saved): a walk target on a point, a mob (its feet,
  refreshed by the sense pass; a gone one is erased) or a player, with its
  speed modifier and `closeEnoughDist`. The sink plans a path to the
  target's block (or toward it with `DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards(10, 7,
  pi/2)`), erases the target once `distManhattan` is within reach or no
  path exists, walks until the path is done or it times out (150 to 250
  ticks; the babies' play package 80 to 120, the sniffer's 500 to 700),
  plans again when the target moved more than 2 blocks, and waits
  `nextInt(40)` ticks after getting stuck. A target set by a movement
  behaviour is walked from the next tick, as Java's core sink starts before
  the activity's behaviours; its `LOOK_TARGET` is followed from the next
  tick too.
- **Movement `RunOne`s** (`GateBehavior`, `SHUFFLED`, `RUN_ONE`): each tick
  without a running option they shuffle by weight and start the first
  option that can; one-shot options stop at once, `DoNothing`, `JumpOnBed`
  and the sniffer's scent and sniff hold them.
- **Villagers** (no more random wandering): the idle package's `RunOne` at
  priority 2, before `SetLookAndInteract`: `InteractWith` a villager (2), a
  villager that can breed (1) or a cat (1) within 8 of those it sees (speed
  0.5, 2 short; it looks at it and, for a villager, `INTERACTION_TARGET`
  holds off `SetLookAndInteract` for `TradeWithVillager`'s 60 ticks),
  `VillageBoundRandomStroll` (10 and 7 inside a village, else toward the
  closest village section within two), `SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget(0.5,
  2)`, `JumpOnBed` (babies: walk to the nearest bed, 100 ticks to reach it,
  3 to 6 jumps 5 ticks apart) and `DoNothing(30, 60)`; the babies' play
  package `RunOne` (5, after the look `RunOne`: weights 2, 1, 1, 1, 2 and
  `DoNothing(20, 40)` 2); within six blocks of its bell, the meet package's
  `TriggerGate` of `StrollAroundPoi(MEETING_POINT, 0.4, 40)` (8 and 6, every
  180 ticks) and `SocializeAtBell` (one in 100, a villager within
  `sqrt(32)`, speed 0.3); and, resting without a home, the rest package's
  `RunOne`: `SetClosestHomeAsWalkTarget` (the closest bed within 48, claimed
  or not, more than 2 blocks off and reachable, checked at most every 20 to
  39 ticks), `InsideBrownianWalk` (a covered standable cell next to it,
  under cover only), `GoToClosestVillage(0.5, 4)` and `DoNothing(20, 40)`.
  The villager's brain activity falls back to idle (looks and movement)
  when work has no job site or a meeting no bell
  (`setActiveActivityIfPossible`), as for a nitwit at work time.
  `Villager.createAttributes`' 0.5 movement speed sets the walks' speed.
- **Piglins** (no more random wandering when idle): the idle movement
  `RunOne` after the look `RunOne` (`RandomStroll.stroll(0.6)` 2,
  `InteractWith` a piglin within 8 at 0.6 and 2 short 2,
  `SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget(0.6, 3)` unless a player holding a loved
  item is seen 2, `DoNothing(30, 60)` 1), and the celebrating piglin's
  `RunOne` (`SetEntityLookTarget(PIGLIN, 8)`, `RandomStroll.stroll(0.6, 2,
  1)`, `DoNothing(10, 20)`, 1 each) in place of 2c's stand-in. The walks
  use the piglin's 0.35 (babies' raised by 0.2 of the base).
- **Sniffers**: the idle `RunOne` (`SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget(1.0, 3)` 2,
  `Scenting(40, 80)` 1, `Sniffing(40, 80)` 1, `SetEntityLookTarget(PLAYER,
  6)` 1, `RandomStroll.stroll(1.0)` 1, `DoNothing(5, 20)` 2) replaces the
  game's fixed 100-tick idle wait and 50/50 pick and 2d's look stand-in.
  As in Java, the scent may start during the 9600-tick `SNIFF_COOLDOWN`
  (only the sniff and the dig wait it out), the sniff needs no walk target
  and an adult that can sniff, and a sniff that times out picks the dig
  spot (the game's own search and dig follow). The idle sniffer walks its
  walk targets with `MoveToTargetSink(500, 700)` instead of wandering.

### 2. The fox (`src/entity/fox.rs`, `src/entity/mob/fox.rs`)

- **States** (`Mob::fox`): Java's flags `sitting`, `crouching`,
  `interested`, `pouncing`, `faceplanted` (sleeping stays the animal's
  `sleeping`), `crouchAmount` (0.2 a tick to 5) and `interestedAngle`
  (0.4 of the way a tick) with their last-tick values, as `Fox.tick` eases
  them. `aiStep` ends the crouch and interest without a target; water or a
  target wakes it; water or sleep ends sitting.
- **Targets**: the variant's `NearestAttackableTargetGoal`s (red: chickens
  and rabbits, then baby turtles on land, at 4, schooling fish at 6; snow:
  fish at 4, the others at 6), each one time in `reducedTickDelay(10)` on
  the fox's full updates, the nearest to its eyes within 32 blocks, no
  sight needed, kept while alive and within 32.
- **Goals** in their priorities beside the shared table (panic 2, breed 3,
  avoid 4, follow parent 8, stroll 11), each holding `MOVE`; a goal starts
  only over strictly worse ones, and the table's goals don't start under a
  better fox goal:
  - `FaceplantGoal` (1): 40 ticks still after a pounce into snow.
  - `StalkPreyGoal` (5): farther than 6 blocks from a chicken or rabbit,
    it walks at 1.5 looking at it; within 6 it crouches, interested, and
    stops; leaving the goal keeps the crouch only if `isPathClear`.
  - `FoxPounceGoal` (6, not interruptable): fully crouched with a clear
    path, it leaps (`+0.8` toward the target, `+0.9` up), looks at the
    target (60, 30), tilts its pitch with its flight, bites within 2
    blocks, and landing nose down on a snow layer faceplants and drops the
    target; a blocked path ends the crouch and interest.
  - `SeekShelterGoal` (6): every 100 updates by day under open sky outside
    a village, it walks at 1.25 to a dark covered spot (`FleeSunGoal`'s ten
    tries).
  - `FoxMeleeAttackGoal` (7): not while sitting, sleeping, crouching or
    faceplanted; `MeleeAttackGoal` at 1.2 (20-tick `canUse` checks, the
    attack box, 20 ticks between bites of 2).
  - `SleepGoal` (7): still for a tick, after its countdown, by day, under
    cover (no sky over the top of its box and a walk value of at least 0),
    with nothing alerting it within 12 (`FoxAlertableEntitiesSelector`:
    prey, monsters, untamed pets, players not trusted or sneaking, any
    other mob awake) and out of powder snow; it sleeps until that ends.
  - `FoxEatBerriesGoal` (10): a `MoveToBlockGoal(1.2, 12, 1)` to a sweet
    berry bush of age 2 or more or glow berries, rechecked every 100 to 200
    updates; 40 ticks beside it, then (with `mobGriefing`) the bush gives
    one to three berries (one more at age 3), the first into an empty
    mouth, and goes back to age 1; glow berries drop off the vine.
  - `LeapAtTargetGoal(0.4)` (10), `FoxSearchForItemsGoal` (11: an item
    within 8, at 1.2, `canContinueToUse` being `canUse`),
    `PerchAndSearchGoal` (13: 2% a full update with nothing alerting it,
    it sits and looks around 2 to 4 times for 80 to 99 ticks each).
  - `FoxBreedGoal` and `FoxFollowParentGoal` clear the states when they
    start; `FoxMoveControl` holds a sleeping, sitting or faceplanted fox
    still.
- **Look rules**: `FoxLookAtPlayerGoal` doesn't start while faceplanted
  or interested; `FoxLookControl.resetXRotOnTick` keeps the pitch while
  pouncing, crouching, interested or faceplanted; the look control rests
  while it sleeps (as before).
- **Saves**: `Sleeping`, `Sitting` and `Crouching` are written as
  `fox_sleeping`, `fox_sitting` and `fox_crouching`, only when set.

### 3. Baby boxes and the shown trade (`src/entity/mob/eyes.rs`, `src/entity/mob/head.rs`)

- **Each kind's `BABY_DIMENSIONS`** for the box (collision, hit box,
  navigation): villagers, zombies, husks, drowned, zombie villagers,
  piglins and zombified piglins 0.49 by 0.98; chickens 0.3 by 0.4; rabbits
  0.24 by 0.4; hoglins and zoglins 0.75 by 0.85; squid and glow squid 0.5
  by 0.5; foxes and armadillos the adult's times 0.6; horses, skeleton and
  zombie horses 0.7; turtles 0.3; dolphins 0.65; the ghastling 0.2375; the
  others' own boxes equal the adult's halved (sheep, goats, cats, ocelots,
  pigs, cows, mooshrooms, polar bears, axolotls, wolves, striders, pandas,
  llamas, donkeys, mules, nautiluses), and the rest halve it
  (`getAgeScale`). Eyes were Track 2d's.
- **`ShowTradesToPlayer`'s held item**: when the player's main-hand item
  changes to one that pays for offers in stock, the villager holds up the
  first offer's result in its main hand (drop chance 0) and looks for 900
  ticks, cycling through the matching results every 40 ticks; with nothing
  to show its hand is cleared (drop chance 0.085) and it looks at most 40
  more; stopping (or the activity ending) clears it. It is saved and sent
  to LAN clients with the equipment, as Java's main hand is.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Villagers**: the work package's `RunOne` (`StrollAroundPoi`,
  `StrollToPoi` and `StrollToPoiList` around the job site, `UseBonemeal`)
  isn't ported: a working villager still stands at its job site (the game's
  `WorkAtPoi` and `HarvestFarmland` stand-ins run there). The panic, raid,
  pre-raid and hide packages' strolls are the game's own fleeing and
  hiding. `PlayTagWithOtherKids` isn't ported: babies always run the play
  `RunOne` (Java runs it only while no other baby is seen).
  `TradeWithVillager` holds the interaction for its 60 ticks but doesn't
  lock both villagers' gazes and walks together or gossip and throw food
  (the game's own sharing pass does that). `NearestBedSensor` is the nearest
  bed within 48, refreshed every second, without its path check;
  `GoToClosestVillage` heads for the closest village section within two
  (`DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards(15, 7)`) rather than scoring
  `LandRandomPos` by section distance; `SetClosestHomeAsWalkTarget` plans
  one path to the closest bed rather than to five at a time. The game's own
  walks (home, job site, bell, crops, a partner, fleeing) keep their
  2 blocks a second.
- **Piglins**: the avoid activity's movement `RunOne` and the brute's idle
  (`StrollToPoi` home, `RandomStroll`) aren't ported (fleeing piglins run
  the game's flight, brutes still wander); the game's walk back toward a
  piglin's home stands.
- **Sniffers** still stand still while scenting or sniffing (the game's
  rule).
- **Foxes**: `DefendTrustedTargetGoal` (no player attacker is tracked
  for it), `FoxStrollThroughVillageGoal`, `ClimbOnTopOfPowderSnowGoal`,
  thunder (waking, seeking shelter) and the fox's own sounds (bite, sniff,
  aggro, spit, eat) and faceplant particles aren't ported; an asleep fox
  still doesn't flee players (the game's rule). `getMotionDirection` is
  `getDirection` for every animal, so the pounce's direction check always
  passes, as in Java. The pounce's and leap's flight is damped 0.91 a tick
  (`travelInAir`'s air friction), which the game's generic air steering
  otherwise leaves out.
- **Boxes**: the goat's long-jump pose (0.7) has no pose in the game to
  apply to.
- **Shown trade**: after a reload the villager keeps showing a saved item
  until its next `ShowTradesToPlayer` stops, as Java's main hand does.
- 2c's and 2d's other remaining differences stand.

## What remains unverified

No one played any of this: villagers meeting, strolling, jumping on beds
and socialising at the bell, piglins strolling and celebrating, sniffers
scenting and sniffing, foxes stalking, pouncing, faceplanting, sleeping,
perching and eating berries, baby mobs' boxes, and villagers holding up
trades. No screenshots were taken; the fox's crouch, pounce and head tilt
and the held trade are drawn by Track 7d. Performance: each brain mob's
movement `RunOne` shuffles at most seven options a tick and its walk plans
a path at most when a target is set or moves 2 blocks; villagers outside a
village look up the closest village section (up to 125 section lookups)
every 20 ticks, babies their nearest bed every second, a homeless resting
villager the beds every 20 to 39 ticks when it picks that option; each fox
looks for alerting mobs within 12 blocks every tick and for targets within
32 one full update in five through the mob index, and its berry search
scans up to 432 cells every 100 to 200 updates.
