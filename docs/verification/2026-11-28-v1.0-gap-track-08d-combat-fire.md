## Scope

What [Track 8c](2026-11-27-v1.0-gap-track-08c-details.md#remaining-differences)
left:

1. Mobs' hurt cooldown as Java's `LivingEntity.hurtServer`, with the arrow
   and trident refusal it causes; players checked against it; every
   damage source through it.
2. Every projectile Java lets catch fire catching fire in fire or lava,
   drawn burning, and lighting campfires, TNT and candles.
3. Burning mobs and players drawn with Java's flame (`FlameFeatureRenderer`),
   in third person and LAN views, replacing the rising cubes.
4. Arrows bouncing off creative players; ownerless TNT's "blew up"; the
   sign editor, a lectern (and any other screen) pausing an unshared world
   only where Java's `isPauseScreen` does.

All four are done; what remains is [listed below](#remaining-differences).

## What changed

### Mobs' hurt cooldown (item 1)

`Mob` carries `damage_cooldown` (Java's `damageCooldownTime`, in ticks)
and `last_hurt`, neither saved (Java saves neither). `take_damage`, the
one place every kind of mob damage ends, now does what `hurtServer` does
after its refusals and before `actuallyHurt`:

- outside the first half of the cooldown (10 or less left) a hit lands in
  full: `last_hurt` is its amount and the cooldown 20;
- inside it, a hit no harder than `last_hurt` does nothing, and a harder
  one lands only by its excess, which then goes through armor, Resistance,
  enchantments and absorption as before; `last_hurt` becomes the new
  amount.

The amount compared is the one `hurtServer` has: after `Armadillo`'s
halving and before any armor. Only a full hit flashes red (`hurtTime`),
kicks the legs (the damage event), plays the hurt sound and takes
`hurtServer`'s own knockback; a hit that kills inside the cooldown dies
without the death sound. The attacker's extra knockback (Knockback,
sprinting, the sweep) lands with any hit that lands, as `causeExtraKnockback`
follows a `true` `hurtServer` ([`Mob::damage_knocked`](../../src/entity/mob.rs)).
The cooldown counts down once a tick (`LivingEntity.baseTick`).
`refuses_hit` includes the cooldown, so:

- **arrows bounce** off a mob refusing them that way, as off the refusals
  Track 8c added; **tridents** bounce with their hit sound and deal
  nothing, as before for refusals;
- a player's melee hit, a mob's (`resolve_mob_hits`) and the sweep that
  the cooldown refuses leave no trace: no blame, anger, kill credit or
  reactions.

Every source of mob damage now reaches it, as each does in Java through
`hurtServer`:

- **Lava** and **fire** hurt every tick they are touched (`lavaHurt`, 4;
  `BaseFireBlock.entityInside`, 1), which the cooldown spaces to one in ten
  ticks; burning outside lava hurts once a second as before. (They ran on
  their own half-second timers; the first touch of fire hit at once.)
- **Water** hurts endermen, blazes, striders and **snow golems** every
  tick in water or rain (`drown` 1), spaced by the cooldown. Snow golems
  lost 20 health a second in water; it is now 1 a half second, as in Java.
  Their melting in hot places is `onFire` 1, spaced the same way.
- **Bees** drowning, **lightning** (each tick of the bolt, now as
  `lightningBolt` damage, so armor counts) and a **sulfur cube**'s contact
  no longer need their own spacing by the red flash.
- **Fall damage** of horses, camels and other mounts, which took health
  directly, goes through `take_fall` (the cooldown, Resistance, a camel
  standing up, a horse's chance to rear); horse armor no longer softens a
  fall (`#bypasses_armor`), as it never did in Java.
- **Dry-out** of fish and dolphins is `dryOut` damage through it (it took
  health directly).
- **Poison, Wither and Instant Damage** hurt as separate hits, so the
  cooldown refuses a second 1 in the same tick; a mob killed by an effect
  now drops its loot (it dropped nothing).
- The **guardian's beam** on a mob sends its two hits whole; the cooldown
  takes only the second's excess (Track 3b subtracted it by hand).

The dragon keeps its own `EnderDragon.hurt` window (Track 4c), which is
the same rule.

### Players (item 1)

Players already had the cooldown (`Vitals::damage`), with the same 10 of
20 ticks, refusals and excess, and already took `hurtServer`'s push only
from a full hit (`knocked_back`). Two differences are fixed:

- **What the cooldown compares.** Java compares the hit before armor;
  VoxelCraft compared it after armor, Protection and Resistance. Now
  `Vitals::hurt` runs the cooldown first and puts only what lands through
  the caller's armor and enchantments, then Resistance; armor wears by
  what landed (`hurtArmor` in `getDamageAfterArmorAbsorb`), not the whole
  hit. Desktop, agents and LAN guests (who are agents on the host) share
  it.
- **The guardian's beam** on a player is its two hits, `indirectMagic`
  (death message "was killed by a guardian using magic") then the attack
  through armor, which the cooldown lets in by its excess; it was one hit
  of the magic plus the armor-reduced attack.

A push without Java's knockback model (a few older hits) also lands only
with a full hit now.

### Projectiles catching fire (item 2)

Snowballs, eggs, bottles o' enchanting, wind charges (a player's and a
breeze's), ender pearls, thrown splash and lingering potions, firework
rockets and fishing hooks carry `remainingFireTicks`: touching fire sets
8 seconds, lava 15, water or rain puts it out, and it burns down a tick at
a time, as an arrow's does (Track 8b), sampled along the tick's move. A
burning one hitting a block lights a candle or campfire or primes TNT
(with `mob_griefing` for a mob's); the block hit now also reaches target
blocks and redstone ore as every projectile's does in Java
(`Projectile.onHitBlock`). Blaze and ghast fireballs are always on fire
(`shouldBurn` ignites them every tick), so they too light what they hit
before bursting. Wither skulls and shulker bullets are never `isOnFire`
in Java, so nothing changes for them; llama spit applies no block
effects.

### Flames on mobs, players and projectiles (items 2 and 3)

`entity::model::flame` (Track 8c) is now drawn over:

- every burning **mob**'s box (`displayFireAnimation`, which a blaze shows
  while charged), replacing the ten rising cubes and the orange tint
  burning mobs had;
- a burning **player**'s box for their pose (standing, crouching, swimming
  or gliding), in third person, for agents and split-screen players, and
  for other players in LAN views; facing the viewer's camera;
- burning **thrown items, pearls, potions, rockets and hooks**, and every
  blaze and ghast fireball, over their Java boxes (0.25, 0.3125 for wind
  charges and blaze fireballs, 1 for a ghast's).

LAN snapshots now carry whether players, mobs, arrows, tridents and these
projectiles burn, so a guest sees the host's flames.

### Arrows and creative players (item 4)

Creative players are hit by arrows and tridents now (only spectators and
the dead are passed through, `canHitEntity`) and refuse them
(`abilities.invulnerable`): an arrow bounces back off them, a trident
bounces with its sound, and neither sets them alight or calls any effect.
The same happens to any player whose hurt cooldown refuses the hit (a hit
no harder than the last within half a second, after the difficulty scales
a mob's). Blaze and ghast fireballs no longer pass through creative
players either: they are spent on them, a ghast's bursting there, with
no damage or fire.

### Ownerless TNT (item 4)

Primed TNT's blast says "blew up" (`death.attack.explosion`), not "was
blown up by TNT". `Cause` carries whether a blast is TNT's, which
`ServerLevel.explode` still treats as TNT (`tnt_explosion_drop_decay`,
blocks broken whatever `mob_griefing` says).

### Pause screens (item 4)

The menu toolkit's screens say whether they are pause screens
(`Screen::pauses`, Java's `isPauseScreen`, true by default), and
`allowed_in_portal` follows it unless a screen says otherwise. An unshared
world now pauses only under a pause screen: the sign editor and a
lectern's book don't pause it, and neither does the console (Java's
`ChatScreen`). The pause menu, options, a book in hand, a book and quill
and the dialogs still pause it; the inventory and containers didn't
before either.

## Remaining differences

- **The credits** still pause an unshared world. Java's End credits
  (`WinScreen` with the poem) aren't a pause screen, but there the player
  has left the level during them; VoxelCraft's player stays in the End
  while they roll, so running the world would leave them to the dragon.
- **Fire isn't saved** on the projectiles that now carry it (nor on
  arrows, Track 8b); Java saves `Fire` on every entity. A burning snowball
  saved mid-flight reloads unlit.
- **Primed TNT names no igniter**: a player's or mob's TNT says "blew up"
  where Java's `explosion(tnt, igniter)` says "was blown up by" them
  (`player_explosion`); the game doesn't record who lit TNT.
- **A rocket's block hit** reaches target blocks and redstone ore only
  when it burns (others already only on their stopping hit); a fishing
  hook's only when it first lands.
- **Other projectiles and players the cooldown refuses**: only arrows and
  tridents read a player's hurt cooldown before hitting; a snowball or a
  fireball hitting a player inside it is resolved by the hit itself, as
  before.
- **One fire texture**, as Track 8c recorded; a mob's flame animates on the
  mob's own clock, so flames on two mobs are a few frames apart where
  Java's share the atlas's.
- Death messages still name players "a player" (Track 8c).
