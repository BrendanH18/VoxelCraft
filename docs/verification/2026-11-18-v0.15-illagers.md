## Implemented

- **Registration.** `MobKind::Vindicator`, `Evoker`, `Vex`, `Ravager` and
  `Illusioner` append after `Endermite`, saved under Java's registry names.
  Sizes, health, follow ranges, speeds, attack damage, knockback, knockback
  resistance, step height, experience and fire immunity (vex) are Java's.
  None spawns naturally: raids, patrols and mansions are later tracks, the
  illusioner is commands only and vexes come only from evokers.
- **Vindicator.** `MeleeAttackGoal(1.0)` with its iron axe (5 + 8 = 13 on
  Normal before difficulty scaling), drawn only while aggressive; avoids
  creakings; targets players, then adult villagers, wandering traders and
  iron golems. A name tag reading `Johnny` sets the saved Johnny flag, after
  which it attacks any mob but its allies. `VindicatorBreakDoorGoal` breaks
  a closed wooden door after 240 ticks on Normal or Hard, only in an active
  raid (the raid flag comes from Track E).
- **Evoker.** Flees players within 8 blocks and creakings; spell goals in
  Java's order: summon three vexes (100-tick cast, 340-tick interval, only
  while `nextInt(8) + 1` beats the vexes within 16 blocks), fangs (40/100;
  five at 1.5 and eight at 2.5 blocks three ticks later when the target is
  within 3 blocks, otherwise a 16-fang line 1.25 blocks apart, each fang
  standing on the first sturdy top found downward), and `wololo` (40-tick
  warmup, 60/140; a random blue sheep within 16 blocks turns red, only
  without a target and with `mobGriefing`). Casting holds it still facing
  its target. Fangs bite 8 ticks after rising for 6 indirect-magic damage
  and spare the evoker and its allies. Drops a totem of undying always and
  0–1 emerald (plus Looting) to a player.
- **Vex.** Flies through blocks with Java's move control (0.05 x speed of
  the remaining distance a tick, air drag 0.91/0.98, no gravity); charges at
  its target's eyes and hits on box contact with its iron sword (4 + 5);
  wanders within 7 x 5 x 7 of its bound origin; copies its owner's target;
  starves for 1 damage when its 20 x (30 + nextInt(90)) tick life runs out
  and every 20 ticks after. Its sword never drops.
- **Ravager.** Melee 12 with attack knockback 1.5 that follows unseen
  targets; 10 immobile ticks after a hit; speed eases toward 0.35 with a
  target and 0.3 without (0 while immobile). A shield facing it stuns it 40
  ticks half the time, else shoves the defender; the stun ends in a roar 10
  ticks into a 20-tick roar: 6 damage to every living thing but ravagers and
  illagers within 4 blocks of its box and the strong push. With
  `mobGriefing` it breaks leaves it pushes into (jumping when there are
  none) and crops it walks through, with drops. Drops a saddle. One illager
  can ride it (`Entities::mount_ravager`; the seat follows its passenger
  attachment and the rider's riding offset); a riding illager's attack box
  spans the mount's.
- **Illusioner.** Bow (`RangedBowAttackGoal(0.5, 20, 15)`), mirror spell
  (Invisibility 1200 ticks when not invisible) and blindness spell
  (Blindness 400 ticks on a new target, when the local difficulty is above
  Normal's 2).
- **Pillager.** `RangedCrossbowAttackGoal(1.0, 8.0)`: moves in while
  farther than 8 blocks or seen under 5 ticks (re-pathing every 20–40
  ticks, at half speed once loaded), charges for the crossbow's charge time
  (25 ticks less 5 per Quick Charge level), holds it 20–39 ticks, fires when
  in sight. Arrows leave from the eye less 0.1 toward the target's lower
  third lifted by a fifth of the distance, at 1.6 blocks a tick with
  `14 - 4 x difficulty` inaccuracy; Multishot fires three at 0 and ±10
  degrees; Piercing pierces; arrow damage is Java's `ceil(speed x 2)`. Spawned
  crossbows get the spawn enchantment roll and a 1 in 300 Piercing I.
  Pillagers walk Java's ground paths now.
- **Shared illager behaviour.** `AbstractIllager.IllagerArmPose` drives the
  model (crossed, attacking, spellcasting, bow, crossbow hold and charge,
  celebrating, neutral). `HurtByTargetGoal(Raider).setAlertOthers()`: hits
  from raiders are ignored, others turn the mob and its idle kin on the
  attacker. Illagers never target each other; the evoker's allies are
  illagers and illager-owned vexes. Celebration (cheers and hops) runs when
  Track E sets the raider's `celebrating` flag. Spell particles use Java's
  spell colours.
- **Raid hook.** `illager::Raider` on every illager and the ravager:
  `wave`, `can_join_raid` (set at spawn), `raid` (raid id), `raid_active`
  (refreshed by the raid owner, not saved), `celebrating`, `patrol_leader`
  and `patrolling`, saved in the mob's `illager.raider` object. Track E
  attaches raids by setting these fields; door breaking and celebration
  already read them.
- **Shared mob combat (MOB-02d).** A mob's main-hand weapon adds its attack
  damage modifier and Sharpness to its melee hits, its Knockback level to
  the attack knockback and its Fire Aspect to the target; this covers
  zombies, skeletons, illagers, vexes and any mob with a melee hit and a
  held item. Worn armor reduces attacks, arrows and blasts by Java's armor
  formula, then Resistance, then the armor's protection enchantments
  (Projectile Protection against arrows, Blast against blasts); netherite
  adds knockback resistance. Mobs' armor takes no wear, as in Java.
  `CanPickUpLoot` (rolled at 0.55 x the special multiplier for zombies,
  husks, drowned, zombie villagers and skeletons; turned on by a pillager's
  load) picks up items within one block sideways under `mobGriefing`,
  following `equipItemIfPossible` and `canReplaceCurrentItem` (armor by
  defense, then toughness; weapons by preferred type, then attack damage;
  then more enchantments, less damage, a custom name; Curse of Binding
  stays), with the replaced item's drop roll and a guaranteed drop for the
  new one; picking up makes the mob persistent. Held items are drawn in the
  weapon hand of zombies, husks, drowned, skeletons, strays and bogged
  holding something other than their bow, illagers and vexes.
- **Models and sounds.** Original box models (robed illagers with their
  own colours, a winged vex, a horned ravager with a jaw, rising fangs) and
  procedural voices and cues through the existing synth path: illager,
  vex and ravager calls; spell preparing and casting; fangs; the vex's
  charge; the ravager's bite, stun and roar; crossbow loading and shots;
  celebrations.
- **Integration.** Saves and LAN: the `illager` state and
  `can_pick_up_loot` go in each mob record; LAN looks carry the arm pose
  state, spell, charge and ravager timers, and held items through the
  equipment. Villagers flee these mobs within `VillagerHostilesSensor`'s
  distances (vex 8, vindicator 10, evoker, illusioner and ravager 12,
  pillager 15; zombies keep 8). The wandering trader now flees evokers (12), vindicators (8),
  vexes (8) and illusioners (12) as in Java. The CLI agent and controller
  reach these mobs through the shared summon, combat and naming paths.

## Differences from Java

- Targets are kept by uid and resolved each tick; `TargetingConditions`'
  invisibility and visibility percent apply to players only through the
  shared player target. `NearestAttackableTargetGoal`'s mob search for
  villagers and golems needs line of sight for every kind (Java's evoker,
  illusioner and pillager villager goals don't).
- The illusioner's bow goal has no strafing; it stands within 15 blocks once
  it has seen the target for 20 ticks and otherwise walks toward it.
- Fangs are not saved (they live under two seconds). Their bite reads mob
  and player boxes at the update after the fang's tick.
- The ravager's sideways push is capped at 4 blocks a tick; Java's
  `dx / d²` has no cap and flings bodies inside the ravager very far. Players
  get the push as a shove. The stun's shield check uses the shield-raised
  flag and facing; the shield's own blocking in the game decides the damage.
- `HoldGroundAttackGoal`, `ObtainRaidLeaderBannerGoal`,
  `RaiderMoveThroughVillageGoal`, patrols, captains, the pillager's 5-slot
  inventory and its banner pickup, raid buffs (`applyRaidBuffs`) and the
  captain's ominous bottle belong to Track E; the hook above is where they
  attach.
- A charged pillager crossbow stays a plain crossbow item; the charge lives
  in the pillager's state. A pillager killed while loaded drops an
  uncharged crossbow.
- Mob armor applies to attacks, arrows and blasts; fire, lava, falls and
  other damage the game doesn't type for mobs skip it, which also leaves
  out Fire Protection and Feather Falling on mobs. Smite and Bane of
  Arthropods on a mob's weapon are not counted.
- Riding: only illagers ride ravagers, through the API; nothing places
  riders yet (raids do). Piglins keep drawing their own weapons; a skeleton
  holding a picked-up sword still shows its bow on the other arm.
- Villagers' fear distances are cited from `VillagerHostilesSensor`, not
  extracted into the fixture; Java's zoglin entry is left to the villager
  track (H).
- Sounds are original stand-ins; volumes and pitches are approximate.
