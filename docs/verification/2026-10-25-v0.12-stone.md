## Design

`src/world/stone_blocks.rs` owns the range. Stairs and slabs join
`slab_stair_states::FAMILIES` as families 39–52 (`LEGACY_COUNT` 39), so
placement halves, corner shapes, slab merging, waterlogging, light faces
and neighbour reshaping come from the existing machinery; unlike the
legacy families, all of a v0.12 family's states sit in its own range. Cut
and cut red sandstone are slab-only families (`stairs` is air). Walls join
`wall_states` as families 20–30 with all four states in the range, so the
stored UP, posts propagating down stacks and waterlogging work as for the
existing walls. Shared files got hook lines only: `block.rs` (registry
arm, `shaped`, `wall_base`, `base`, `with_facing`, mining, opacity, solid
and self-cull, palette), `slab_stair_states.rs`, `wall_states.rs`,
`waterlog.rs` (wet wall canonical), `nether_blocks::placed` (pillar axis),
`furnace::smelt`, `crafting.rs` (`stone::add_recipes`), `textures.rs`,
`audio/sounds.rs`, `player/travel.rs` and `simulation`.

## Delivered behaviour

- **Mining** (Java `Blocks`): tuff family and chiseled stone bricks 1.5;
  deepslate bricks/tiles and their cracked and chiseled forms 3.5
  (`ofLegacyCopy(COBBLED_DEEPSLATE)`); quartz blocks, bricks, pillar and the
  sandstones 0.8; smooth quartz, smooth sandstones and the nether bricks
  2.0; end stone bricks 3.0; blocks of coal and diamond 5.0. All need a
  pickaxe; diamond needs iron. Stairs and walls copy their block. Slabs
  copy their block except Java's `registerSlab(…, 2.0F)` overrides: quartz,
  red sandstone and cut red sandstone slabs, and the cut sandstone slab and
  the **existing sandstone slab** (now 2.0 rather than 0.8, a fix). Tinted
  glass (0.3) and slime (0) need no tool. Everything drops itself, double
  slabs two (loot tables).
- **Quartz pillar**: `RotatedPillarBlock` axis from the clicked face; a
  pillar never turns with the player's facing; ends face along the axis
  and the grain turns on faces it crosses.
- **Tinted glass**: translucent, solid, self-culling, light dampening 15
  (blocks block and sky light), drops itself.
- **Slime block**: translucent, solid, self-culling, dampening 1
  (`noOcclusion` full block). Friction 0.8, `bounceRestitution` 1.0 (a
  falling player bounces back unless sneaking, through the existing
  `restitute` port), `stepOn` scales a nearly level walk by `0.4 + |dy| ×
  0.2` unless sneaking, and `fallOn` cancels fall damage whether or not
  the player sneaks (26.3's `fallOn` does nothing when the bounce is
  suppressed). Slime conducts redstone like a solid block.
- **Recipes**: every Java 26.3 crafting recipe producing these blocks
  (polished tuff, tuff bricks, chiseled forms from two slabs, deepslate
  bricks and tiles, quartz block/bricks/pillar, red sandstone, cut forms,
  red nether bricks, end stone bricks, nine-item mineral blocks and their
  reverse, tinted glass, slime block and slimeballs, 12 stairs, 14 slabs
  and 11 walls), including Java's alternatives: quartz stairs and slabs
  from chiseled quartz or pillars, sandstone and red sandstone slabs from
  the chiseled form and stairs also from the cut form. The existing
  sandstone stairs and slab recipes were replaced to accept them.
- **Smelting** (0.1 experience): deepslate bricks and tiles, nether bricks
  to their cracked forms; sandstone, red sandstone and quartz block to
  their smooth forms; also stone bricks to cracked stone bricks, which
  Java has and VoxelCraft lacked.
- **Stonecutter**: the 99 Java stonecutting recipes with these results are
  data in `crafting::stone::STONECUTTING` (Java ids kept beside each entry,
  checked against the registry names); the screen stays with v0.17.
- **Retired blocks** (owner, 2026-10-25): calcite stairs/slab/wall,
  deepslate stairs/slab/wall, smooth stone stairs and wall, and the
  polished granite, diorite and andesite walls no longer have recipes or
  creative entries. Placed ones still render, mine and drop themselves;
  their IDs are untouched.
- **Sounds**: stone by default; tinted glass uses glass and the slime
  block the nearest shared material (honey's squelch).
- **Trades**: the mason's "quartz block" trade now names Java's "block of
  quartz"; the existing chiseled stone brick and quartz pillar trades
  resolve now that the blocks exist.

## Differences from Java and not done

- Mobs, items and other entities don't bounce on or slow down on slime;
  only the player does. Slime blocks don't stick to pistons: pistons push a
  single line and have no block-structure resolution yet.
- The slime block's inner cube is drawn into its texture, not modelled.
- Block of coal has no fuel value yet (16,000 ticks; the data pass,
  item 6, registers fuels).
- Red sandstone doesn't generate in badlands, and structures weren't
  changed: the jungle temple still builds stone bricks where Java has
  chiseled stone bricks (`temples.rs`), and other structures weren't
  checked for the new blocks.
- Diamond blocks use the stone sound (VoxelCraft has no metal material,
  as for iron blocks).
- Not played by hand: owner play is the acceptance evidence.
