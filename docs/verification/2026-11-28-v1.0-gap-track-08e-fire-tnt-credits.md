## Scope

What [Track 8d](2026-11-28-v1.0-gap-track-08d-combat-fire.md#remaining-differences)
left:

1. Projectile fire saved and loaded as Java saves entity fire (`Fire`) for
   every projectile that can burn.
2. Primed TNT recording its igniter as Java's `PrimedTnt.owner` does, so a
   player's TNT reads "was blown up by" them; an old save's "was blown up
   by TNT" death reading back as the blast it was.
3. The End credits taking the player out of the level as Java's do, so
   they no longer pause an unshared world and the player stays safe.

All three are done; what remains is [listed below](#remaining-differences).

## What changed

### Projectile fire saved (item 1)

Only tridents (with their fire, Track 8b) and Wither skulls were saved;
every other projectile and primed TNT was dropped from a save, so a
burning one couldn't come back burning. Java saves them all but the
fishing hook, each with its `Fire`. The level file now keeps, each under
its own key and written only when there is one
([`saved_projectiles`](../../src/entity/saved_projectiles.rs)), with the
ones stored in unloaded chunks:

| Key | What | Kept |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `arrows` | arrows, tipped and spectral | position, motion, facing, age, stuck, potion and custom effect, owner or shooter, pickup, critical, enchantments (Flame, Power, Punch, Piercing), PvP, death message, `fire`, doused |
| `thrown` | snowballs, eggs, bottles o' enchanting, wind charges, llama spit | kind, owner or shooter, position, motion, age, origin, `fire` |
| `thrown_potions` | splash and lingering potions | potion, lingering, owner, position, motion, age, `fire` |
| `fireworks` | firework rockets | the rocket item with its fireworks, position, motion, life, lifetime, owner or shooter, shot at an angle, `fire` |
| `fireballs` | blaze and ghast fireballs | position, motion, heading, age, size, punched, owner (always burning, so no fire) |
| `primed_tnt` | primed TNT | position, motion, fuse, power, owner |
| `ender_pearls` | thrown pearls | owner, position, motion, age, `fire` |

A burning snowball, pearl, potion, bottle, rocket or arrow saved mid-flight
reloads burning, and burns down from where it was. Fishing hooks aren't
saved (`noSave` in Java too). A rocket a glider is holding reloads loose,
as Java doesn't save `attachedToEntity`. What Java doesn't save stays
unsaved here too: a piercing arrow's list of what it passed through and a
breeze's deflection.

### Who lit the TNT (item 2)

Primed TNT carries its owner (`PrimedTnt.owner`, saved). It is:

- the player, for flint and steel and (new) a fire charge used on TNT
  (`TntBlock.useItemOn`; the charge is used up outside creative);
- a burning projectile's owner (`onProjectileHit`): a player's arrow,
  snowball, pearl, potion, rocket, trident or punched fireball, or the
  skeleton, blaze, ghast, drowned or other mob whose projectile it was;
- whoever was behind the blast that set it off (`wasExploded`,
  `getIndirectSourceEntity`): the TNT's own owner down a chain, a creeper,
  the Wither (its blast and its skulls), a ghast's fireball's ghast;
- no one for fire, redstone, dispensers, End crystals, beds, anchors and
  TNT minecarts.

When it blows, the owner counts only while it is still in the level
(`EntityReference.getLivingEntity`): a player always, a mob while alive.
With an owner the blast is `player_explosion`: a death in it reads "was
blown up by a player" (or "by a skeleton", "by the Wither", any mob by
name), and mobs a player's TNT kills drop what a player's kill drops.
Without one, or once the owner has gone (a creeper is discarded after its
blast, so TNT it chained names no one), it "blew up". TNT keeps its TNT
rules either way (`ExplosionInteraction.TNT`), and TNT that names a ghast
doesn't set fires as a ghast's fireball does.

Every blast's cause now carries the entity behind it (`Cause::by`, Java's
`DamageSource.getEntity`), which is what lets chained TNT inherit it.

An old save's dead player whose death message was "was blown up by TNT"
or "was blown up by an End crystal" (the texts before Tracks 8c and 8d)
reads it back as that blast, in today's words ("blew up"), instead of as
a mob's hit (`Cause::RETIRED`, `Cause::saved`, `Vitals::restore`).

### The End credits (item 3)

As Java's `showEndCredits` takes the player out of the level while
`WinScreen` rolls, and that screen isn't a pause screen, the credits no
longer pause an unshared world. While they roll the player has left it:

- they don't move (no physics, no portal, no block contact);
- the entities don't see them (`entity_ctx` holds no host): no mob
  targets them, no projectile hits them, no spawning counts them, the
  dragon flies as with no player there;
- blasts and wind bursts don't reach them, and they pick nothing up;
- LAN guests don't see their body.

Their own tick runs on, as `ServerPlayer.doTick` does for a removed
player: effects count down, hunger ticks, burning burns out. Time,
weather and the rest of the world carry on. At the end (or on a skip)
they respawn in the Overworld as before.

## Remaining differences

- **Players are "a player"** in TNT's message, as in every death message
  (Track 8c); Java names them.
- **Pearls are saved with the dimension** they fly in, not with their
  owner's player data (`ender_pearls`); a pearl whose chunk is unloaded
  stays in memory until the save, as other projectiles do, and Java's
  pearl keeps its chunk loaded for its owner.
- **Eyes of ender** still aren't saved (Java saves them); they don't burn.
- **During the credits the chunks around the player keep ticking.** Java
  drops the removed player's chunk tickets, so in an unshared world the
  blocks and entities near them stop; here they run on, though nothing
  there can find the player.
- **A mob behind TNT isn't angered or blamed** beyond the death message:
  Java's `player_explosion` makes the igniter the attacker of whatever the
  blast hurts (`lastHurtByMob`).
- **Fire charges** still can't set fire to blocks or be used on campfires
  other than lighting them (`FireChargeItem.useOn`); only TNT and candles
  take them.
- From Track 8d, still open: a rocket's block hit reaches target blocks
  and redstone ore only when it burns; only arrows and tridents read a
  player's hurt cooldown before hitting; one fire texture.
