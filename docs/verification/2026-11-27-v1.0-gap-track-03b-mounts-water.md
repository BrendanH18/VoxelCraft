## What changed

### Horses rear (FOO-04c)

`AbstractHorse`'s rearing behaviour (`mounts::State`): `standIfPossible`
sets 20 ticks of `FLAG_STANDING` (`stand_counter`) for horses, donkeys,
mules and undead horses (`mounts::rears`; llamas, trader llamas and camels
can't, `canPerformRearing`). Triggers:

- a hit that hurts, one time in three (`hurtServer`);
- `RandomStandGoal`, every tick while the AI runs: `nextStand` counts up
  from -80 (`getAmbientStandInterval`), and once past 0 a
  `nextStand`-in-1000 roll resets it and, one time in ten, rears with the
  ambient call (`getAmbientStandSound`);
- `makeMad` (rear, then the angry sound, unless already rearing): a failed
  taming throws the rider off (`RunAroundLikeCrazyGoal`), and any item that
  isn't food used on an untamed horse, donkey, mule, zombie horse or llama
  (`Horse`, `ZombieHorse`, `AbstractChestedHorse.mobInteract`) — a llama
  makes only the sound;
- the rider letting go of jump on a saddled horse (`onPlayerJump`, and the
  server's `handleStartJump`), with `allowStandSliding`.

While it rears a horse is immobile (`isImmobile`): its AI doesn't run and it
walks nowhere; ridden, the rider's input is held on the ground unless a jump
is pending or sliding is allowed (`getRiddenInput`). `standAnim` (and the
tick before it) follows `AbstractHorse.tick` and moves the rider 0.15 up and
0.7 back at full rear (`getPassengerAttachmentPoint`). **Handoff to Track
7b:** the rearing animation reads `State::stand_anim(partial)` (or
`stand_anim`/`stand_anim_o`), and `State::standing()`; LAN replicas carry
both values in the mob's look.

### Camel sounds and brain (FOO-04c)

- **Sounds** (`mounts::Cue`, `audio::mounts`, original procedural
  stand-ins in the existing voice style): `sitDown` and `standUp` play
  `entity.camel.sit` and `.stand`; the server's `handleStartJump` (a release
  the camel `canJump` from) plays `entity.camel.dash`; the cooldown running
  out plays `entity.camel.dash_ready`. Camel husks play their own
  `entity.camel_husk.*`. The angry calls are `entity.horse.angry`,
  `entity.baby_horse.angry` (a foal's), `entity.donkey.angry`,
  `entity.mule.angry`, `entity.zombie_horse.angry` and
  `entity.llama.angry`; skeleton horses have none.
- **The dash a release in the air costs.** `handleStartJump` sets `DASH`,
  and a change of `DASH` starts the 55-tick cooldown when none runs
  (`onSyncedDataUpdated`), so a release off the ground, which
  `onPlayerJump` refuses to dash, still costs the cooldown and plays the
  sound, as in Java.
- **Look target and `SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget`.** The brain's
  `LOOK_TARGET` memory: `SetEntityLookTargetSometimes(PLAYER, 6, 30..=60)`
  takes the nearest visible player within 6 blocks when its ticker comes
  round; `LookAtTargetSink(45, 90)` turns the head to it (not while a rider
  steers, `CamelLookControl`) for 45 to 90 ticks, while it stays visible,
  then erases it. The idle `RunOne` now picks evenly among the stroll,
  `SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget(2.0, 3)` (with a look target, not while it
  refuses to move), `RandomSitting(20)` and `DoNothing(30, 60)`.
- **Leash pull.** `onElasticLeashPull`: a lead pulling past its elastic
  reach stands a seated camel up once it has finished sitting down and has
  room (`Entities::tick_leashes`).
- **Head and body.** `CamelBodyRotationControl` (Java's
  `BodyRotationControl`, skipped while it refuses to move) with
  `MAX_HEAD_Y_ROT` 30: walking, the head stays within 30 degrees of the
  body; standing still, a head turned more than 15 degrees since it settled
  pulls the body round to within 30, and once the head has been still 10
  ticks the body turns to face it over the next 10. While it sits or changes
  pose the body holds still and the head stays within 30 degrees
  (`clampHeadRotationToBody`). A steered camel follows its rider.
- **Animations.** `entity::model::camel_anim` holds Java's `CAMEL_SIT`,
  `CAMEL_SIT_POSE`, `CAMEL_STANDUP` and `CAMEL_DASH` keyframes, sampled as
  `KeyframeAnimation` does (linear and Catmull-Rom), and the camel model
  plays them as `Camel.setupAnimationStates` starts them: the sit for its 40
  ticks then the held pose, the 52-tick stand-up, the looping dash while
  `DASH` is on (no walk cycle then, `updateWalkAnimation`), and the head
  tipped down after a dash by `45 × cooldown / 55` (`applyHeadRotation`,
  which also limits the head to ±30 degrees of yaw and -25 to 45 of pitch).
  The model's parts now follow `AdultCamelModel`'s hierarchy (head, tail and
  saddle on the body; legs pivoting 23 pixels up) with the same boxes where
  they were, so its look at rest is unchanged.

### Mounts in fluids (FOO-04c)

- **Lava currents.** `Entity.updateFluidInteraction` pushes a pushed mob
  along flowing lava, 0.0023 a tick (0.007 in the Nether, `FAST_LAVA`):
  ridden horses and camels (`equine_ridden_tick`), mobs on Java ticks
  (guardians, polar bears in water) and every land mob.
- **Currents tick by tick.** Land mobs in VoxelCraft's continuous fluid
  model now carry the current as its own part of their motion
  (`Mob::current`, `fluid_current_flow`), a Java tick at a time: they move
  by what the current built so far plus this tick's push, and keep it
  dragged by `getWaterSlowDown` (0.8; a skeleton horse 0.96, Dolphin's
  Grace 0.96) and 0.8 upward, or lava's 0.5 (0.8 upward in shallow lava).
  From rest that is 0.014, then 0.0252, ... toward Java's steady 0.07 a tick
  (five pushes; Track 3's steady drift was four). A push under 0.0045 on a
  mob nearly at rest rises to 0.0045 (`CurrentAccumulator.applyTo`). Out of
  both fluids the current joins the ordinary motion; a blocked axis stops it.
- **The climbing lift.** `AbstractHorse.onClimbable` is false, so Java
  never gives a horse, donkey, mule, llama, camel or undead horse
  `travelInWater`'s 0.2 climbing lift or `handleOnClimbable`; their
  unridden walk in a ladder or vine no longer climbs (it did, through the
  shared mob model).
- **A sprinting camel's 0.9 water slow-down.** `travelInWater` uses 0.9
  when the *mob* `isSprinting`; in both 26.3 jars nothing ever sets a
  camel's (or any mount's) sprint flag: `Camel.canSprint` only lets the
  rider keep sprinting (`LocalPlayer.vehicleCanSprint`). A ridden camel's
  water slow-down is therefore always 0.8, which is what VoxelCraft already
  did; no change.

### Mount screens (FOO-04c)

Opening a horse's, donkey's, mule's, llama's, camel's, undead horse's,
nautilus's or zombie nautilus's inventory now shows Java's mount layout
(`Entities::mount_screen`, the HUD): its name as the title
(`getDisplayName`), the saddle slot at (8, 18) and the body slot at (8, 36)
where the mount has them (no saddle slot for llamas; the body slot only for
horses, zombie horses, llamas and nautiluses), the chest's columns of three
from (80, 18) (a donkey's or mule's five, a llama's one per strength), the
mount in the dark 52-pixel box at (26, 18) turning toward the pointer
(`extractEntityInInventoryFollowsMouse`, size 17), and the player inventory
at (8, 84). Empty saddle and body slots show the item they take as the
armor slots do (a saddle; iron horse armor, a white carpet or iron nautilus
armor), standing in for Java's `container/slot/saddle`, `horse_armor`,
`llama_armor` and `nautilus_armor_inventory` sprites. The slots keep their
indices, so every path the screen had (keyboard and mouse, shift-clicks,
LAN, the CLI's `chest take/put`) is unchanged; the controller's grid menu
shows the same empty-slot sprites.

### Baby dolphins (MOB-03c)

- **`forcedAge`.** Feeding a calf is `ageUp(seconds, true)`
  (`Mob::age_up`): the growth adds to `forced_age` and starts 40 ticks of
  happy-villager particles, one every fourth tick (the green-tinted effect
  the dolphin's treasure particles already use). `forced_age` saves (only
  once set, so older records save as before) and becomes the age of a mob
  that comes of age through `ageUp`, counting down as an adult's positive
  age does. A feeding takes a tenth of the time left, so in Java as here it
  never brings a calf to 0 itself; the forced age is kept and saved for
  that.
- **Eyes and drying.** A dolphin breathes at its own eyes, 0.3 up and a
  calf's 0.09375 (`BABY_DIMENSIONS`), 0.11111111 below them
  (`updateFluidOnEyes`); it dries by its box (`isInWaterOrRain`).

### Guardians (MOB-03c)

- **The 1-in-5 roll.** With no target, `NearestAttackableTargetGoal` looks
  only on the target selector's full updates (every other tick,
  `serverAiStep`) and then only one time in five (`randomInterval`,
  `reducedTickDelay(10)`), for players and mobs alike: nine ticks on
  average.
- **The two-part hit.** At the end of a charge on a squid or axolotl,
  `indirectMagic` hits whole (1, 2 more on Hard, 2 more from an elder) with
  the 0.4 push, then `doHurtTarget` (6, an elder's 8) adds only what it
  exceeds that by, inside the same invulnerability window, unpushed
  (`EntityEvent::MobHit` twice, the second `melee`).

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Camels:** the brain's other `LOOK_TARGET` writers (`FollowTemptation`,
  `AnimalMakeLove`, `BabyFollowAdult`) don't set the memory, so only a
  nearby player becomes a look target; the walk to it reuses the stroll's
  path rather than Java's `WALK_TARGET` memory. `RandomLookAround` and the
  idle head bob stay VoxelCraft's. The sounds are procedural stand-ins. The
  keyframes are applied at whole ticks (no partial-tick blend) to
  VoxelCraft's camel boxes, whose legs are 20 pixels long to Java's 21, and
  a calf plays the adult's keyframes scaled down (`CamelBabyAnimation` and
  the separate calf model are Track 7b's baby-model work). The ears don't
  fold during the dash (they are part of the head box), and the idle
  animation (ear flicks, tail sway) isn't played.
- **Horses:** the rearing *animation* is Track 7b's; until it lands the
  model doesn't rear (the rider's seat does move). `handleStartJump`'s
  horse jump sound (`entity.horse.jump`) isn't played. Eating grass
  (`setEating`, also `isImmobile`) isn't modelled.
- **Mount screens:** the entity preview draws the model's boxes
  flat-shaded, without textures or face noise; the empty-slot sprites are
  item silhouettes rather than Java's own sprite art. The controller's
  grid menu keeps its grid order (with the sprites), not Java's
  positions.
- **Baby dolphins:** the forced-growth particles use the green effect
  sprite, VoxelCraft's stand-in for `happy_villager`. Other animals' feeding
  (Java's `Animal.mobInteract`, also `ageUp(…, true)`) doesn't keep a
  forced age yet: `Mob::age_up` is there for it, but changing every
  animal's feeding was outside this track.
- **Guardians:** candidate mobs are still sensed twice a second (Track 3).
- **Currents:** the current's part is kept in VoxelCraft's continuous
  model, exact at 20 updates a second and interpolated between ticks at
  other frame rates. Players in Nether lava still take the Overworld's
  0.0023 push (`player::travel` passes no `FAST_LAVA`; outside this
  track's mobs and mounts).
