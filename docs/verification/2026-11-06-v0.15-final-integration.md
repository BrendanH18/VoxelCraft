## Cross-track wiring

- **Sniffer eggs:** the warm ocean-ruin archaeology table's 1-in-15 slot
  now yields `Item::SNIFFER_EGG` (it yielded nothing while the sniffer
  track was separate), so sniffers are obtainable in survival the Java way.
  `exact_java_table_weights_and_original_item_registrations` now requires
  every table entry to resolve.
- **Mob kinds and LAN indices:** `Creaking`, `Allay`, `Sniffer` append
  after `CopperGolem` in that order, matching the reserved LAN indices 69,
  70 and 71, so `lan_index` is the position in `MobKind::ALL` again and
  the per-track special cases are gone. Mobs save by name, so no save
  changes.
- **Item icons:** offsets from `passive_icon_base()` are sniffer items
  9–10, sherds 11–17 and the resin brick 18 (two tracks had both claimed
  9); `icon_count` covers all of them.
- **Sounds:** allay actions keep `BASE_COUNT + 44..48` and the sniffer cues
  move to start at 48 (both tracks had started at 44). `Sound::COUNT`,
  `Voice::ALL` (40) and the per-dimension save keys (21) cover every track.
- **Block textures:** `tex::COUNT` is the highest layer any of the merged
  tracks uses (ocean ruins' 3512).
- **Allay test:** expects Creaking at index 69 and Sniffer at 71 instead of
  empty slots.

No saved identifier changed meaning: every block state, item and texture
layer is the one the track allocated inside its reservation.

generations 1–3 are part of `cargo test --release`.
