## What changed

### Golden spears on piglins and zombified piglins

- `nether::Weapon` gains `GoldenSpear` at the end, saved by name as
  `golden_spear`; every older name loads as before.
- `Piglin.createSpawnWeapon`: an adult's weapon is a crossbow when
  `nextFloat() < 0.5`, else a golden spear when `nextInt(10) == 0`, else a
  golden sword. `ZombifiedPiglin.populateDefaultEquipmentSlots`: a golden
  spear when `nextInt(20) == 0`, else a golden sword (a strider jockey's
  fungus stick replaces it, as before). The spear draw is its own random
  roll seeded by the spawn, as `CanPickUpLoot` already is, so the shared
  random stream and every seeded spawn sequence stay unchanged. A
  zombified piglin now spawns with its weapon set (`held`), rather than
  leaving `None` for the sword.
- A golden spear adds no attack damage (`0 + attackDamageBonus`, gold 0),
  so a zombified piglin with one hits for its base 5; a piglin never
  swings it (`MeleeAttack` skips it: `canUseNonMeleeWeapon` counts a
  kinetic weapon).
- Drops: a piglin's weapon already dropped at the default chance; a
  zombified piglin's main hand (`held`: sword, spear, or a converted
  piglin's crossbow or axe) now drops too, on a player kill with
  `max(nextFloat − 0.01 × Looting, 0) < 0.085`, randomly worn — it used
  to drop nothing.
- Drawn: a golden spear in the hand of both models (an original stand-in:
  a wooden shaft through the fist and a gold head forward), couched level
  while the spear is lowered.

### Piglins fight with spears (`SpearApproach`, `SpearAttack`, `SpearRetreat`)

Brain behaviours, ticked every tick (`mob::spear::PiglinSpear`; the
`SPEAR_*` memories aren't saved, as in Java's brain):

- Each behaviour not running tries to start, in the activity's order,
  then each running one stops or ticks. `SpearApproach(1, 10)` starts with
  no `SPEAR_STATUS` (setting `APPROACH`), walks at the target
  (`moveTo(target)`) while it is more than 10 blocks off, then stops with
  `CHARGING`. `SpearAttack(1, 1, 2)` starts on `CHARGING`: `SPEAR_ENGAGE_TIME`
  = `computeDamageUseDuration` = 14 + 275 = 289 ticks for gold (not
  reduced: behaviours tick every tick), lowers the spear, charges and,
  within 2 blocks or at the path's end, picks a spot 6–7 blocks away
  (`LandRandomPos.getPosAway`) and runs there; it stops with `RETREAT`
  when the time is up. `SpearRetreat(1)` starts on `RETREAT` when a spot
  9–11 blocks away is found, walks there while its fleeing time is under
  100, its spot is set and its path isn't finished, then clears the
  status. Riders add 2 to each distance.
- The status outlives a fight (only `ATTACK_TARGET` is erased when the
  fight activity ends), so a piglin that lost its target mid-approach
  starts its next fight with the charge, as Java's does. Admiring, fleeing
  or any other activity stops the running behaviours.
- When no behaviour moves it, the walk-target sink does
  (`SetWalkTargetFromAttackTargetIfTargetOutOfReach`, `MoveToTargetSink`):
  it keeps its current path, or heads for the target while out of melee
  reach. This is also the path `SpearRetreat`'s first `canStillUse` sees.

### Mob stabs (`KineticWeapon.damageEntities`, `LivingEntity.stabAttack`)

Moved from `Mob::spear_charge_tick` to `Entities::mob_spear_stabs`, run
once per update before the mobs move (Java's `LivingEntity.tick` runs
`updatingUsingItem` before `aiStep`):

- The ray: from the eye along the **head's** look (`getHeadLookAngle`),
  from 1 to 2.25 blocks plus the mob's own forward motion
  (`getKnownMovement`, none while riding), stopped at the first block
  collision box (`ClipContext.Block.COLLIDER`; nothing when that block is
  nearer than 1 block). It stabs every player (alive, not spectating; a
  creative player too, whom the damage then spares) and every **mob**
  whose box grown by 0.125 it crosses, except the spear's own mount and
  that mount's other passengers (`isPassengerOfSameVehicle`).
- Each target is remembered for the 10-tick contact cooldown, before the
  conditions are tested; the memory starts afresh with each lowering.
- Speeds are taken along the **body's** look (`getLookAngle`), each
  entity's from its root vehicle (`getMotion`): a zombie horseman's or
  camel husk rider's own speed is its mount's (before, a rider's was
  zero, so its conditions could never pass).
- Damage: the base attack damage plus `floor(relative speed ×
  multiplier)`, plus Sharpness (`EnchantmentHelper.modifyDamage`); a mob
  target adds Smite or Bane of Arthropods as for a melee hit and goes
  through the mob-hit path (retaliation, kill credit, loot, Bane's
  Slowness); a player gets Fire Aspect's fire when hurt.
- **Knockback without damage**: a contact that knocks back pushes 0.4
  along the mob's facing and then its attack knockback
  (`causeExtraKnockback`), whether or not damage landed, and each push
  slows the mob (× 0.6). Players get the new `EntityEvent::PlayerShove`;
  mobs `Mob::knock_back`. A damaging contact's own push away from the
  attacker stays with the hit.
- **Dismount**: a contact whose dismount condition holds takes a riding
  target off its vehicle: players through the new
  `EntityEvent::PlayerDismount` (the same dismount as sneaking off),
  mobs by leaving their vehicle. No entity in VoxelCraft is in
  `#cannot_be_dismounted_by_item_usage` (only interactions are).
- **No wear**: `LivingEntity.stabAttack` calls only `ItemStack.hurtEnemy`,
  which wears nothing; the wear is in `postHurtEnemy`, which only
  `Player.attack` (through `itemAttackInteraction`) calls. So mob stabs
  leave the spear as it is, as in Java. Players' stabs already wore it.

### The look turns by Java's limits (`LookControl`, `Mob.lookAt`)

A mob aiming a spear keeps Java's `yHeadRot` and `xRot`
(`Mob::look`, not saved):

- `SpearUseGoal` (zombies, husks, zombie villagers, zombified piglins), on
  its full updates: `Mob.lookAt(target, 30, 30)` turns the body by at most
  30°, and `setLookAt(target, 30, 30)` asks the look control for 30° a
  tick and 30° of pitch for two ticks. A piglin's spear behaviours set
  `LOOK_TARGET`, which `LookAtTargetSink` turns into `setLookAt` at the
  defaults: 10° a tick and 40° of pitch.
- Every tick after the move control: the pitch restarts level
  (`resetXRotOnTick`) and turns toward the wanted point by at most its
  limit; the head turns by its limit while the request lasts, otherwise
  10° back toward the body; while walking a path it stays within 75° of
  the body. The drawn head follows it.

### Spear paths

`SpearUseGoal`'s and the behaviours' `moveTo(target)` now plan with reach
1, as `PathNavigation.moveTo(Entity)` does (Track 2 used 0 for an entity);
`createPath` keeps an unfinished path to the same block, as before.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Ray edges**: Java's `getManyEntityHitResult` counts a hit on the
  0.125 margin only when a block doesn't stand between the margin point
  and the box's centre; here the margin always counts (the block clip
  along the ray itself is Java's). Only mobs and players are stabbed:
  minecarts, boats, armor stands, item frames, end crystals and the
  dragon's parts, which Java's ray also reaches, are not.
- **Body turn**: the body still turns toward its travel at the game's
  8 rad/s rather than `MoveControl`'s 90° a tick; the stab's speeds use
  that body yaw. `Mob.lookAt`'s 30° body turn is applied.
- **Mob knockback**: a damaging contact on a mob pushes it as the other
  mob melee hits here do (a set velocity away from the attacker), then
  Java's facing push; players get Java's sequence. A stabbed mob's Fire
  Aspect isn't applied (mob-on-mob melee here doesn't apply it either).
- **Piglin targets that are mobs** (hoglins, wither skeletons) are
  tracked at the look-around's last sighting (every ~0.5 s), and their
  eyes are taken 1.2 blocks up, as the crossbow aim already does.
- **Dismounted player**: Java knocks back before `stopRiding`; here the
  player is unseated first so the push isn't lost when the dismount
  zeroes the velocity. The outcome (off and pushed) is the same.
- **Not done** (Track 2's smaller notes, item 3 of the brief): the strider
  jockey's rider looking around (`LookAtPlayerGoal`) and the strider's
  look goals stopping while it is ridden, and the riding baby piglin's
  look behaviours (`createLookBehaviors`), remain the retained head code.
  REASON
- The golden spear's look in the hand is an original box model, not the
  item model.

## What remains unverified

No one played any of this: piglins and zombified piglins with golden
spears, their charges and retreats, mobs stabbed in a charge's way,
players knocked back or unseated by a charge, and the turning head. No
screenshots were taken; the spear in the piglins' hands is unseen.

builds one uid map over the loaded mobs and, per lowered spear, one block
clip and one box test per mob and player. The piglin's behaviours plan
paths as Java's do, kept while the target stays in the same block.
