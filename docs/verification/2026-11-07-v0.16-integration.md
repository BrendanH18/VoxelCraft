Each track had to hold another track's slot open while it worked alone.
Integration made every ordering contiguous; nothing that was ever saved by
a released build moved.

- `MobKind`: `Warden` (LAN 72), `Breeze` (LAN 73). The breeze's explicit
  wire mapping is gone; `lan_index` is again the position in `MobKind::ALL`.
- `Enchantment`: `SwiftSneak` (save bit 40), `WindBurst` (41). Track D's

  `#tradeable`.

  computed from a list of each track's end.

## Duplicated work reconciled

Tracks C and D both implemented Java's `currentImpulseImpactPos` fall rule
(wind charges and mace smashes) on `Vitals`, under different fields. One
field (`impulse_impact_y`) and one per-tick grace countdown remain; the
wind charge's `wind_impulse` and the mace's `smash_impulse` both set it.

## Cross-track wiring

- **Jukebox → vibrations.** `world::jukebox::vibration_hook` posts
  `jukebox_play` / `jukebox_stop_play` at the block centre through
  `World::emit_game_event` with the jukebox state, so sculk sensors and
  wardens hear records.
- **Tridents → vibrations.** Player and drowned throws post
  `projectile_shoot`; a trident sticking in a block posts
  `projectile_land`, with its owner as the source, as arrows do.
- **Ancient cities → shriekers.** Track B places the summoning shrieker
  state; Track A's shriekers summon from it.
- **Mace ← breeze rod.** `weapons::breeze_rod()` resolves Track C's item by
  name, so the mace recipe and its anvil repair now exist in survival.
- **Wind Burst → wind charge blocks.** `weapons::mace::wind_burst_blocks`
  runs Track C's `World::wind_trigger` with radius 3.5: doors, trapdoors,
  gates, buttons, levers, bells and candles. Gust particles for Wind Burst
  are not drawn.
- **Trial loot ← other tracks.** The heavy core, the creator, creator music
  box and precipice discs, tridents and Wind Burst books resolve by Java
  name in the vault and chamber tables. A test checks they are registered.
- **Loot tables.** `loot_tables.json` was merged as data, table by table,
  with no table changed by two tracks.

## Known gaps after integration

The per-track records list their own. Integration adds:

- Wind charges and other thrown items don't post `projectile_shoot` /
  `projectile_land` yet (arrows and tridents do).
- Wind Burst draws no gust particles.
- `relic` has no survival source until trail-ruin archaeology exists.
