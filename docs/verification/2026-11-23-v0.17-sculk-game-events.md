## Implemented behaviour

**Tables.** `GameEvent` gained `allay_listens` and the listener radius
constants; the existing frequencies, radii and tags already matched Java and
are now checked against the fixture. `World::emit_block_event` posts at a
block's centre (Java's `BlockPos` overloads).

**Redstone and automation** (table in
[redstone.md](../redstone.md#game-events-and-sculk-sensors-v017)):
`World::use_redstone_by(pos, source)` posts what Java's use posts (levers,
buttons, trapdoors, daylight detectors, bells, note blocks) for players,
wind charges and arrows alike; repeater and comparator uses no longer post
the `block_change` Java doesn't. Plates post `block_activate` from the first
entity found on them (`World::redstone_contacts_from` names players; mobs and
arrows are named too) and `block_deactivate` when their tick releases them;
buttons post `block_deactivate` on release. Tripwire hooks post their
`emitState` event at both hooks; shears cutting string post `shear`. Powered
doors, gates and trapdoors post `block_open` / `block_close`; TNT primed by
power or fire posts `prime_fuse`. Pistons post `block_activate`,
`block_deactivate` and `block_destroy` for blocks they break, with Java's
context states. Dispensers post the empty click, bucket `fluid_place` /
`fluid_pickup`, mob bucket and TNT `entity_place`, and their projectiles'
`projectile_shoot`.

**Block and item uses** (`PlayerSession::block_use_event`,
`item_use_event`, `entity_use_event`, from the player with their sneaking,
creative and spectator state): block transforms (paths, stripping,
scraping, tilling, waxing, sign ink) post `block_change` naming the new
state; carving pumpkins `shear`; candles, candle cakes, statues, flower pots,
decorated pots, campfire food, lighting, dousing and anchors `block_change`;
the last slice of cake `block_destroy` after `eat`; flint and fire charges
`block_place` for fire and `block_change` for lighting a candle or campfire;
cauldrons `fluid_place`, `fluid_pickup` and the bottle's `block_change` with
no source as Java; chests, barrels, ender chests and shulker boxes
`container_open` for their first viewer (dispensers and hoppers post
nothing), and the last viewer leaving `container_close` (copper golems
count); jukeboxes and shelves their `block_change`, `item_interact_finish`
and `entity_interact`; bone meal `item_interact_finish` at the player; end
crystals, cushions, frames, paintings, armor stands, minecarts (naming the
block under the rail) and boats `entity_place`; item frames `block_change`;
eyes of ender, rockets and wind charges `projectile_shoot`; fishing rods
`item_interact_start` and the bobber's shot, then `item_interact_finish`.
Eye insertion no longer posts the `block_change` Java doesn't.

**Entities.** Uses on mobs (feeding, leads, taming, trading, shearing,
bogged) post `entity_interact` at the mob, after `shear` for a sheared
sheep or bogged; mounting posts `entity_mount` at the vehicle (and
`entity_interact` for a mob); a chest boat or chest minecart opening posts
`container_open`; dismounting posts `entity_dismount` at the vehicle. Every
projectile posts `projectile_shoot` where it starts (mob arrows, witch
potions, breeze, ghast and blaze charges, llama spit, wither skulls, shulker
bullets, snow golem snowballs, ominous item spawner drops, dispensed and
crossbow rockets) and `projectile_land` when it hits: at the struck block's
centre naming it (so wool dampens it) or where it hit an entity (arrows,
pearls, potions, thrown items, fireballs, wither skulls, shulker bullets).
Rockets post `explode` from their owner. Endermen, shulkers and chorus fruit
post `teleport` where they left. Creepers post `prime_fuse` when their fuse
lights, witches `drink` when a potion is finished, grazing sheep `eat`,
chickens `entity_place` when they lay; mob block changes post `block_place`
(turtle eggs, frogspawn), `block_destroy` (trampled eggs, blocks a mob
broke) and villager farming `block_destroy` then `block_place`. Mobs entering
water post `splash`.

**Players** (`World::player_moved`, `World::player_vitals`): entering water
posts `splash`, a slime block or bed bounce `bounce` (the new
`Player::bounced`), gliding `elytra_glide` every tenth tick, lost health
`entity_damage`, death `entity_die`, and an armor slot that changed `equip`
or `unequip`.

**Sensors, shriekers, wardens and allays.** The listener code is unchanged
and was re-read against Java: candidates per tick (nearest, then the higher
frequency), chosen a tick later, travelling `floor(distance)` ticks;
activation for 30 (calibrated 10) ticks, then 10 of cooldown; power
`max(1, 15 - floor(15 / radius * distance))` by block distance; comparator
output of the last frequency while active; resonance through amethyst with
the frequency's `resonate_N` and pitch; the calibrated sensor's back input
filter and its unpowered back side; wool occlusion of all six nudged rays;
wool and carpet dampening; sneaking hiding the `ignore_vibrations_sneaking`
events; spectators and wardens heard by nothing. Shriekers still hear only
tendril clicks from a player; because plates, uses and dismounts now name
their player, a player pressing a plate by a sensor beside a shrieker warns
them as in Java. Wardens hear the new events through the same queue
(`warden_can_listen`). Allays keep their own note block channel, which has
the same radius and occlusion.

## Differences from Java

- Events posted where only the entity simulation is at hand (projectiles,
  teleports, dismounts, chicken eggs, rockets) are queued and reach
  listeners at the next entity update, a part of a tick later, as the v0.16
  record already noted for eating and arrows.
- `projectile_land` is posted when a projectile is removed by a hit or
  sticks, at the solid block just ahead of it (or its own cell), otherwise
  at the projectile; Java uses the exact hit result. Piercing arrows post
  once, when they stop. Fireworks don't post `projectile_land`.
- Some sources aren't named: witch potions, ghast and blaze fireballs,
  chicken eggs, villager farming, mob block changes and a copper golem's
  container use. Listeners only read a source for shriekers (players) and
  wardens (anger), so these differ only there.
- Players are always named as not sneaking for bow and crossbow shots,
  rocket bursts and dismounts (`EventSource::player(id, false)`), as before
  for bows.
- `container_open` for chests is posted on the use when no player or golem
  views the chest; Java counts openers in the block entity. Barrels and
  ender chests post `container_open` on every use (the game doesn't track
  their viewers) and no `container_close`.
- Doors toggled by power post at the lower half; Java posts at whichever
  half received the neighbour update first.
- The tripwire hook's sound still plays once, at the hook that changed; the
  events are at both hooks as in Java.
- Mob `step`, `swim`, `hit_ground` and `splash` are emitted by every mob
  kind; Java's per-type `MovementEmission` (flyers emit none) isn't
  distinguished (unchanged from v0.16).
- Equipment events cover player armor only; Java's mobs post `equip` or
  `unequip` for every slot change after their first tick.

## Remaining gaps

The `gap` rows of `COVERAGE` in `tests/game_events.rs` list every Java call
site the game has the action for but doesn't post yet. In short: flapping;
mob bounces and mob mounts; leash, lead knot and leash shearing events;
`/kill`; totems; bow, crossbow, shield and spyglass `item_interact_start` /
`finish`; armadillo, camel, nautilus, sniffer, panda, fox, rabbit, ravager,
creaking, sulfur cube and evoker actions; mooshroom, snow golem, copper golem
and sulfur cube shearing; armor stand, frame, painting, item, boat and
minecart damage and breaking; shulker peeking; boats over bubble columns;
vehicle chest closing; goat horns; frost walker; monster and trial spawners;
`Level.destroyBlock` from mobs, falling blocks and fluids; harvesting
beehives; dripleaf tilting; falling suspicious blocks; cauldrons filling
from weather and dripstone; decorated pot wobbles; dried ghasts; eyeblossoms;
farmland and paths reverting; dripstone transferring fluid; powered shelves;
sniffer eggs; berry bushes growing; turtle eggs cracking; bees entering and
leaving hives; campfires finishing food; creaking hearts. The `absent` rows
list Java actions the game doesn't have (spawn eggs, dispensers using shears,
flint and steel or bottles, endermen carrying blocks, snow golem trails,
wolves shaking, ice and snow melting by light, and others).

## Seams with other tracks

- Track B (pistons): `extend_piston` posts `block_destroy` for the block it
  breaks and `block_activate` after extending; `retract_piston` posts
  `block_deactivate`. A reworked piston should post the same at
  `triggerEvent` and `moveBlocks` (including blocks a sticky retraction
  destroys).
- Track C2 (dispense table): `dispense` posts the empty click (dispensers
  only), bucket, mob bucket and TNT events; dispensed projectiles post in
  `Entities::dispense_*`. New behaviours should post their Java events
  (shears, flint and steel, spawn eggs, bottles, carved pumpkins).
- Track D1 (carts): minecart placement posts `entity_place` in the use path,
  and `Entities::dismount` posts `entity_dismount`. Chest minecart closing
  isn't posted.
- Track A (update order): the events are posted where today's redstone code
  makes the change; a reordered update keeps them with the change.
- Shared APIs added: `World::use_redstone_by`, `ring_bell_by`,
  `strike_note_by`, `redstone_contacts_from`, `player_vitals`,
  `animal_block`, `container_has_viewers`, `emit_block_event`;
  `PlayerSession::event_source`; `Entities::vehicle_pos`; `Player::bounced`;
  `EntityEvent::Vibration` (a new variant, matched in `app/mobs.rs`). No IDs,
  saved formats or append-only orderings changed.
