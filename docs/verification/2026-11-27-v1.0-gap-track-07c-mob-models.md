## What changed

### 1. Baby models (MOB-06)

Java 26.3 registers a baby layer for 41 entity kinds. Track 7b gave 9 of
them their own models; the ghastling is Java's adult happy ghast scaled by
0.2375, which this game already drew. This track adds the other 31
(armadillo, axolotl, bee, camel, cat, ocelot, dolphin, donkey, mule,
horse, skeleton and zombie horses, fox, goat, llama and trader llama,
panda, polar bear, rabbit, squid and glow squid, turtle, hoglin and
zoglin, piglin, zombified piglin, strider, zombie villager, mooshroom,
sniffer and nautilus; the nautilus was not on the brief's list but is in
the jar's), drawn at full scale.

They are built on a new rig (`src/entity/model/rig.rs`): a table of
Java's parts, parents first, with each part's `PartPose` offset and
rotation in Java's axes and its boxes written from Java's `addBox`
numbers (`jbox`); the animation sets the parts' offsets and turns as
Java's `setupAnim` does, and `Rig::parts` composes each part with its
parents (`ModelPart.translateAndRotate`) and maps Java's space onto this
game's by the half turn about X Track 7b established. Keyframe
animations run on the same rig (`src/entity/model/keyframes.rs`, Java's
sampling: linear and Catmull-Rom keys, looping, `applyWalk`'s speed and
strength; the model's own root part for "root" channels). Colours,
patterns and details (eyes with glints, noses, snouts, tusks, stripes,
manes, belly patches) are this game's own, in the adults' palettes, and
the adults' recolouring applies (coat variants, mooshroom and brown
panda hides, glow squid, axolotl variants, collars). The babies are in
`src/entity/model/baby_rigs.rs`; each animates with its Java model:

- **Quadrupeds** (goat, polar bear, panda, llama, turtle): `QuadrupedModel`'s
  walk and look; the goat kid holds its head 22.5 degrees down unless it
  rams, its horns as it has them; the panda cub sits, eats, is scared,
  sneezes, frets and lies on its back (`BabyPandaModel.animateSitting`);
  the turtle hatchling sweeps its flippers on land and rows in water.
- **Hoglin and zoglin**: ears flopping with the walk, the head looking
  round and butting up 2.5 pixels higher over the attack, legs on
  `cos(pos) * 1.2`.
- **Camel calf**: `CamelBabyAnimation`'s walk on the walk, its sit, sit
  pose, stand-up and dash on Track 3's pose clock; the head within 30
  degrees and -25 to 45.
- **Fox kit**: `FOX_BABY_WALK`, the look (tilted while begging), sitting
  and curled asleep with its legs hidden (`BabyFoxModel`'s poses).
- **Kitten and ocelot kit**: `BabyFelineModel.setupAnim` (walk, sprint,
  crouch, sit); the tamed kitten's collar.
- **Rabbit kit**: `BabyRabbitAnimation`'s hop and head tilt on Track 7b's
  states.
- **Foals** (horse, skeleton and zombie horse; donkey and mule):
  `AbstractEquineModel.setupAnim` with each foal model's overrides,
  including grazing, chewing and rearing (section 3); the donkey foal's
  head stays at Java's fixed 30 degrees up.
- **Axolotl**: `BabyAxolotlModel`'s one keyframe animation at a time as
  `Axolotl.setupAnimationStates` picks it (a new `mob_anim` state):
  playing dead, swimming, walking, floating, resting.
- **Bee, squid, glow squid, dolphin**: `BeeModel`, `SquidModel` (with
  `SquidRenderer`'s baby offsets), `DolphinModel` on their baby parts;
  the dolphin calf replaces Track 3's own calf model.
- **Armadillo pup**: the look within its clamps and
  `ARMADILLO_BABY_WALK`; rolled up, the ball (`cube`).
- **Piglin, zombified piglin**: the babies' 5-degree ears; a piglin cub
  walks, swings, dances and admires gold; a zombified cub raises its arms
  with its golden sword.
- **Strider**: `BabyStriderModel.customAnimations` (bob, leg lift,
  bristles).
- **Zombie villager**: a humanoid with zombie arms; it shakes while cured.
- **Snifflet**: `SniffletModel` with `BABY_TRANSFORM`'s larger head; the
  look, walk and digging poses as this game's adult sniffer.
- **Baby nautilus**: `NautilusModel`'s turn and `NautilusAnimation.SWIMMING`
  on the walk position plus a fifth of `ageInTicks`.
- **Mooshroom calf**: the calf model, painted red or brown, no mushrooms
  (as `MushroomCowRenderer`).

Babies with their own models are no longer squashed by the adults'
height factors (`animals::model_scale`): Track 7b's wolf pup stands at
Java's full height (it was drawn at 0.8 of it).

### 2. Java's joints (MOB-06)

- **Guardians**: the tail on `GuardianModel`'s three joints (each turning
  `sin(tail) * pi` times 0.05, 0.1 and 0.15 on the last, with Java's
  segment sizes), Java's twelve spikes on the body's edges (`SPIKE_*`
  positions scaled to this game's body, Java's turns), and the eye
  (section 3).
- **Phantom**: each wing in two parts, the tip beating by the base's
  angle again; the tail on its two joints, drooping 5 to 10 degrees on
  `cos(2 * anim)`.
- **Pufferfish**: the blue fins flapping, `zRot` -+0.2 +- 0.4
  sin(ageInTicks * 0.2).
- **Parrot**: each leg on its own joint, walking standing
  (`cos(pos * 0.6662) * 1.4 * speed`, the left leading) and splaying 20
  degrees while it dances.
- **Rabbit and bat**: rebuilt on `AdultRabbitModel`'s and `BatModel`'s
  part trees (Java's proportions: the adult rabbit is twice as large as
  this game drew it, and no longer flattened), with `RabbitAnimation`'s
  hop and head tilt and `BatAnimation`'s flying and resting keyframes on
  each part.
- **Horse rearing per part**: Java's `AbstractEquineModel` placement: the
  body (and what it carries: tail, markings, chests, saddle and armor)
  turns up 45 degrees about its pivot, the front legs rise 12 pixels and
  come back 4, pawing from 60 degrees forward, the head rises 8 and
  comes back 8; Track 7b turned the whole front of the horse instead.
- **Walk phases on Java's sides**: the right hind leg (-X) on the walk's
  cosine for the pig, cow, mooshroom, sheep, creeper, polar bear, panda,
  llama, trader llama, wolf, fox and goat; the cat's and ocelot's legs,
  the hoglin's and zoglin's, the horse family's (front and hind) and the
  strider's (with its rock) on Java's sides; the wolf's tail wag turns
  Java's way.

### 3. Grazing, the eye, the crossbow and LAN state (MOB-06)

- **Grazing and chewing** (`eatAnim`, `mouthAnim`, from Track 2c's
  `mounts::State`): the head drops 7 pixels and turns down toward 125
  degrees, the yaw fading, and shakes `sin(ageInTicks) * 0.05` while it
  chews, for adults and foals.
- **Guardian eye**: it slides up to two pixels toward the side of what
  the guardian watches (its attack target, else the viewer, as
  `GuardianRenderer.getEntityToLookAt`; the square root of the bearing's
  sine, twice) and a pixel down when that is below
  (`aquatic::track_eye`, from the build's camera).
- **Piglin crossbow draw**: `CROSSBOW_CHARGE`'s arms over the 25 ticks the
  crossbow loads (`Mob::crossbow_draw`, the first 1.25 s of the piglin's
  `charge`), before Track 2b's `CROSSBOW_HOLD`.
- **Admiring piglins**: the model draws `PiglinModel`'s `ADMIRING_ITEM`
  (head 0.5 down and level, the off hand raised; adults already did, cubs
  now too), whatever the look says, so Track 2c's 34-degree look
  stand-in can be dropped by a later track.
- **LAN**: the squid's tentacle cycle, the bee's `shouldRoll` and the
  piglin's draw ride with every tick's pose (`tc`, `roll`, `draw` in
  `app::lan`; the squid's and bee's are also in `Mob::look`), so guests
  open the tentacles, tumble and draw as the host's mobs do.
- **Client random rolls** (guardian spikes out of water, horse tail
  swishes, the rabbit's idle timeout) stay on Track 7b's per-mob,
  per-tick hash. Not observable: Java's client draws them from each
  entity's own unseeded `RandomSource`, so no two clients agree and
  nothing reproduces them; both give independent uniform values each
  tick. Decided not to change.

## Remaining differences

- **Baby keyframes not run**: the camel calf's idle (ears and tail, every
  80 to 120 ticks on a random timeout), the armadillo pup's roll-up,
  roll-out and peek (rolled up it shows only the ball, as this game's
  adult), the snifflet's sniffer keyframes (it moves as this game's adult
  sniffer does), and every `SCALE` channel (a rig can't scale boxes:
  `FOX_BABY_WALK`'s 4, `BabyAxolotlAnimation`'s 11, one each in the
  camel calf's and the rabbits' sets).
- **Baby layers not drawn**: foal markings, the cria's decor (Java's
  babies carry no chests; the trader cria's blanket isn't drawn), the
  zombie villager cub's profession and armor, baby armor on the piglin
  cub drawn with the baby zombie's proportions (`PIGLIN_BABY_ARMOR` isn't
  separate).
- **Adult nautilus faces the other way**: Java's nautilus (adult and baby)
  has its mouth at the back and swims shell first; the baby follows
  Java, this game's adult has its tentacles in front. A decision for the
  owner (the adult's art).
- **Guardian**: this game's body is 12 x 13.6 x 12 against Java's 16-pixel
  cube, so the spikes and tail are placed on it, scaled; the whole
  guardian doesn't pitch with its look (`head.xRot`).
- **Horses**: the adults' boxes are this game's own; their parts now move
  on Java's placements, but the head pivots where this game's head
  stands (offset by Java's moves), not at Java's neck pivot.
- **Not converted** (outside this track): the bamboo stalk and leaf
  planes (Track 7b's open decision); armadillo, sniffer and camel adults'
  walk keyframes per part (they keep Track 7's phase approximations);
  LAN guests still don't know a piglin is fighting except while it draws.
