## What changed

### Zombies break doors (`mob::door_break`)

- `Zombie.setCanBreakDoors` adds `BreakDoorGoal` at priority 1 with **no
  flags** for every zombie kind with `CanBreakDoors` (zombies, husks,
  drowned, zombie villagers and zombified piglins: all have ground or
  amphibious navigation), and sets `navigation.setCanOpenDoors`, so their
  paths go through closed wooden doors (`Caps::can_open_doors`). The
  saved `CanBreakDoors` and its spawn roll (`0.1 × special multiplier`)
  were already in place.
- `canUse` (full updates only): pressed against something
  (`horizontalCollision`), an unfinished path with a wooden door (any door
  but iron) in the cell one above one of its nodes up to the one after
  next, within 1.5 blocks of the mob measured to the cell's corner (as
  Java's integer `BlockPos` coordinates do), else one above the mob's own
  block; then `mobGriefing`, **Hard** (`DOOR_BREAKING_PREDICATE`) and a
  closed door.
- Every tick (`requiresUpdateEveryTick`): one tick in 20 the
  `zombie.attack_wooden_door` sound (`levelEvent(1019)`, volume 2) and an
  arm swing; `breakTime += 1`; the crack stage
  `(int)(breakTime / 240 × 10)` (`destroyBlockProgress`) is drawn on the
  door like a player's mining crack (`Entities::door_cracks`, also sent to
  LAN clients with the host's cracks). At tick 240, if the difficulty
  still allows it, `EntityEvent::DoorBroken`: the world removes the cell
  silently (`removeBlock`), the door's other half loses its support and
  breaks (`destroyBlock` through `updateShape`: break particles, its break
  sound, a `block_destroy` vibration and, for the lower half, the door
  item), then `zombie.break_wooden_door` (`levelEvent(1021)`, volume 2).
- It stops (full updates) after tick 240, when the door opens, when the
  mob is 2 blocks from the cell's centre, or when the difficulty changes.
- The two sounds are original procedural stand-ins (a wooden knock and a
  splintering crash), appended to the hostile cue bank
  (`illager::Cue::DoorAttack`, `DoorBreak`; keys
  `zombie.attack_wooden_door`, `zombie.break_wooden_door`).
- **Vindicators** now share this goal (`VindicatorBreakDoorGoal`: its
  1 in `reducedTickDelay(10)` start roll, Normal or Hard in an active raid,
  and the `MOVE` flag, so it stands at the door). They used to remove both
  halves with no drop and no progress; they now show the crack, play the
  sounds and drop the door as Java's do.

### Goal scheduling for the shared chase (`mob::chase`)

- **Targets** for the shared hostile chase in `Mob::think` (zombies,
  husks, drowned, zombie villagers, zombified piglins, spiders, cave
  spiders, creepers, slimes, magma cubes, silverfish, witches, wither
  skeletons, skeletons and the other kinds that used it) now follow
  `NearestAttackableTargetGoal` and `TargetGoal`: a search only on the
  selector's full update (`(tickCount + id) % 2 == 0`, or the first two
  ticks), and then only one time in `reducedTickDelay(10)` = 5, for the
  nearest player in sight within the follow range (slimes and magma cubes
  only within 4 blocks up or down). The target is kept, rather than the
  nearest player taken every tick, while it is alive, targetable and
  within the follow range and was seen within `reducedTickDelay(60)` = 30
  full updates; out of sight longer, it is dropped (about 62 ticks; this
  replaces v1.0 Track 7's 3-second timer). Reinforcements' `setTarget` and
  a hit (`HurtByTargetGoal`) still set the target at once. Spiders in
  daylight let go one full update in 100 (`SpiderAttackGoal`).
- **`MeleeAttackGoal`** for the shared melee (every kind above except
  cubes, skeletons, witches, creepers and endermen, which keep their own
  attacks): `canUse` at most every 20 ticks
  (`COOLDOWN_BETWEEN_CAN_USE_CHECKS`), needing a path to the target or the
  target in reach; `canContinueToUse` ends the goal when the path runs out
  (spiders, `followingTargetEvenIfNotSeen`, keep going); `start` sets
  `ticksUntilNextAttack` to 0, so a fresh start may hit at once; every
  tick it re-plans through the existing `Mob::pursue` and hits when the
  attack box (the mob's box widened by `sqrt(2.04) − 0.6`) meets the
  target's and the target is in sight, then waits 20 ticks. The hit itself
  (`Mob::hostile_melee_hit`) is the old one: damage with the held weapon,
  push and attack knockback, Fire Aspect, husk Hunger, wither skeleton
  Wither and cave spider Poison.
- **Zombies' goal table**: zombies, husks and zombie villagers
  (`Zombie.addBehaviourGoals`) and zombified piglins run `SpearUseGoal`
  (priority 2; 1 for zombified piglins; `MOVE | LOOK`, full updates only)
  and `ZombieAttackGoal` (3; 2; `MOVE | LOOK`, every tick) through the
  existing `GoalSelector` port (`mob::selector`), with the door goal beside
  them. Drowned keep their own trident and attack goals (they use the
  shared `MeleeAttackGoal` without a trident).

### Mobs use spears (`SpearUseGoal`)

- A zombie, husk, zombie villager or zombified piglin with a spear in its
  main hand (a `KineticWeapon`: zombies' 1-in-6 rare iron spear, the camel
  husk rider's and the zombie horseman's iron spear, or a picked-up one)
  and a target: walks at it until within 10 blocks; lowers the spear
  (`startUsingItem`) for `reducedTickDelay(delay + damage window)` full
  updates (119 for an iron spear: 12 + 225 ticks); charges at the target;
  within 2 blocks of it, or at the path's end, picks a spot 6–7 blocks
  away from it (`LandRandomPos.getPosAway`, with Java's `lerp(sqrt(r),
  min, max) × √2` ring and quarter-turn spread) and runs there, then
  charges again; when the time is up it raises the spear, retreats 9–11
  blocks and finishes there or after `reducedTickDelay(100)` = 50 updates,
  and the selector starts it again. Riders add 2 to every distance.
- While lowered, each tick `KineticWeapon.damageEntities` stabs the
  players its look ray crosses within the mob attack range (1–2.25
  blocks, plus a tick of its own forward speed; 0.125 margin), each at
  most once per 10 ticks: the conditions with the mob factor 0.2 (an iron
  spear: dismount at 2.2 b/s, knockback at 1.02 b/s, damage at 0.92 b/s
  relative speed, within their tick windows after the 12-tick delay), for
  the mob's base attack damage (zombie 3, zombified piglin 5) plus
  `floor(relative speed × damage multiplier)`, with the hurt push and the
  0.4 extra knockback.
- Zombie horsemen and husks on camel husks: Java's rider drives its
  mount's navigation (`getNavigation` is the controlled vehicle's), so the
  mount now steers to the spear goal's destination at its
  `chargeSpeedModifier` (zombie horse 1.4, camel husk 4).

### Strider jockeys steer (MOB-04c)

- `Mob.getControllingPassenger`: a zombified piglin riding first (it isn't
  in `#non_controlling_rider`) controls the strider, and
  `updateControlFlags` turns off the strider's `MOVE` goals (panic, breed,
  tempt, go to lava, follow parent, stroll). Its goals walk the strider's
  navigation with the strider's malus (`shouldPassengersInheritMalus`): a
  calm piglin's `WaterAvoidingRandomStrollGoal` (one full update in
  `reducedTickDelay(120)` = 60, to a `LandRandomPos.getPos(10, 7)` spot,
  speed modifier 1), an angry one's `ZombieAttackGoal` (or `SpearUseGoal`)
  toward its target. Before this, a ridden strider's generic hunt aimed it
  at the nearest player whatever the piglin's mood.
- A baby strider riding an adult runs the strider goal table itself, so
  the adult keeps its own goals.

### Baby piglins ride baby hoglins (MOB-04c, `entity::piglin_ride`)

See [nether mobs](../nether-mobs.md#known-gaps): Java's sensor, ticker,
ride target memory, `Mount(0.8)`, `DismountOrSkipMounting(8,
wantsToStopRiding)`, `updateActivity`'s dismount, `getTopPassenger(hoglin,
3)` with `canAddPassenger` (three piglins at most), and the seats (baby
hoglin 0.875, adult hoglin 1.49375, baby piglin 0.98, less the baby
piglin's 0.1875 vehicle attachment). Riding links were already saved;
the ride memories aren't (Java's aren't either), so riders get off after
a reload, as Java's `updateActivity` makes them.

## Existing tests changed

The scheduling change makes a hostile mob find its target a few ticks
later (Java's one-in-five search) instead of on its first update, so these
tests now run until the hit or chase they look for, at the game's 0.05 s
step, rather than expecting it on the first call:
`husks_inflict_hunger_and_zombie_variants_follow_biome_rules`,
`wither_skeleton_hits_wither_and_drops_coal_bones_and_rare_skulls`,
`magma_cubes_hurt_when_tiny_and_only_larger_cubes_drop_cream`,
`thorns_rolls_fractional_damage_up_to_five` (now at night, so the zombie
doesn't burn while it looks), `spiders_hunt_at_night_or_when_hit_and_climb_walls`
and `zombie::tests::reinforcements_hunt_their_target_out_to_follow_range`.
`zombie_attacks_with_cooldown` now checks Java's cadence (20 ticks
between hits, up to 24 when the goal restarts at its next 20-tick
`canUse` check) instead of the old 1-second cooldown at 60 updates a
second. Two spawn tests whose counts depend on the random sequence, which
the target search now draws from, accept a zombie horse (a rare natural
spawn) and up to two mobs over the monster cap (a zombie horseman is two):
`spawn_rules` and `distant_players_get_their_own_mob_caps`. In
`tests/mob_navigation_swim_fly.rs`, the zombie now waits until it has
found its target before the wall goes up (it can't find one through the
wall), and the detour counts once the zombie passes the wall's face
beside it (it hugs the corner at |z| ≈ 1.49, just inside the old 1.5).

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Piglins with spears**: Java's piglins spawn with a golden spear in
  one of ten melee rolls (`createSpawnWeapon`) and zombified piglins in
  one of twenty, and piglins fight with it through `SpearApproach`,
  `SpearAttack` and `SpearRetreat` in their fight activity. VoxelCraft's
  piglins and zombified piglins model their weapon as a saved
  `nether::Weapon` (sword, crossbow, axe), so neither spawn roll nor the
  piglin's spear behaviours are built; a zombified piglin that holds a
  spear in its equipment does use `SpearUseGoal`.
- **Spear stabs** hit players only (Java's ray also stabs other mobs in
  the way); a contact that only knocks back or only dismounts does
  nothing, because the game applies a mob's knockback to a player only
  with damage, and there is no event to dismount a player; spears don't
  wear when a mob stabs; the mob's look is the exact direction to the
  target's eyes rather than `lookAt`'s 30° per tick turn.
- **Riders** keep a `MeleeAttackGoal` while their target lives (their
  navigation is the mount's, which steers by `hunt` here), and the
  undead mounts' approximate hunt (nearest player) remains when the rider
  has no spear goal.
- **Door goal**: `LandRandomPos` home pulls are left out for the spear's
  spots (zombies have no home); the zombie still walks into the door
  while breaking it (the goal is flagless), as Java's does.
- **Strider jockeys**: the rider's stroll uses the strider's stroll speed
  and the land-position search from the strider's position; Java's
  rider also looks around (`LookAtPlayerGoal`) and the strider's look
  goals stop, which the retained head code doesn't model.
- **Baby piglin rides**: the activity check approximates Java's activity
  list (admiring, fighting, avoiding and celebrating come first); the
  riding piglin's look behaviours aren't modelled.

## What remains unverified
