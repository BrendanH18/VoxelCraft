## Scope

The plan's Track 4: the dragon landing on Java's scanning perch rather than
the flaming one; a player switching to creative and the strafe; the
dragon's death flight to the portal (`DragonDeathPhase`); End gateway
cooldowns and beams (`TheEndGatewayBlockEntity`) and entities' portal
cooldown; and Java's `EndIslandFunction` for new worlds' End islands in a
new generation 7 (owner decision, 2026-11-27), with the End's biome
thresholds, chorus, cities and spike layout kept consistent with the new
island mass, and island-shape fixtures checked against the jar.

## What changed

### Generation 7: Java's End islands (GEN-04b)

`Generation::V7` (`worldgen=7`) is what new worlds use. It changes only the
End; the Overworld and Nether generate as in generation 6, and generations
1–6 are untouched (their golden chunk hashes pass unchanged; see the checks).
Existing worlds keep their islands.

`world::end_islands` ports Java 26.3's End noise router. A block's final
density is
`squeeze(interpolated_8x4x8(0.64 · lerp(y 4→32, −0.234375,
lerp(y 56→312, −23.4375, islands + base_3d_noise))))` (plus the beardifier,
zero here), and the block is end stone where it is above zero:

- `islands` (`end/islands`, the router's `erosion`): the main island's
  falloff `(clamp(100 − distance to the origin at y 0, −100, 80) − 8) / 128`,
  max `EndIslandFunction`: per 8-block section, over the 25 × 25
  surrounding chunks more than 64 chunks out whose `SimplexNoise` (a legacy
  random on the seed, 17292 ints in, no offset) is below −0.9, an island of
  size `(|x|·3439 + |z|·147) % 13 + 9`, giving `(doffs − 8) / 128`.
- `base_3d_noise`: `BlendedNoise(0.25, 0.25, 80, 160, 4)` on a legacy random
  on the seed: two 16-octave and one 8-octave `SmearedPerlinNoise` stacks,
  the main one choosing between the limits.
- The interpolation fills each cell as `InterpolatedFunction`'s volume
  sampler does (z, then x, then stepping up in y), as chunk generation and
  `getBaseColumn` use it.

Every step uses the jar's arithmetic: its floats where it uses floats, its
doubles where it uses doubles, in the same order. `JavaRandom` gained
`next_double`.

`world::end::EndGen` builds generation 7's End on it: the terrain per
16-block chunk (cached, 4 KiB each), `column` (top and bottom of a column,
which in generation 7 can overhang), `solid`, the End biomes from Java's
island value at each chunk's middle (`TheEndBiomeSource`, the same
thresholds), chorus plants on the highlands' end stone (their room test
now asks the 3D terrain), End city starts on the new surface
(`first_free`), the podium on the island's top at the origin, generation 5's
spike layout and gateway order, and gateway exits through the existing
`findOrCreateValidTeleportPos` search over the new chunks. Terrain now
reaches y 127 in generation 7 (generation 1–6 chunks above y 104 stay air).

New fixtures, `tests/fixtures/java/26.3/end_islands.json` (described in the
[fixture README](../../tests/fixtures/java/26.3/README.md#the-ends-islands-v10-gap-closing-track-4)),
written by the new `tools/extract_java_end_islands_fixtures.py` with
`tools/JavaEndIslands.java`, which runs the jar's own router: for seeds 0,
42 and −4172144997902289642, `EndIslandFunction` and `end/islands` as raw
float bits on 961 points each, and the noise columns
`NoiseBasedChunkGenerator.getBaseColumn` generates for every column of five
chunks each (the central island, its edge, and the first island chunks
along rays past the void). The port matches all 5,766 values bit for bit and
all 3,840 columns block for block.

### The dragon (PRO-05)

- **Landing** (`DragonLandingPhase`): flies to the bottom centre of the
  egg's cell (worked out on the phase's first tick) and, within a block of
  it, resets the flame count and sits **scanning**
  (`SITTING_SCANNING`). Before, it went to `SITTING_FLAMING` (counting a
  flame), snapped onto the perch and landed anyway after 400 ticks.
- **Sitting** (`DragonSittingScanningPhase`, `DragonSittingAttackingPhase`):
  the scan takes only players within 20 blocks and at most 10 above or
  below (the `scanTargeting` selector; VoxelCraft ignored height), measures
  the facing angle from the body and turns from the head as Java does, and
  the roar lasts until `attackingTicks++ >= 40` (one tick longer than
  before). The flame and takeoff rules were already Java's.
- **Strafe** (`DragonStrafePlayerPhase`): the phase keeps its `attackTarget`,
  so a player who switches to creative (or dies, or leaves) is still the
  target, flown at and shot at, until a fireball ends the strafe. Before,
  VoxelCraft returned to the holding pattern. A strafe with no target (a
  reloaded wild dragon) goes back to circling, as Java's does. A crystal
  broken by a creative player no longer starts a strafe
  (`onCrystalDestroyed`'s `canAttack`).
- **Death** (`handleKillingBlow`, `DragonDeathPhase`): a killing blow in
  flight holds the dragon at one health in `DYING`; it flies (fly speed 3)
  to the bottom centre of the cell above the exit portal's column
  (`MOTION_BLOCKING`, so the egg counts), and dies (health 0) once within
  10 blocks of it or beyond 150; only then does `tickDeath` rise and pay
  out. Killed while perched (or hovering) it dies where it is, as before.
  While flying it can't be hurt, and crystal healing is undone each tick
  (Java heals before the phase resets the health). The death flight saves:
  the fight record writes a `;death_flight` section (an older build reads
  it as nothing and loads a one-health dragon); a dragon no fight owns
  saves its phase and health. The death rays start with `tickDeath`.

### Gateways (GEN-04b)

`entity::dragon::gateway` keeps each End gateway's block entity
(`TheEndGatewayBlockEntity`) with the fight, which knows every gateway (the
twenty kills open and the exits built for them):

- **Age**, saved: a new gateway (a kill's, or an exit built on a first trip)
  starts at 0; gateways from older saves load past their spawn beam.
- **Cooldown**: using a gateway (the player, or a thrown pearl) sets it to
  40 ticks; every 2400 ticks of age it does so by itself. While it cools
  down nothing can use it: the player passes through, and a pearl flies on
  through it. Gateways tick only while their chunk is loaded.
- **Beams** (`TheEndGatewayRenderer`): magenta while spawning (200 ticks),
  purple while cooling down, at `sin(percent·π)` scale, `height =
  floor(scale × (world height or 50))` below and above the block, radii 0.15
  and 0.175, drawn within 256 blocks. LAN clients get the gateways' state
  with the fight replica.
- **Portal cooldown** (`Entity.portalCooldown`, `Player`'s 10 ticks): after
  a gateway trip the player can't use a gateway for half a second, and
  touching one meanwhile restarts the wait (`setAsInsidePortal`) and still
  sets the gateway cooling, as Java does.

The fight's save adds `;g x y z age cooldown` per gateway; older builds
ignore the sections.

## Remaining differences

- **Small islands and return gateways.** Java's End biomes also place
  `end_island_decorated` (small floating islands in `small_end_islands`)
  and `end_gateway_return` (return gateways in the highlands); neither is
  generated, in any generation, as before. Chorus plants still draw from a
  seeded `JavaRandom` per chunk rather than Java's decoration seed (the
  outer-End record's difference), so plant positions don't match Java's
  even on Java's islands; End city starts do (Java's placement on Java's
  terrain).
- **Spawn platform.** VoxelCraft still generates the obsidian arrival
  platform at (100, 49, 0) with the terrain (Java builds it on arrival
  only); on generation 7's islands it may sit inside or beside island
  terrain where Java's would be carved on arrival. Arrival rebuilds it as
  before.
- **The dragon's flight** between targets is still VoxelCraft's `fly`
  (Track 7b's note): the death flight and landing use Java's targets and
  speeds, but the exact track isn't claimed tick for tick. Arrows pass
  through a dragon in its death flight (Java's would hit and do nothing).
- **The roar's growl** plays once as the roar starts; Java's client plays
  it every tick of the phase.
- **Gateways' age in older saves** isn't known (Java saves it): they load
  at 200, so their first attention flash comes 2200 ticks after loading.
  Only gateways the fight knows of keep block entities; a gateway placed
  some other way (none can be in survival) has none and never cools down.
- **Portal cooldown** is modelled for gateways only; the Nether and End
  portals keep their own rules (the Nether portal's step-out lock).
- **Beams** use VoxelCraft's beam boxes (as beacons do), not Java's
  textured, scrolling beam.
