## What changed

### 1. Villager inventories and farm work (`src/entity/villager_breeding.rs`, `src/entity/mob/brain_walk.rs`, `src/entity/mob/head.rs`, `src/entity/villager.rs`, `src/app/mobs.rs`, `src/world/composter.rs`)

- **The 8-slot inventory** (`AbstractVillager.inventory`) holds what Java's
  villagers carry, not only food. It is the saved `food` list (no new key):
  older saves hold food only, and loading now keeps any item in it.
- **Pickup**, every tick with `mobGriefing` (`Mob.aiStep`, before only once
  a second during the village pass), by `Villager.wantsToPickUp`: the
  `villager_picks_up` tag (wheat seeds, potatoes, carrots, beetroot seeds,
  torchflower seeds, pitcher pods, bread, wheat, beetroots), villager food,
  or the profession's requested items (the farmer's wheat, wheat seeds,
  beetroot seeds and bone meal), with room for it (`canAddItem`), onto
  stacks of the same item and then the first empty slot (`addItem`).
  Sleeping villagers pick up too, as Java's do.
- **Food**: food points count only `VILLAGER_FOOD` (bread 4, carrots,
  potatoes and beetroots 1); breeding eats only food (`eatUntilFull`), so
  carried seeds and wheat stay.
- **`HarvestFarmland`** no longer replants at once: breaking a ripe crop
  (`destroyBlock`, now with the block's break particles and sound) drops
  its items for the farmer to pick up, and on empty farmland a farmer with
  `hasFarmSeeds` plants the first `villager_plantable_seeds` stack in its
  inventory that places a block (`item.crop.plant`, `block_place`), one
  item at a time.
- **`WorkAtComposter`** (a farmer's `WorkAtPoi.useWorkstation` at a
  composter): `makeBread` (with 36 bread or fewer, up to three bread from
  three wheat each; bread it can't carry drops half a block up) and
  `compostItems` (a full composter is emptied first, one bone meal at its
  top within 0.35 across; then from the last slot back, wheat and beetroot
  seeds beyond ten of each kind, at most 20, each `insertItem`: the first
  layer always, then a layer on the item's chance, stopping at level 7,
  whose 20-tick ripening is scheduled as the player's composting does).
- **`UseBonemeal`** (priority 4 for farmers, 7 for others in the work
  `RunOne`): every 10 ticks, 160 after the last session, with bone meal
  and no look or walk target, it picks a growing crop among the 27 cells
  round the villager (each found replacing the pick one time in its
  count), walks and looks at it (0.5, 1 short) and, within 1 of its centre
  and 40 ticks after the last, uses one bone meal on it
  (`BoneMealItem.growCrop`, the world's own bone meal with the player's
  bone meal sound) and picks the next; 80 uses or 60 ticks end it.
  `Mob::using_bone_meal` says when it runs (Java holds bone meal in the
  main hand), for the model.
- **`TradeWithVillager`'s throws**, each tick the partner is within
  `sqrt(5)`: excess food (24 points or more) to a farmer or a partner that
  wants more food (under 12), a farmer's wheat beyond 32, and a farmer
  partner's requested items it doesn't request itself; each the first
  matching stack's half (over half a stack) or excess over 24, thrown from
  0.3 below the eyes at 0.3 blocks a tick toward the partner's feet, 10
  ticks before pickup. The game's own direct food sharing between
  villagers within `sqrt(5)` (outside any behaviour) is gone. `Seen` now
  carries whether a villager is a farmer and wants more food.

### 2. Brutes and the fox (`src/entity/mob.rs`, `src/entity/fox.rs`, `src/entity/mob/fox.rs`, `src/entity/lifecycle.rs`, `src/app/lan.rs`, projectiles)

- **The brute's home**: every brute gets `HOME` where it spawns
  (`PiglinBrute.finalizeSpawn`), so spawn eggs, `/summon` and other spawns
  stroll home as bastion residents do; a saved brute loads its own
  (`home` was already written, null when absent).
- **`DefendTrustedTargetGoal` against players and projectiles**: a player's
  `lastHurtByMob` is now a mob or a player. Besides mob melee and arrows
  (which already recorded it), the shooters of tridents (already), breeze
  wind charges, llama spit, shulker bullets and Wither skulls are recorded,
  and a LAN PvP hit that lands records the attacking player. A fox defending
  a trusted player targets that player unless it trusts it too, or it is
  creative, spectating or dead; it bites the player for 2 with Java's melee
  knockback (`PlayerHit`), which records the fox as the bitten player's
  attacker in turn.

### 3. Item particles and the goat (`src/particles/`, `src/render/particles.rs`, `src/render/shaders/particles.wgsl`, `src/app/hands.rs`, `src/entity/thrown.rs`, `src/entity/animals.rs`, `src/entity/animals/long_jump.rs`, `src/audio/goat.rs`)

- **`ItemParticleOption`** (`particles::Request::Item`, sent to LAN
  clients): Java's `BreakingItemParticle`: its own random motion cut to a
  tenth plus the given velocity, gravity 1, half the usual size, white, a
  random quarter of the item's sprite, filtered by the Particles setting
  and drawn within 32 blocks. Item sprites come from the item icon array,
  which the particle shader now samples (`light.w` 4); block items show
  their block's texture.
  - **Eating** food (not drinks, which have no consume particles): 5 with
    each chew sound and 16 on finishing, 0.6 ahead of the eyes
    (`spawnItemParticles`).
  - **Breaking a tool**: 5, and every break (mining and shooting too, not
    only attacks and uses) now plays the item-break sound.
  - **Snowballs and eggs breaking** (entity event 3): eight, still for a
    snowball, up to 0.04 a tick each way for an egg (blue and brown too).
  - **The fox eating** (entity event 45 with `FOX_EAT`): eight of its
    mouth item, half a block ahead at its feet, rising.
- **The goat's `LongJumpToRandomPos`** replaces the stand-in straight-up
  jump. Off its `LONG_JUMP_COOLDOWN_TICKS` (600 to 1200 ticks, set at
  spawn; the saved `jump_cooldown`), not tempted, breeding, walking or
  ramming, on the ground, out of water and lava and not on honey (else half
  a cooldown), it lists every cell within 5 each way, weighted by
  `ceil(distSqr)`. Each tick (20 tries) it draws candidates, removing
  them, until one is acceptable (another column, over a solid-rendering
  block, a free path type), has an arc at one of 65°, 70°, 75° or 80° in a
  shuffled order (`LongJumpUtil`: gravity 0.08, at most 1.5 blocks a tick,
  0.95 of it, the `LONG_JUMPING` box swept along the arc) and can't be
  walked to within 8 blocks of path. It then looks at the spot for 40
  ticks, standing still (moving ends the jump with half a cooldown), and
  launches; `LongJumpMidJump` flies it without friction or drag under 0.08
  gravity until it is on the ground (or 100 ticks), then keeps a tenth of
  its speed across and waits a full cooldown. The jump times out after 200
  ticks.
- **Its sounds**: `entity.goat.long_jump` (a screaming goat's
  `entity.goat.screaming.long_jump`) at the launch and `entity.goat.step`
  at volume 2 on landing (`goat::Cue`, original procedural stand-ins in
  the established voice style, `src/audio/goat.rs`; `NEUTRAL`).

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Villager work sounds**: `playWorkSound` (the professions'
  `entity.villager.work_*`) still isn't played, and the composter's level
  events (1500's fill sound and particles, `block.composter.empty`) aren't
  drawn or played for villagers, as they aren't for the player's
  composting either. Level event 1505's happy-villager particles aren't
  drawn (the game's bone meal has none); its sound is the player's bone
  meal stand-in.
- **Planting**: the torchflower seeds and pitcher pods a farmer carries are
  planted as Java plants them, but `HarvestFarmland` and `UseBonemeal`
  recognise only the crops `Block::as_crop` knows (wheat, carrots,
  potatoes, beetroots), so a farmer doesn't harvest or fertilise
  torchflower crops (Java's `TorchflowerCropBlock` is a `CropBlock`).
- **The bone-meal hand**: the villager's held bone meal isn't drawn
  (`using_bone_meal` is there for the model).
- **Pickup reach**: the game's 1.75-block reach round the item, not Java's
  box inflated by 1 across.
- **Eating particles** follow the game's chew sound (every 5 ticks from the
  start), not Java's every 4 ticks after 21.875% of the eating time; only
  the host player's eating and tool breaks make them (split-screen and CLI
  players' don't yet). Mobs other than the fox (pandas, for instance)
  don't make eating particles.
- **Projectile attackers**: blaze and ghast fireballs carry no shooter in
  the game, so they don't record the player's attacker.
- **The goat's long jump**: the path type uses the game's
  `static_type` for the landing cell; the activity's "no walk target" is
  read as no running goal (the goat's movement is on the goal selector
  here). The look at the spot is the head's look target; the body turns
  as the game turns it (Java sets `yRot` to the body's at launch).
- 2f's other remaining differences stand (the trading lock's tick, play
  tag's order, the village stroll, chorus fruit's teleport sound,
  `ClimbOnTopOfPowderSnowGoal`).

## What remains unverified

No one played any of this: farmers picking up, planting, baking,
composting and using bone meal, villagers throwing items to each other,
brutes from spawn eggs strolling home, foxes defending against players
and projectiles, the item particles (their look in the item icon array is
untested on a GPU: the agent ran on the Pi and Boat has none, and the
shader change isn't covered by a screenshot), goats' long jumps and their
sounds (original procedural stand-ins nobody has listened to).
Performance: a villager checks every item in the world against its wants
once a tick through the mob index (only while items and villagers exist);
a farmer's `UseBonemeal` scans 27 cells one tick in ten; a goat starting a
long jump lists 1330 cells and, while it hasn't chosen, may test many of
them in one tick, each with up to four arcs and an 8-block path search,
as Java does once every 30 to 60 seconds per goat.
