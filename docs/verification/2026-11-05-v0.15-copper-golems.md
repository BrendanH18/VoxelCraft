## Reference and delivered behavior

The [entity record](2026-11-05-v0.15-copper-golem-entity.md) and
[statue record](2026-11-05-v0.15-copper-golem-statue.md) identify the installed
Java 26.3 artifacts, hashes, observed mechanics and original implementation.
Integration additionally inspected `CarvedPumpkinBlock`, `CopperChestBlock`,
`CopperGolem`, `CopperGolemStatueBlockEntity` and `BehaviorUtils` with the
installed `javap`. No reference source, models, textures or sounds were copied.

- Placing a carved pumpkin or jack o'lantern last next to one of the eight
  full copper blocks constructs a persistent golem. The two-cell pattern can
  be upright, inverted or horizontal. The pumpkin disappears and the copper
  becomes a chest, keeping stage/wax and using the pumpkin's facing. Existing
  chest pairing rules apply. The golem inherits oxidation but starts unwaxed,
  at the pumpkin's X/Z center and Y + 0.05, facing south.
- Copper-chest pickup and ordinary/trapped-chest delivery use the shared
  mutable-world simulation. At most sixteen items move at pickup. All stack
  components survive partial transfers, cargo saves, unload/reload and drops.
  A 60-tick chest inspection also occurs for empty/ineligible containers.
  Player/golem viewers, cats, double chests, replacement and loaded activity
  constrain access. The entity record gives search, queue and memory limits.
- Weathering uses absolute 504000–552000-tick deadlines from the saved global
  simulation clock, independent of time-setting and the day-cycle rule.
  Wax freezes it; axes remove wax before one oxidation stage. Empty-hand
  interaction returns the whole cargo with Java's directed 0.3-per-tick toss,
  eye-minus-0.3 origin and ten-tick pickup delay. A poppy equips an empty antenna;
  shears return its exact stack at Y + 1.5 and charge one durability.
- A fully oxidized golem converts only when its feet cell is air and active.
  The statue copies its name, chooses one of four poses and keeps facing.
  Cargo and antenna drop intact even with mob/entity loot disabled; a lead
  separately follows `entity_drops`. Conversion removes the mob without death
  loot. The fresh statue retains no hidden health, cargo or equipment.
- Shared statue uses cycle poses, wax/scrape, and reactivate an unaffected,
  unwaxed statue with an axe. Sneaking with either hand occupied bypasses the
  block callback; both hands empty still cycle poses. Waxed honeycomb clicks
  cycle poses without consumption. Reactivation restores only the name and
  facing on a fresh full-health golem, preserving water in a wet cell.
- Statue name/pose survive placement, pick-block, mining, explosions, saves
  and LAN component snapshots. Names persist across weathering/waxing and are
  cleared on removal. Inventory, first-person, dropped, player-held and
  golem-carried models use pose-bearing original geometry. Original synthesized
  golem action sounds append to existing sound keys.

## Limits

Owner play remains outstanding. Existing shared-engine limits include rich
custom-name text, container lock metadata, navigator parity and Java's precise
idle/panic Brain scheduling. Equal-distance container ties use spatial index
order. Original art/audio do not reproduce Mojang's animation curves or samples.
The existing copper wax/scrape sounds are generic material effects; lightning
cleaning state changes lack Java's level-event-3002 particle effect. These are
recorded gaps, not claims of full Java fidelity or Verified acceptance.
