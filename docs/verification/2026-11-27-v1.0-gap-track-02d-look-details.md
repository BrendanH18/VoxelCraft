## What changed

### 1. Each kind's own eye (`src/entity/mob/eyes.rs`)

- `Mob::eye_height` is Java's `getEyeHeight`: the `EntityTypes` eye height
  (or 0.85 of the height where Java gives none), each kind's baby eye
  (`BABY_DIMENSIONS.withEyeHeight`, or the adult's scaled by
  `getAgeScale`: 0.5, horses 0.7, turtles 0.3), 0.2 for a sleeping
  villager, a sitting camel's 0.845 (0.41 a baby), a digging sniffer's
  0.81, a warden digging or emerging 0.85, phantoms by size
  (`1 + 0.15 × size`), slimes, magma and sulfur cubes by size, and a floor
  shulker raised by its peek. `Mob::look_eye` uses it.
- Every look, sight line and target in the simulation that took 0.85 (or
  0.9) of a mob's box, a hunted mob's "one block up", or 1.62 for any
  player now takes the mob's own eye and the player's pose eye
  (`Target::eye`): attack and table goals' looks, line of sight for
  targeting (hostiles, golems, polar bears, pandas, illagers, raids, the
  Wither, wardens, dolphins, endermites, piglins and hoglins, creakings,
  bats, ocelots' prey), the witch's healing aim, a llama's spit, the wind
  charge's push, trident returns, `getFluidJumpThreshold`, the dolphin's
  thrown item, the allay's deposit. Iron golems, endermen and undead
  mounts keep the eye of what they hunt (`Mob::hunt_eye`); illagers,
  endermites, polar bears, pandas, wardens, piglins and witches keep their
  target's eye with its position.

### 2. Priorities, customers and brain writers (`src/entity/mob/head.rs`)

- **`GoalSelector`'s flag rule for the idle looks**: a running idle look
  goal holds `LOOK` (and `MOVE` for `RandomLookAroundGoal` and
  `InteractGoal`) at its priority. The table goals, strolls and raider
  goals whose priority the game knows hold their flags at theirs; a goal
  can take a flag only from strictly worse ones, and stops them when it
  starts. So the parrot's `LookAtPlayerGoal` (1) interrupts and holds off
  `FollowOwnerGoal` (2); the witch's `RandomLookAroundGoal` (3) holds off
  `LongDistancePatrolGoal` and `RaiderMoveThroughVillageGoal` (4) and an
  equal `PathfindToRaidGoal` (3) but yields to
  `ObtainRaidLeaderBannerGoal` (1) and its strolls and attacks (2); the
  panda's random look (10) interrupts its stroll (14) and follow-parent
  goal (13). Table goals' own look requests count at their priority, not
  as an outside request.
- **The customer**: villagers and wandering traders keep `customer`
  (`tradingPlayer`, not saved) beside `trading`, set by the app for the
  host, controller players and LAN players with the merchant's screen
  open (LAN players now count as trading too). `LookAtTradingPlayerGoal`
  and `LookAndFollowTradingPlayerSink` follow it.
- **Villager interaction looks**: `SetLookAndInteract(PLAYER, 4)` makes the
  nearest visible player within 4 blocks `INTERACTION_TARGET` and
  `LOOK_TARGET`; `ShowTradesToPlayer(400, 1600)` (adults, within
  `sqrt(17)`) keeps `LOOK_TARGET` on them for 40 ticks, 900 while the
  player holds the cost of an offer in stock, then erases the interaction;
  in the idle package at priority 3 (before the look `RunOne`), in work
  and meet at 10 (after); panic erases it. `WorkAtPoi.start` looks at the
  job site's centre.
- **The piglin's `SetLookAndInteract(PLAYER, 4)`** at the end of its idle
  activity; as in Java, nothing erases a piglin's `INTERACTION_TARGET`
  (it isn't saved).
- **Brain movement looks**: for brain kinds, `AnimalMakeLove` locks the
  partner's eyes in `LOOK_TARGET` each tick, `FollowTemptation` the
  tempting player's, and `BabyFollowAdult` the adult's when it sets its
  walk; the first two erase it when they stop. Goats, axolotls,
  armadillos, frogs and sniffers now sense the mobs around them
  (`EntityTracker.isVisibleBy`).
- **Brain attacks through the sink**: piglins', brutes', hoglins' and
  zoglins' attacks set `LOOK_TARGET` on the target's eyes
  (`MeleeAttack`, `CrossbowAttack`,
  `SetWalkTargetFromAttackTargetIfTargetOutOfReach`), which
  `LookAtTargetSink` follows; goal mobs (a zombified piglin or strider
  rider) keep asking the look control.
- **`RunOne` with a target set**: a look `RunOne` still starts; its
  `SetEntityLookTarget`s fail, so it runs its `DoNothing`, which delays the
  next pick (2c skipped it).

### 3. Kinds' own conditions

- **Bee**: `BeeLookControl.resetXRotOnTick` keeps the pitch while
  `BeePollinateGoal` runs (a saved flower, no nectar, not angry), and a bee
  hovering at its flower looks at it (`setLookAt(flowerPos)`).
- **Panda**: `PandaBreedGoal` finding no bamboo points its
  `PandaLookAtPlayerGoal` at the nearest player within 8
  (`BREED_TARGETING`, `setTarget`), which its next `canUse` takes; a found
  or set `lookAt` is kept while the panda can't act; while unhappy it turns
  toward its target (`lookAt(target, 90, 90)`).
- **Llama**: `RangedAttackGoal(1.25, 40, 20)`: an angry llama (or one with
  a wild wolf to spit at) looks at the target's eyes every tick
  (`setLookAt(target, 30, 30)`) and spits when `attackTime` counts down:
  40 ticks after the first, then every 40.

### 4. Own look controls

- **Warden**: a brain kind with the plain look and body controls. Its
  behaviours' look points (`SetWardenLookTarget` on the roar target or
  disturbance, `Roar`, the fight's walk target, `setDisturbanceLocation`)
  become `LOOK_TARGET` for `LookAtTargetSink(45, 90)`; the sonic boom asks
  the look control for the target's feet each tick. Its steering no
  longer turns its heading toward the look point.
- **Shulker**: `ShulkerLookControl` turns `yHeadRot` in the shell's frame
  (south turned by the opposite face's `Direction.getRotation`, right as
  its normal crossed with it), never pitches the head and doesn't clamp
  it to the body, whose `yBodyRot` stays 0 (`ShulkerBodyRotationControl`).
  Its goals: `LookAtPlayerGoal(8, 0.02, onlyHorizontal)` at 1,
  `ShulkerAttackGoal` at 4 (`setLookAt(target, 180, 180)`), the peek goal
  and `RandomLookAroundGoal` at 8; the look at 1 stops a running attack
  (the lid closes) and holds it off while it runs.
- **Bat**: the plain controls; resting, it sets `yHeadRot` to a random
  0..360 one tick in 200, and the head and body turn toward each other.
- **Phantom**: `PhantomLookControl` does nothing;
  `PhantomBodyRotationControl` puts the head on last tick's body, then the
  body on the heading.
- **Ghast**: `GhastLookGoal` sets `yBodyRot = yRot` each tick before the
  plain look control. **Happy ghast** (adult): `HappyGhastLookControl`
  turns heading, body and head together on a request or a still timeout,
  and otherwise faces the movement (body only); carrying anyone, head and
  body take the heading. The ghastling keeps the plain controls.

### 5. Spears on the dragon's parts

- `Entities::spear_dragon_parts` finds the fight's and wild dragons' parts
  (`EnderDragonPart` is pickable; the dragon isn't) along a spear's reach,
  with the margin's block check. A mob's stab lands on them, is
  remembered for the contact cooldown and does nothing
  (`EnderDragon.hurt` takes health only from players and explosions). A
  player's jab deals its damage and Sharpness (the dragon's part damage:
  a quarter plus one away from the head and neck), and a charge its
  contact damage; each landed stab wears the spear, tires the player and
  slows it as a stab on a mob does.

## Existing tests changed

- `entity::mob::head::tests::villagers_mostly_look_at_cats`: the player
  stands 6 blocks off instead of 3. Java's idle villagers run
  `SetLookAndInteract(PLAYER, 4)` before the look `RunOne`, so a player
  within 4 blocks becomes the look target first; the test is about the
  `RunOne`'s weights.
- `entity::piglin_ride::tests::baby_piglins_ride_baby_hoglins_for_a_while`:
  the pair stand in a two-block-high pen. With the look `RunOne` starting
  its `DoNothing` while a target is set (Java), the shared random stream
  moved and the baby hoglin's strolls took it more than 8 blocks off,
  where Java's `MAX_RIDE_DISTANCE` ends the ride target as soon as it is
  set; the expectations (mounting after 150 to 900 ticks, riding 150 to
  620) are unchanged.
- `release_0_7::a_hurt_llama_spits_once_and_the_spit_hits`: the first
  window is 60 ticks instead of 30. Java's `RangedAttackGoal` spits 40
  ticks after the llama starts aiming (2c spat at once); one spit still
  lands and none follows.
- `release_0_7::llamas_spit_at_wild_wolves_which_keep_away`: it checks that
  the llama spits at the wolf after its 40-tick aim (and that the wolf
  keeps away) rather than that the spit hurts it. The wild wolf flees the
  llama at 1.5 during the aim, and Java's `Llama.spit` aims where the
  wolf is without leading, so the spit misses the running wolf.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Scope decisions (stopped, not built)**:
  - Villagers' and piglins' `InteractWith` and the sniffer's and the
    celebrating piglin's other `RunOne` options (`SetWalkTargetFromLookTarget`,
    `Scenting`, `Sniffing`, `RandomStroll`) are movement behaviours: they
    set walk targets with the look, so porting them means replacing the
    villagers' idle wandering, the sniffer's own scent and sniff scheduler
    and the piglins' celebrate movement with Java's idle `RunOne`s. The
    look `RunOne`s keep 2c's stand-ins (the celebrate stroll counts as
    doing nothing for 10 to 20 ticks, the sniffer's other options as
    `DoNothing(5, 20)`).
  - The fox's faceplant, interest, crouch and pounce don't exist in the
    game (no `StalkPreyGoal` or `FoxPounceGoal`); their look rules
    (`FoxLookAtPlayerGoal`'s conditions, `resetXRotOnTick`) need the fox's
    hunting AI ported first. Only sleep stops its looks, as before.
- **Boxes**: eye heights follow Java's baby dimensions, but the game's
  baby boxes are still the adult's scaled by the age scale, where Java's
  own `BABY_DIMENSIONS` differ for some kinds (chickens, rabbits, piglins,
  zombies, villagers, hoglins and zoglins among them). The goat's
  long-jump pose and the camel's sitting box are as before.
- **`ShowTradesToPlayer`'s held item**: the villager doesn't hold up the
  offers matching the player's item (no display item in its hand).
- **The llama** stands while it aims (`RangedAttackGoal` also walks toward
  a target out of range or unseen for 5 ticks).
- **The shulker's drawn head** is its yaw in the shell's frame; Track 7c's
  model draws it inside the clinging rotation.
- **Players' spears** reach dragon parts but not End crystals, minecarts
  or decorations (mob stabs do since 2c).
- **The trading player** is whoever has the merchant's trade screen open
  (the host, a controller seat or a LAN player).
- 2c's other remaining differences (one goal selector read from game
  state outside these kinds, the sensing scan, camels' other writers,
  turtles' growth, the procedural sounds, the mount preview) stand.
