## What changed

### 1. The right hand (MOB-06)

Track 7 mapped Java's model space onto this game's by turning it through
the origin, which keeps every rotation's sign but is a mirror image: the
humanoids held their weapons on this model's +X, the mob's own left. The
true mapping is a half turn about X: Java's right goes to -X, the mob's
own right, and every y and z turn changes sign
(`Part::on_java_side`, `Parts::java_side` in
[`src/entity/model.rs`](../../src/entity/model.rs)). The humanoids
(zombie, husk, drowned, skeleton, stray, bogged, parched, wither skeleton,
zombified piglin, piglin, piglin brute, enderman), the illagers, the vex,
villagers, the wandering trader, witches, zombie villagers and the iron
golem build their limbs as before and move them onto Java's sides; the
head keeps its own turn (it follows the world, not a Java formula), and
the arms' aim takes Java's `head.yRot` as the head yaw. Held items
(`ItemInHandLayer`) and the piglins' modelled weapons are now in the right
hand, the walk's leg and arm phases are on the matching sides, the body
twists toward the swinging arm and armor's right and left pieces follow.
Piglin ears flop on Java's sides too.

### 2. Animations beyond the walk (MOB-06)

Java's client-side entity state is in
[`src/entity/mob_anim.rs`](../../src/entity/mob_anim.rs) (`Mob::anim`,
ticked with the walk in `Mob::advance_walk`, for LAN replicas too, read
with the partial tick); the models are in `src/entity/model*`.

- **Chicken and parrot.** `flap` and `flapSpeed` as `Chicken.aiStep` and
  `Parrot.calculateFlapping` keep them (speed +1.2 a tick in the air,
  -0.3 on the ground; `flapping` held at 1, decaying 0.9); wings open by
  `(sin(flap) + 1) * flapSpeed` (they used to flap on a timer). The
  parrot takes Java's poses: dancing (body, head, wings and tail circle a
  pixel a tick, the head rocks 0.4), sitting (a pixel down, tail up 30
  degrees, legs folded), flying (legs tucked 40 degrees) and standing;
  standing and flying it bobs `0.3 * flapAngle`, the tail swings with the
  walk and the wings open from 5 degrees. Its head, tail and legs are now
  their own parts (the same boxes in the same places), so it looks round.
- **Rabbit.** The hop runs on `RabbitAnimation.HOP`'s 0.75 s clock from the
  tick it leaves the ground (`jumpDuration` 15), the idle head tilt for 4 s
  every 180 to 220 ticks at rest (the head stops following the look while
  that state runs, as Java's does); this game's own drawn motion (body
  pitch, level head, kicking feet, a rocking tilt). The head is its own
  part; it used to turn the whole rabbit.
- **Fish.** Tails (the salmon's back half) on `sin(0.6 * ageInTicks)`,
  harder out of water; the body sways 4.3 degrees on the same wave and,
  out of water, lies on its side lifted a little (`CodRenderer` and
  friends). The pufferfish bobs `cos(age * 0.05) * 0.08` blocks.
- **Squid and glow squid.** Tentacles open outward by `tentacleAngle`; the
  body turns about its middle by `xBodyRot` and `zBodyRot`, from the
  simulation's tentacle cycle and the tick's movement (they used to sway
  inward on a timer).
- **Dolphin.** The body pitches with its swimming pitch and turns with its
  head; swimming it rocks on `cos(age * 0.3)` and the fluke beats.
- **Guardians.** The tail swings `sin(clientSideTailAnimation) * pi` times
  0.15 and the fin another 0.15 (Java's three joints 0.05, 0.1, 0.15);
  the spikes stand out by `1 + cos(age * 1.5 + i) / 100 - (1 - spikes) *
  0.55`: drawn in while it swims, flickering on land (they used to draw in
  only for the beam).
- **Bat.** Wings on Java's 0.5 s flying loop from the tick it took off.
- **Bee.** Flying, wings beat `cos(age * 120.32 degrees) * 0.15 pi`, and
  unless angry it bobs 0.9 pixels and pitches on `cos(age * 0.18)`; an
  angry bee at its target tumbles forward by `rollAmount` toward 177
  degrees (`bee::State::rolling`, set where `shouldRoll` is).
- **Allay.** Wings on `cos(age * 20 degrees + pos) * 0.15 pi + speed`,
  tipped back 25 degrees while idle; flying, the body leans 45 degrees;
  idle, a quarter-pixel bob and arms hanging 25 degrees out and swaying;
  holding an item (5 ticks to raise) the arms reach forward; dancing it
  sways 16 degrees and spins two turns in the first 15 of every 55 ticks.
- **Vex.** Arms hang 36 degrees out swaying `cos(age * 5.5 degrees) * 0.1`,
  body leaning 9 degrees; charging, the body straightens and the arms
  reach (70 degrees) or the armed right one thrusts up and over (210); the
  wings beat `cos(age * 45.84 degrees) * 16.2 degrees`.
- **Phantom.** Both wings beat together, `cos(flapTime * 7.448 degrees) *
  16 degrees`, `flapTime` the age plus the phantom's own offset (they used
  to beat opposite each other).
- **Blaze.** Its twelve rods exactly on `BlazeModel`'s rings (radii 9, 7,
  5; heights and angles from `ageInTicks`).
- **Silverfish and endermite.** Segments turn and shift on their models'
  sway (the silverfish used a faster sine of its own; the endermite's turn
  is now on Java's side).
- **Ghast.** Tentacles trail back by `0.2 * sin(age * 0.3 + i) + 0.4`
  (they twisted about their own axes).
- **Breeze.** `BreezeAnimation.IDLE` on its 2 s loop from the breeze's
  first tick: rods three turns round, sinking a pixel mid-loop as the head
  rises one, the top and middle wind tiers tracing half-pixel squares.
- **Shulker.** The lid rises Java's full `8 - 8 sin((0.5 + peek) pi)`
  pixels (it rose half that), bobs `sin(age * 0.1) * 0.7` past half open
  and twists `q^4 pi / 8` past 0.3 open.
- **Wither.** Already on `WitherBossModel`'s formulas; it now runs on its
  own clock.
- **Pillager.** The crossbow draw (`animateCrossbowCharge`): the pulling
  arm goes from 0.4 to 0.85 round and up to level over the charge, from
  the crossbow goal's use timer (it stayed at the start).
- **Drowned.** An aggressive drowned with a trident raises it overhead
  (`THROW_TRIDENT`, which `animateZombieArms` leaves be). Hunting in the
  water it swims (`swimAmount`, 0.09 a tick): arms reaching forward over
  the head and stroking, legs kicking, the head level, the body tipped
  `swim * (-10 - pitch)` degrees (`DrownedRenderer`). In Java only players
  and the drowned swim visually (`isVisuallySwimming`); the drowned
  overrides `HumanoidModel.setupSwimAnimation`, so that is the humanoid
  swim mobs show.
- **Horses, donkeys, mules, skeleton and zombie horses.** The tail is its
  own part (the same box): it lifts with the walk (`0.75 * speed`) and,
  for the 8 ticks of `tailCounter` (one tick in 200), swishes
  `cos(age * 0.7)`. Rearing (`standAnim`, 20 ticks): when hit (one hit in
  three), when an untamed horse throws its rider (`makeMad`) and on
  `RandomStandGoal`'s timer at rest; the body turns up 45 degrees about
  its pivot carrying the head (which lifts 15 degrees less) and the front
  legs, which paw on `cos(age * 0.6 + pi)` from 60 degrees forward, the
  hind legs planted at 15 degrees. Llamas don't rear, as in Java.

### 3. Each mob's clock and the overlay (MOB-06)

- `Mob::tick_count` is Java's `tickCount` (not saved), and every model's
  idle animation reads `Mob::age_in_ticks` (it plus the partial tick)
  instead of the shared clock, so mobs bob out of step. `model::build`
  no longer takes the shared time.
- The hurt overlay's red takes the light map's colour alone
  (`overworld_light_map` in `entity.wgsl`); the texel keeps this game's
  face shade and sun-facing tint, which used to tint the red too.

### 4. Baby models (MOB-06)

Java 26.3 draws babies of 30 kinds with models of their own (larger heads
and shorter legs, at full scale) instead of the adult halved. This track
gives nine of them their own models in
[`src/entity/model/babies.rs`](../../src/entity/model/babies.rs): the
chick, piglet, calf, lamb, wolf pup, villager child, and baby zombie, husk
and drowned. The main boxes take Java's baby proportions and pivots;
colours, patterns and every detail (eyes with glints, snouts, muzzles,
patches, wool tuft, folded arms, torn shirts) are this game's own, in
the adults' palettes. They animate as their adults' models do; a baby
zombie's armor is the adult's scaled as Java's baby armor is (body and
limbs half, head three quarters) and its held item half size.

### 5. Bamboo (UX-01)

- **Potted bamboo:** the post starts at the pot's floor and the leaf plane
  runs from 2 to 18 as Java's `potted_bamboo` does; its sprite (layer
  4066, redrawn in place) moves up two rows so the plane's whole height
  carries it, as Java's texture spans its plane.
- **Not done (needs a decision):** the stalk's width by age (2 texels, or
  3 from 6.5 to 9.5), Java's random XZ offset (up to a quarter block in
  1/30-block steps from `Mth.getSeed(x, 0, z)`) and the leaf planes'
  0.8 to 15.2 extents. Terrain quads hold whole sixteenths in 5-bit
  fields with no spare bits, and the vertex shader knows only the
  camera-relative chunk offset, not the world column the offset is
  seeded from. Exact geometry needs a terrain-format extension (special
  coordinate codes like frogspawn's 1.5, and the world column for
  offset blocks); the alternative is whole-texel rounding, which the
  rules allow only with an explicit decision. Left for the owner.

## Remaining differences

- **Baby models of the other 21 kinds** Java 26.3 has (armadillo,
  axolotl, bee, camel, cat, ocelot, dolphin, donkey, mule, horse, skeleton
  and zombie horses, fox, goat, llama and trader llama, panda, polar
  bear, rabbit, squid and glow squid, turtle, hoglin and zoglin, piglin,
  zombified piglin, strider, zombie villager, mooshroom, sniffer and the
  happy ghast's ghastling) still draw their adults halved.
- **Bamboo stalk and leaf planes** (section 5): need a decision.
- **Model shapes.** As in Track 7, motion follows each Java model on this
  game's own parts: Java's multi-joint parts are folded into fewer here
  (the guardian's three tail joints into two, the phantom's wing tips, the
  pufferfish's fins and the parrot's legs and tail-only walk swing are
  absent), the keyframe-driven rabbit, bat and breeze run their own drawn
  motion on Java's clocks and triggers (the breeze's channels are Java's,
  the rabbit's and bat's are not), and the horse's rearing turns this
  game's whole body rather than placing each of Java's parts.
- **Not converted:** quadruped walk phases are still on the mirrored
  sides (each diagonal pair half a cycle from Java's, which looks the
  same); the horse's grazing (`eatAnim`) and mouth (`mouthAnim`), which
  this game's horses don't do; the guardian's eye tracking; the
  pillager's and piglin's crossbow on the player's charge timings beyond
  the goal's timer; LAN replicas get no squid tentacle cycle, bee roll or
  rearing from the host (their state isn't replicated; they animate from
  their own ticks).
- **Clocks.** Client random rolls (guardian spikes on land, horse tails
  and random rearing, the rabbit's tilt timeout) come from a hash of the
  mob and tick, not Java's random sequence.
