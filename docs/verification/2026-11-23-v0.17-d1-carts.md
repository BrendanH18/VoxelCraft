## What was built

- **The furnace minecart**: item 1800 (`Item::FURNACE_MINECART`, "minecart
  with furnace", stack 1), `CartKind::Furnace` (cart code 42), its
  shapeless recipe, its drop, placement only on rails and through the
  existing `place_cart`/`spawn_cart` API (unchanged, so dispensers place it
  through `CartKind::from_item`). Right-clicking it runs
  `MinecartFurnace.interact` on the shared use path (desktop, controller and
  CLI agents): coal or charcoal adds 3600 ticks of fuel up to 32000 and sets
  the push from the player's feet; the click is used either way and it never
  mounts. Its motion: half the speed cap on land, three quarters in water;
  `calculateNewPushAlong` and the push slowdown, then the shared one; fuel
  burns after each move and the push clears at zero. It smokes (large smoke,
  one tick in four, 0.8 above its feet) and shows a lit mouth while fuelled.
  Fuel and push are saved and travel with the cart string to LAN guests.
  `--cart x,y,z,furnace` and `furnace_lit` place one.
- **Cart collisions** (`AbstractMinecart.push`, `pushOtherMinecart`,
  `pushAndPickupEntities`): box intersection instead of the old 0.7-block
  test, the quarter push back to players and mobs, the 0.8 facing alignment,
  the furnace's 95% keep and hand-off, pickup by a moving rideable cart of
  any pushable mob except iron golems (no size limit), and moving rideable
  carts shoving players and the mobs they don't pick up. Each cart runs its
  pushes right after its own move, in tick order, as Java does. Mob
  lookups use the existing `MobIndex` buckets, not a scan per cart.
- **Water**: `isInWater` counts waterlogged blocks, so carts on wet rails
  get the water cap, slope and slowdown; gravity in water is 0.005. The
  water slowdown, the air drag and the container slowdown use Java's float
  constants. Every rail kind already had its `waterlogged` partner (BLD-04f),
  and shape and power changes keep the water; the gap in the doc was stale
  apart from carts not seeing the water.
- **TNT minecarts**: the blast is a new `EntityEvent::CartExplosion` centred on
  the cart's feet; a primed cart's blast uses
  `World::rail_sparing_exploded_cells`, where rails and the blocks under them
  cost no resistance and are never destroyed. A cart set off at once by an
  impact, fall or burning arrow without a lit fuse is not primed, so it
  breaks rails. `World::exploded_cells_with` now passes the cell and the
  world to its resistance function and takes a `shouldBlockExplode` filter.
- **Burning projectiles**: blaze fireballs hit carts, boats and cushions for
  5 damage (`EntityShot` with the new `arrow: false`), which breaks them; a
  TNT cart broken by any burning projectile is primed with
  `nextInt(20) + nextInt(20)` instead of dropping; ghast fireballs explode
  against vehicles. Burning arrows still set a TNT cart off at once.
- **Mob passengers**: villagers, zombie villagers, wandering traders and the
  golems now save the vehicle they sit in (other mobs already did); LAN mob
  poses carry it (`"r"`).
- **Sounds**: `Sound::Minecart` (rolling, inside, inside underwater), original
  procedural half-second stretches under a Hann window, started every five
  ticks with Java's volumes (`minecart::rolling_volume`, `inside_volume`).

## Differences from Java

- Pushes between carts and the mobs or players they touch run once a tick
  per cart in VoxelCraft's entity order, not Java's section order; a
  player's push of a cart is resolved before the carts move.
- `isPushable` is modelled only for bats and mounts carrying someone;
  climbing mobs, a digging warden and team collision rules still push and
  get picked up.
- Fireball hits test the fireball's point against the cart's box each
  0.2-block step, as they already do for players, not Java's swept
  `ProjectileUtil` hit test. Small fireballs don't set carts on fire
  (carts don't burn in VoxelCraft).
- Arrows only burn with Flame; Java arrows also catch fire passing through
  fire or lava.
- The rolling and inside sounds are original stretches, not looping
  `SoundInstance`s: a stretch's volume and position are fixed for its half
  second, at most eight carts within 32 blocks roll at once, and a LAN guest
  hears the host's rolling sounds but not its own inside sound.
- Cargo (the furnace and its mouth) is a coloured box, not Java's block
  model.
- The furnace cart's push keeps Java's vertical component in memory between
  ticks but, as in Java, only the horizontal part is saved.

## Gaps left

- Textured cargo models for every cart.
- Tripwire "sag": in 26.3 the attached and detached tripwire models both
  sit at y 1.5 and differ only in which texture rows they sample (rows 4–6
  detached, 6–8 attached), so the visible difference is a texture change,
  not geometry. Left for Track E2 with the other redstone components.
- Owner play, frame-time runs, screenshots and LAN play on the reference Mac
  are not done; the sounds and the lit furnace were not heard or seen.
