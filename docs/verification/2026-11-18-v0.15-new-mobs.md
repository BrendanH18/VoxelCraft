## What is implemented

**Dried ghast** (`world::dried_ghast`): the block with its 10×10×10
column shape, placement facing the player and waterlogging (the shared
waterlog pairs), the recipe (eight ghast tears around soul sand), instant
breaking, zero explosion resistance and the self drop. A random tick
schedules a tick 5000 game ticks away when the block is waterlogged or
damp and none is pending; the tick raises hydration in water, removes the
block (leaving its water) at level 3 and hatches a baby happy ghast facing
the block's way, and lowers hydration out of water. Pending ticks save with
the redstone schedule.

**Happy ghasts** (`entity::happy_ghast`): 20 health, a 4×4 box (the
ghastling's scaled by 0.2375). Ghastlings grow up over 24000 ticks; a
snowball takes a tenth of what is left (`getSpeedUpSecondsWhenFeeding`).
They panic when hurt, follow players holding snowballs to within 3 blocks
at 1.25, follow nearby players and adult happy ghasts at 1.1, stroll, and
fly with `FlyingMoveControl` at the flying speed. Adults float with the
careful `GhastMoveControl` (pushes of 0.05 × 5/3 every 2–6 ticks, refusing
moves near `#happy_ghast_avoids`) toward `RandomFloatAroundGoal(16)`
targets, are tempted to within 7 blocks by snowballs and harnesses (only
snowballs once harnessed), and never breed. Both keep a home: 64 blocks for
a bare adult, 32 otherwise, re-homed beyond the radius plus 16. Healing is 1
point per 600 ticks, every 20 in rain or the cloud layer.

A harness goes on from the hand and comes off with shears while no one
rides; it drops on death. Up to four players board (the four passenger
attachments, 1.7 blocks out at the top); the first steers with
`getRiddenInput` (look-pitch climb, half speed backwards, jump to rise,
scaled by 3.9 × the flying speed), turns 8% of the way to the rider's yaw
a tick and travels with `travelFlying` at 0.05 × 5/3 (0.91 drag in air,
0.8 in water, 0.5 in lava). Getting off lands on top. A player standing on
it, or anyone dismounting, starts a 10-tick still timeout (decremented only
after 60 ticks alive) in which it stops in place, snaps to a quarter turn
and can't be steered; the goggles are down while anyone is aboard.

**Nautiluses and zombie nautiluses** (`entity::nautilus`): 15 health,
attack 3, knockback resistance 0.3, movement speed 1.0 (zombie 1.1).
Nautiluses spawn from the ocean `water_creature` lists between 25 and 5
blocks below sea level with water below and above. Both swim with
`SmoothSwimmingMoveControl` (0.011 of their speed in water, nothing out of
it, 0.9 drag, no sinking). A pufferfish or pufferfish bucket tames one in
three (the bucket comes back as water); tame adults take a saddle and
nautilus armor (which protects with its `BODY` defense; zombie nautiluses
are shaded from the sun by it), carry one rider (`getRiddenInput` unscaled,
0.0325 × speed in water, 0.02 on land, turning half way a tick), dash on
the jump key along the rider's look (1.2 × speed in water, 0.5 on land,
40-tick cooldown) and give the rider Breath of the Nautilus (60 ticks,
refreshed every 40 or when missing). Fish and fish buckets feed babies
(ageing them), heal tame adults by 2, and put tame nautiluses in love;
their babies are tame to the same owner. Nautiluses dry out on land (2
damage once the air passes −20). Wild adults charge the player who hurt
them (400 ticks of anger) in water within 11 blocks, at 0.6 (zombie 0.5)
for up to 12 blocks, then wait 80 ticks; every 2400–3600 ticks a coin
flip sends them after the nearest pufferfish. Drops: a nautilus shell
(0.05, or 0.06 + 0.01 per Looting level above I) on a player kill; 0–3
rotten flesh from a zombie nautilus on a player kill. Half of the natural
trident-holding drowned outside rivers ride a zombie nautilus (the coral
variant in warm oceans); the drowned's pursuit steers it.

**Camel husks** (`entity::camel_husk`, the camel's mount code): 32 health,
the camel's size, speed, step, sitting, two seats and dash; ridden without
taming, steered with a saddle; a rabbit's foot heals 2; never a baby or in
love. One natural husk in ten with room for a camel husk rides one with an
iron spear, and a parched sits behind it; the husk's pursuit drives the
camel husk and it doesn't sit while a mob rides. Drops 2–3 rotten flesh.

**Parched**: the skeleton rig with 16 health, the desert monster list
(weight 50, packs of 4) under open sky (`checkSurfaceMonstersSpawnRules`),
bows, arrows carrying Weakness for 600 ticks, 50/70-tick attack intervals
(hard/other), immunity to Weakness, no burning in daylight, the
`entities/parched` loot with its player-kill Weakness tipped arrow, and the
undead creature type.

Every new mob saves and reloads (mount gear, seats, still timeout, ages,
air, anger, variant and mob riders), has a LAN look (harness, goggles,
still flag, saddle, armor, variant, dash) and draws an original model. The
controller and CLI-agent paths use the same session interaction code
(`survival_items::use_animal_target`, `Entities::use_mount`); `summon`
completes the new names.

## Differences from Java

- Movement uses the existing per-tick Java travel (`mob::swim`) but not
  Java's brains: goals are evaluated each update from the same inputs
  rather than by `Brain` activities. The ghastling's stroll picks a random
  spot within 10 × 7 blocks; the nautilus's swim stroll samples water cells
  within 10 × 7. `isGoodTarget` and the heightmap are read from loaded blocks.
- `ChargeAttack` contact is a box overlap checked each tick; its knockback is
  the existing approximate `PlayerHit` push, not `causeExtraKnockback`.
  Pufferfish targets are not checked for line of sight.
- Players don't collide with mobs, so nobody can stand on a happy ghast
  (`canBeCollidedWith`); the still timeout for a player on top is tested
  with a player placed there and otherwise comes from dismounting.
- Riders sit with their feet on the happy ghast's top (Java subtracts the
  player's 0.6 riding offset from the 4.0 attachment); the nautilus seat is
  its 1.1375 attachment.
- Boarding a happy ghast clears the still timeout at once; if a player is on
  top, the next tick restores it (Java keeps `min(timeout, 10)`).
- The still timeout's `syncPacketPositionCodec` and the quad-leash holder
  behaviour (`IS_LEASH_HOLDER`, leash offsets) are not modelled; leads use
  the existing single-leash code.
- Sneaking doesn't stop a player boarding (Java's `isSecondaryUseActive`
  check); the nautilus inventory screen (sneak-use) is not built: saddle and
  armor go on and off by hand and shears.
- The camel husk's spear-charge `chargeSpeedModifier` (4.0) has no effect:
  mounted spear charges are not implemented. The husk's spear is held only.
- Sounds: one synth voice per mob (ambient, hurt, death). The ghastling
  shares the happy ghast's voice; the dried ghast's ambient, place,
  transition and hatch sounds, the harness goggles and equip sounds, the
  nautilus dash and dash-ready sounds and the land/underwater variants are
  not played. The dried ghast uses the default block sound type.
- Particles: the dried ghast's smoke and happy-villager particles and the
  nautilus's bubbles are not drawn.
- The nautilus's per-tick upkeep (air, cooldowns, anger) runs before its
  movement, so a dash's 40-tick cooldown starts counting a tick later than
  in `AbstractNautilus.tick`.
- `continuousHeal`'s cloud check uses a fixed 192.33–196.33 band instead of
  the cloud-height environment attribute.
- `CamelHusk.removeWhenFarAway` applies; its mob riders, like the existing
  undead horsemen, are kept loaded while they ride.
- Natural nautilus spawns go through the shared category spawner; their
  `finalizeSpawn` cooldown is drawn from the entity RNG.
