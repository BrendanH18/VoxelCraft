## Bubble columns

`src/world/bubble.rs`. Two block states hold a column cell. Every other rule
sees a still water source: `Block::is_water`, `fluid`, `fluid_level`,
`fluid_drop` (so meshing draws them as water), the flow simulation
(`World::cell` reads them as `Block::WATER`, so they spread like a source and
harden lava beside them), buckets (`pick_up` takes a column cell as a water
bucket and leaves air), sponges (`holds_water`), `waterlog::carried`
(a waterloggable block set over a column is waterlogged) and the fishing
bobber. They are replaceable and not targetable, like water.

| Rule | Behaviour | Test |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Start | A water source directly above soul sand becomes an up column, above a magma block a down column; flowing water never does | `soul_sand_and_magma_start_columns_through_every_water_source_above`, `water_that_is_not_a_source_over_the_base_makes_no_column` |
| Extent | The column runs up through every water source (or column cell) above its base and stops at the first other block | same |
| Updates | Any change to a cell queues it and the cell above; the queue is worked once per fluid tick (0.25 s, Java's five-tick delay). New water on top joins; swapping the base reverses the whole column; a block in the middle cuts the rest back to water; a missing base turns it all to water; the queue drains | `a_column_follows_its_base_and_the_water_above` |
| Buckets, flow, lava | A bucket on a column cell gives a water bucket; the rest reverts; a column spreads flowing water like a source; lava beside it becomes obsidian | `buckets_take_a_column_cell_as_water_and_the_rest_reverts`, `flow_treats_a_column_cell_as_a_source`, `lava_hardens_beside_a_column_like_any_water` |
| Replacement | A block placed over it leaves no water; a waterloggable one keeps the water | `a_block_set_over_a_column_leaves_no_water` |
| Entities | Inside a cell: up `+0.06` a tick to at most `0.7`, down `-0.03` to at least `-0.3`; where the cell above is air: up `+0.1` to `1.8`, down `-0.03` to `-0.9`. Every overlapped cell applies, as Java's per-block loop does. Applied to the player (after `ai_step`, resets fall distance, not while flying), mobs in water, dropped items (replacing the surface bob) and boats (replacing the surface spring; the existing 60-tick underwater ejection still ends rides in a down column) | `entities_are_lifted_and_dragged_at_the_familiar_speeds`, `a_player_rises_in_an_upward_column_and_sinks_in_a_downward_one`, `bubble_columns_lift_and_sink_items`, `boats_ride_up_bubble_columns_and_are_dragged_under_by_magma_ones` |
| Air | Eyes in a column spend no air and refill it (`eye_in_bubble_column`; the underwater overlay and sounds still apply) | `eyes_in_a_column_keep_their_air` |
| Natural water over magma | Generated chunks scan for water over soul sand or magma (`seeds`, only chunks that contain either) and queue those cells, so underwater magma from ravines and oceans forms columns on the first fluid tick. Nothing in generation changed | `generated_water_over_magma_and_soul_sand_seeds_columns` |
| Save and reload | The states are ordinary chunk-data ids (saved through the RLE/V3 path) | `columns_save_and_reload` |
| LAN | Columns replicate as ordinary block edits and chunk data; nothing extra is sent. Clients simulate none of it themselves (no reconciliation of player motion in columns beyond the existing prediction, which runs the same `Player::tick`) | not separately tested |
| Particles | A rising bubble per random-tick sample in an up cell and a sinking one in a down cell (`ambient.rs`) | existing particle tests |

Not done: Java's bubble-column sounds (whirlpool and upwards ambience, the
splash on entering at the surface) have no counterpart in the sound set; the
boat's wobble animation in a column; minecarts (rail-bound) and arrows do not
react; the column mesh is the water mesh (its two textures only show where a
column face meets air, as in Java where the block has no model).

Performance: `bubble_changed` is one predicate on two blocks per edit. The seed
scan runs once per generated chunk on the worker that builds it
(`GeneratedChunk::new`): Bytes chunks use one `contains`, paletted ones read the
palette, and only chunks holding soul sand or magma are walked. Measured with
`bubble_seed_scan_cost` (below).

Measured with `bubble_seed_scan_cost`: 448 chunks of seed 11 take 346 ms to
generate and 0.25 ms to scan (0.1% of generation; that sample held no magma
or soul sand, so it measures the scan that most chunks take).

## Flammability table

Compared family by family against the wiki's flammable-block table (the
encouragement to spread to a block, then its chance of burning away). A
sweep test, `every_flammable_family_matches_the_documented_flammability_table`,
walks every registered state, so a new block with no entry fails it unless it
is listed as not burning.

catch from lava.

### Fire behaviour

| Rule | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Fire tick delay | 30 + 0..9 game ticks, rescheduled before anything else; correct |
| Aging | `+0..1` per tick (`nextInt(3)/2`), capped at 15; correct |
| Rain | A rained-on cell or any of its four neighbours puts fire out with chance `0.2 + 0.03 x age` per tick, unless the fire is on an infiniburn block; correct |
| Infiniburn | Netherrack and, **new**, magma blocks (every dimension); bedrock too in the End. Soul fire is a separate block that never ages and lives only on soul sand and soil |
| Burning out | A fire with no neighbour fuel dies when its floor cannot hold it or its age passes 3; age-15 fire on a non-burnable floor dies one tick in four; correct |
| Neighbour burn-away | Chance `roll < flammability` against 300 (250 above and below, 50 lower in humid biomes); the block turns to fire with chance `5 / (age + 10)` (not in rain), TNT is primed instead; correct |
| Spread to empty cells | Within one block sideways, one below and four above, chance `(max encouragement + 40 + 7 x difficulty) / (age + 30)`, halved in humid biomes, out of `100 + 100 x (height above the first)`; correct |
| Lava | Random-tick walk up 1 or 2 cells for air beside lava-ignitable blocks, or three nearby cells at the lava's height; correct |
| Lightning | **New** `World::lightning_fire(p, extra)` (`LightningBolt.spawnFire`: Normal or Hard, fire ticking allowed, the cell and `extra` neighbours within one block). Nothing in the game strikes lightning yet, so nothing calls it |

Humid biomes (`INCREASED_FIRE_BURNOUT`) are still decided by the existing
foliage-group test; its biome list was not compared.

New fire tests: the sweep above, `doors_saplings_mushrooms_and_furniture_without_an_entry_do_not_burn_by_fire`,
`magma_blocks_burn_forever_too_and_the_end_adds_bedrock`,
`lightning_lights_its_cell_and_neighbours_only_from_normal_difficulty`.

## Off-hand, shield and totem

The review found none of these in the game: there was no off-hand slot, no
shield item and no totem. They were built.

(the hit sound is reused); the controller inventory menu has no off-hand
