## Implemented

- **Raids** (`src/entity/raid.rs`). `Raids` per dimension, saved as the
  dimension's `raids` property with Java's codec fields (`raids`,
  `next_id`, `tick`; each raid's `id`, `started`, `active`, `ticks_active`,
  `raid_omen_level`, `groups_spawned`, `cooldown_ticks`,
  `post_raid_ticks`, `total_health`, `group_count`, `status`, `center`,
  `heroes_of_the_village`). Membership lives on the raiders (`raid_id` and
  `wave` in Track A's `illager.raider` record), so a reload restores the
  raid and its members as Java's `readAdditionalSaveData` does. `Raid.tick`
  is followed line by line: Peaceful and the `raids` rule stop raids; the
  centre moves to a village section within two sections or the raid is
  lost (stopped before the first wave); the 48000-tick timeout; the
  300-tick countdown with spawn searches every 5 ticks (8 tries) and a
  20-try search when due, five failures stopping the raid; waves per
  difficulty (3, 5, 7) with `RaiderType`'s table, `getPotentialBonusSpawns`
  and a bonus wave above level 1; the first raider able to lead is the
  wave's captain with the ominous banner (drop chance 2.0); ravager
  riders (a pillager on Normal's last wave, an evoker then vindicators from
  Hard's); `applyRaidBuffs` (iron axe, Sharpness I/II and Quick Charge
  I/II by `getEnchantOdds`); `updateRaiders` (beyond 112 blocks, or idle
  outside the village for 30 checks after 600 ticks); 40 ticks after the
  last raider, victory and Hero of the Village for 48000 ticks at level −
  1 to every living hero (players who killed a member); 600 ticks of
  celebration or defeat, then the raid stops. `getRaidAt` is the nearest
  active raid within 96 blocks.
- **Raid bar.** Java's `ServerBossEvent` (red, ten notches): the countdown
  fills it, then the living raiders' health over the wave's total; "Raid",
  "Raid - Raiders Remaining: n" at two or fewer, "Raid - Victory",
  "Raid - Defeat"; shown to living players whose nearest raid it is,
  updated on Java's 20-tick cadence. The HUD draws it under the dragon's
  bar. CLI agents see it in their state (`raid`).
- **Omens** (`src/session/raid.rs`, host and agents). Bad Omen in a
  village (an occupied village POI within one section) whose raid is below
  level 5 becomes Raid Omen for 600 ticks at the same amplifier, and the
  position is kept (saved as `raid_omen_position`); with one tick left the
  Raid Omen starts or extends the raid there (`createOrExtendRaid`: the
  average of occupied village POIs within 64 blocks is the centre; levels
  add `amplifier + 1`, clamped to 5). Not for spectators, on Peaceful, in
  the Nether (`can_start_raid`) or with `raids` off.
- **Raiders.** Raid membership refreshes `raid_active` and `celebrating`
  (on a loss) every tick; raiders with `can_join_raid` join a raid at
  their position every 20 ticks or one a member within 16 blocks belongs
  to (`PathfindToRaidGoal.recruitNearby`), unless idle over 2400 ticks.
  Goals (`src/entity/mob/raider.rs`), when there is nothing to attack:
  fetch a dropped ominous banner when the wave has no living leader
  (picked up within 1.414 blocks, becoming captain); walk toward the raid
  centre in steps up to 15 blocks while outside the village; walk to
  random homes within 48 blocks not among the last three visited.
  `HoldGroundAttackGoal` for patrolling pillagers and vindicators outside
  raids. In a raid they don't despawn.
- **Captains and patrols.** `PatrollingMonster.finalizeSpawn`'s 6% captain
  roll for raiders that can lead (not ravagers or witches) on natural,
  egg and command spawns; never for raid, patrol or structure spawns.
  `PatrolSpawner`: every 12000 + `nextInt(1200)` ticks with
  `spawn_monsters`, `spawn_mobs` and `spawn_patrols`, in the Overworld
  after clock tick 120000 by day, one in five, around a random
  non-spectator player not within two sections of a village, 24–47 blocks
  away per axis, not in mushroom fields; `ceil(effective difficulty) + 1`
  pillagers on the `ON_GROUND` floor in block light 8 or less, the first a
  captain with a target −500..500 away. `LongDistancePatrolGoal`: steps of
  10 blocks curving around the target by Java's `yRot(90)` (radians, as
  the jar passes them), the leader's step handed to companions within 16,
  a lone member stops patrolling, 200 ticks of wandering after a failed
  path. Patrollers despawn only beyond 128 blocks. The captain's banner
  always drops; a pillager captain drops its ominous bottle.
- **Witches in raids.** Raid witches carry the raider state (natural ones
  can't join, as in Java), are saved with it, walk the raid when not
  chasing a player and keep the witch's own attack and drinking.
- **Village reaction.** During a wave (first wave out, not between waves,
  raid undecided) villagers within its range stop sleeping and trading and
  shelter at home (the existing bell-hide path); after a victory they
  cheer one tick in 100. A ringing bell with raiders within 32 blocks five
  ticks in resonates (`block.bell.resonate`), and 40 ticks later the
  raiders it found within 48 blocks glow for 60 ticks.
- **Rewards.** Hero of the Village (no particles, with icon). The price
  hook is `entity::raid::hero_price_cut(base_count, amplifier)`, called
  from `Villager::priced_for` with the trading player's amplifier from the
  unsaved `Villager::heroes` list the raid tick copies in each tick:
  `max(floor((0.3 + 0.0625 × amplifier) × base count), 1)` off each offer,
  as `updateSpecialPrices` does, on top of the gossip discount; wandering
  traders give none. `GiveGiftToHero`: a villager whose nearest visible
  player within 16 is a hero waits 600 ticks, walks to within 5 blocks,
  throws a roll of its profession's gift table 20 ticks later (babies the
  baby table, others `unemployed_gift`), then waits 600 + `nextInt(6001)`.
- **Rules.** `raids` and `spawn_patrols` are enforced; `disableRaids`
  (Java's legacy name) is read and written inverted, as
  `GameRuleRegistryFix` does ([game rules](../game-rules.md)).
- **Sounds.** Original procedural raid horn (13 blocks from each player
  within 64 toward the wave, or in the raid), bell resonance and the
  villagers' cheer, through the synth path (`Sound::Raid`).

## Differences from Java

- Village POI occupancy is derived from villagers' `home` and `job`
  claims (stored villagers included); bells count when a claimed POI is
  within 48 blocks, since VoxelCraft doesn't track meeting-point claims.
  `isVillage` reads a section cache rebuilt every 20 ticks.
- Raid membership counts raiders in loaded chunks; a raider stored with its
  chunk is not counted until it returns (Java keeps it in the wave set
  until `updateRaiders` notices after 600 ticks). Leaders are derived from
  the living members' `patrol_leader` rather than a separate map.
- The raid uses the entities' random stream (Java's `Raid` has its own
  unseeded `RandomSource`); the natural captain roll uses a roll seeded by
  the spawn so existing spawn sequences are unchanged.
- Heroes are credited from the last player to hit the raider within five
  seconds before its death (Java reads the killing damage source).
- `noActionTime` is VoxelCraft's idle timer × 40 (Raider adds 2 a tick).
- `DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards` is approximated by a point up to 15
  blocks toward the centre within a quarter turn; heights use the column
  surface rather than `MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES`. The village walk picks
  a deterministic home among the unvisited ones within 48 blocks.
- `HoldGroundAttackGoal` doesn't call nearby raiders (its 8-block shout);
  `AbstractIllager.RaiderOpenDoorGoal` is not added.
- Raid witches don't throw healing or regeneration at raiders
  (`NearestHealableRaiderTargetGoal`) and keep attacking players meanwhile.
- Villagers before the first wave and between waves (`PRE_RAID`) don't
  ring the bell or gather at the meeting point; during waves they shelter
  through the bell-hide path (home, not a random home within 24 blocks);
  celebrating villagers don't launch fireworks or seek open sky.
- The Hero of the Village discount applies live while the effect lasts
  rather than when the trading screen opens.
- `Stats.RAID_TRIGGER`, `RAID_WIN` and the advancements are absent: there
  are no statistics or advancements.
- The captain's ominous banner is not drawn on the mob's head.
- `can_pillager_patrol_spawn` reads VoxelCraft's day clock
  (`clock_time`), which `/time set` changes.
- Sounds are original stand-ins.
