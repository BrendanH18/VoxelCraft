## What was built

- **One storage transaction layer.** `src/session/storage.rs`
  (`session::storage`) now holds the click, shift-click, number-key swap,
  throw, cursor toss and drop logic of every storage screen: chest-backed
  blocks (chests and both halves of a double chest, trapped and copper chests,
  barrels, shulker boxes, hoppers, dispensers, droppers), furnaces, smokers,
  blast furnaces, brewing stands, vehicles with storage (chest and hopper
  carts, chest boats, pack animals) and the ender inventory. The app's
  `screen::Screen` (desktop host, controller seats, LAN peers) now delegates
  those slots to it, so the fuzz drives the code players use. Workstation
  screens stay in the app. `Entities::pick_up_items` is the player pickup the
  desktop used inline.
- **Number keys and the drop key over storage slots** on the desktop host:
  Java's `SWAP` and `THROW` (`AbstractContainerMenu.doClick`). Number keys
  over a slot used to change the selected hotbar slot; they still do when no
  slot is under the mouse. LAN clients and controllers don't send these.
- **The fuzz**, `tests/storage_transactions.rs`: nineteen stations on a
  bedrock platform (chest, double chest, trapped chest, barrel, copper chest,
  shulker box, furnace, smoker, blast furnace, brewing stand, hopper,
  collecting hopper, dispenser, dropper, chest and hopper carts on detector
  rails, chest and hopper carts under feeding hoppers, ender inventory), each
  with a chest-fed hopper above, a hopper below draining into a sink chest
  (furnaces and brewing stands also a side hopper) and a comparator in front.
  A seeded generator picks operations: open a station, click (left or right),
  shift-click, number-key swap, throw from a slot, toss the cursor, drop from
  the inventory, toss onto a collecting hopper or hopper cart, pick up a
  dropped item, the CLI and headless LAN `chest`/`furnace take|put` commands,
  pulse a dispenser, dropper or hopper with a redstone block, survival mining
  of a station (with `block_drops` on or off), punching a cart to pieces, an
  explosion, rebuilding what's gone, waiting, a platform chunk unload, and a
  save to disk and reload into a new world. Every tick runs in `Game::tick`'s
  order (carts, contacts and lids, world rules with hoppers and redstone,
  automation entities, entity simulation, block drops, pickups).
- **Checks after every step:** each item identity (the item and every
  component: damage, enchantments, repair cost, name, dye, potion, lingering
  contents; bundle and shulker box contents counted item by item) is
  conserved over the player, ender inventory, every container, furnace,
  brewing stand, cart, dropped item and placed shulker box, read back from the
  save strings; brewing fuel is the only consumption and is accounted for; no
  stack exceeds its limit, sits past a hopper's 5 or a dispenser's 9 slots or
  a cart's slot count, or breaks a slot rule (shulker box in a shulker box or
  bundle, non-fuel in a fuel slot, more than one potion per bottle slot,
  non-ingredients); and every tick, once a station's Java reading has held
  for four ticks, its comparator must show it, also after reload. The item
  pool is chosen so nothing smelts or brews (`pool_never_transforms` checks
  it), which keeps the ledger exact.
- **Extractor and fixture:** `tools/extract_java_storage_fixtures.py` writes
  `tests/fixtures/java/26.3/storage_transactions.json` from the pinned jar
  with `javap` (the quick-move ranges and directions of each storage menu,
  slot limits, hopper cooldown, the comparator's float arithmetic, contents
  dropping without game rules, brewing fuel `setChanged`, furnace `setItem`'s
  component check, shulker box and chest explosion conditions);
  `storage_menus_match_the_java_fixture` checks the game against it.

## Defects found and fixed

Each has a minimal reproduction in `tests/storage_transactions.rs`.

1. **Loss: container contents vanished with `block_drops` off.** Breaking a
   chest (any chest-backed block), furnace or brewing stand with the rule off
   deleted what it held. Java drops contents in
   `BlockEntity.preRemoveSideEffects` through `Containers.dropContents`, which
   no game rule gates (only `Block.popResource`, the loot, reads
   `BLOCK_DROPS`); the furnace's stored experience is released either way.
   Reproduction: `contents_spill_with_block_drops_off` (a chest with 7
   diamonds, a furnace with 5 coal, a stand with 3 nether wart, removed with
   tile drops off). Fix: `world::chest`, `world::furnace`, `world::brewing`.
2. **Missed comparator update: brewing fuel.** A brewing stand loading blaze
   powder into its fuel bar didn't wake comparators; Java's
   `BrewingStandBlockEntity.serverTick` calls `setChanged`. Reproduction:
   `loading_brewing_fuel_wakes_comparators` (a stand with one fuel use left
   and one blaze powder; a water bottle one tick and four nether wart the
   next start a brew, the tick after loads the powder, and the comparator
   that read the stand in between showed 4 instead of 3 until the brew
   ended). Fix: `World::tick_brewing` wakes comparators whenever a stand's
   slots change.
3. **Component loss: bottle slots dropped a potion's components.** Clicking
   or shift-clicking a potion into a brewing stand bottle slot built a new
   stack of the bare item: a named potion lost its name and a lingering
   potion its contents. Reproduction: `bottle_slots_keep_potion_components`.
   Fix: the stack keeps its components (`session::storage`).
4. **Hopper-fed furnaces never finished.** Every hopper insertion into a
   furnace's input restarted the cook, so a hopper topping up the input every
   eight ticks kept it from finishing anything. Java's
   `AbstractFurnaceBlockEntity.setItem` restarts it only for a different item
   (`isSameItemSameComponents`), and `HopperBlockEntity.tryMoveInItem` grows
   the stack in place. Reproduction: `hopper_fed_furnaces_keep_cooking` (raw
   iron and coal in a furnace fed by a hopper; no ingot after 230 ticks
   before). Fix: `World::set_container_stack`. Not a loss or duplication, but
   found driving hoppers into furnaces.

Java behaviours the old screens differed from, fixed while moving them
(`storage_screens_follow_java_menus`): right-click on a furnace output takes
half (`FurnaceResultSlot`); shift-clicking a furnace's input or fuel moves it
to the main grid first (`AbstractFurnaceMenu` moves forwards); blaze powder
shift-clicked into a brewing stand stops at the fuel slot and only tries the
ingredient slot when the fuel slot took none; an ingredient or potion a stand
can't take stays put instead of moving between hotbar and grid.

The fuzz found no duplication, no other loss, no stack over its limit or in a
refused slot, and no other comparator mismatch in the runs below.

## Differences from Java and gaps

- **Not modelled:** dragging a held stack across slots (`QUICK_CRAFT`) and
  double-click collecting (`PICKUP_ALL`); the fuzz can't drive what the game
  doesn't have. Number keys and the drop key over storage slots work on the
  desktop host only, not for LAN clients (whose packets carry clicks and
  shift-clicks only) or controllers.
- **No hopper under a rail.** VoxelCraft rails need a full block below
  (`World::rail_unsupported`), so the classic hopper-under-rail unloader isn't
  buildable and the fuzz doesn't drain carts through a hopper. Java lets
  rails sit on hoppers. That's Track D1's rail and cart scope.
- **The LAN click path** itself (the desktop host's `remote_click` decoding a
  peer's packet into `Screen`) needs the desktop game and isn't run headless;
  it reaches the same `session::storage` code the fuzz drives. The headless
  LAN host's `chest` and `furnace` commands are fuzzed.
- **Hopper sided transfers** (furnace extraction order, which slots each face
  reaches) are fuzzed as they are today; Track C2 is changing them.
- **The CLI `furnace put input`** still restarts the cook every time (it
  isn't a Java path).
- **Vehicles** other than chest and hopper carts (chest boats, pack animals)
  share the layer but have no fuzz station yet.

## Seam for the other tracks

A new storage kind joins the fuzz as one entry in `KINDS`
(`tests/storage_transactions.rs`): a name, a `Form` (a block constructor, a
double chest, a cart on a detector or plain rail, or the ender inventory),
whether hoppers feed and drain it, whether a comparator checks it, whether
it keeps its contents when broken, and the miner's tool. If its screen isn't
chest-backed, furnace or brewing, add a `Storage` variant and its slots to
`session::storage` and the app's `screen::storage_of`. The crafter (C1)
would be one `KINDS` entry once it has storage; C2's sided transfers and
D1's cart changes are exercised as they land.
