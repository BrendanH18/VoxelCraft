# v0.13 track 2: density terrain (2026-10-30)

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Roadmap v0.13](../roadmap.md#v013-scope-terrain) ·
[Overworld terrain](../overworld-terrain.md#shape)

Generation 2 worlds shape the Overworld from a 3D density field instead of
generation 1's climate heightmap (GEN-01a/b). Generation 1 output is
unchanged. Status: implemented, unverified. The owner has not played it
yet, and the integrated v0.13 checks (save/reload of both generations, the
streaming benchmark over the merged tree) are still owed.

- Requirements: GEN-01a (terrain shape), GEN-01b (surface rules), with the
  cave and aquifer fitting from GEN-02 that the shape needs.
- Reference: behaviour only, under fidelity exception FE-01. The noise and
  density function are original; Java's density functions were not read
  or ported. Java's behaviour as players see it guided the work:
  overhanging cliffs, surface blocks on floors under overhangs, sandstone
  ceilings that don't fall, and caves that open onto the surface.
- Base: `4216966` plus the staged v0.13 generation-version change.

## What changed

- `world::climate`: `ClimateNoise::relief` (how far the surface may move,
  from continentalness, erosion, peaks-and-valleys, rivers and swamps) and
  `ClimateNoise::relief_noise` (two octaves of 3D Perlin noise, clamped to
  `[-1, 1]`). `height` itself is unchanged.
- `world::terrain::density` (new): the per-chunk-column density field and
  generation 2's fill, carving and dungeon point query.
  - Density: a block is solid where
    `y <= T(base + relief × n' − 2 (y − base)₊² / relief) + hoodoos`, where
    `n'` is the noise, cut to 0.65 above zero, and the squash term applies
    only above the base. `T` is generation 1's badlands
    terrace, and the hoodoos are generation 1's eroded-badlands spires.
    Relief is about 1 in oceans, 2–4 on plains and coasts, up to about 45 in
    jagged peaks, about 20 on windswept ground and at least 10 in badlands
    above the sea. It is worked out on the 4-block grid and interpolated, so
    it changes smoothly across biome borders.
  - Grid: noise nodes every 4 blocks across and 8 up, at world
    coordinates, interpolated trilinearly. Nodes are only sampled within
    the band the relief can reach (per-column bounds with a 2-block
    margin). Below the band everything is solid and above it everything is
    air, without sampling.
  - `Generator::column` for generation 2 reads the topmost solid block
    from the cached chunk column (the same 512-entry cache as before).
    `nearest_biome`, `find_spawn` and the mob `biome()` query now check
    climate before asking for a height, so they don't build density
    fields for columns they only test for biome. For generation 1 they
    return the same results.
  - Surface rules: every solid block learns the distance to the open air
    above it, and whether the block below is open, from a 64-bit solid mask
    per column. Faces get the biome's surface block and filler. Steepness
    is measured from the density gradient. Undersides become stone, or
    sandstone and red sandstone under sand. Badlands strata run through
    everything above y = 57.
  - Caves (`carve_v2`): depth is measured from the nearest open air above.
    Nothing is carved within 6 blocks above open air, or within 2 blocks
    under the sea. Where the relief is above 8, nothing is carved above the
    base height either, so tunnels can't cut through spires and leave
    their caps floating. Aquifers flood against `base − relief`, a 2D estimate
    every chunk agrees on. Aquifer fluid is not placed beside open dry
    air, or above a block where the aquifer itself is dry. In generation 1
    that case leaves water over cave air: 49 cells in 16 chunk columns
    around the origin, seed 12345.
- `world::terrain`: small dispatch hooks (`column`, `chunk_columns`,
  `generate`'s fill and carve, `natural_block`, the tree and plant soil
  check), plus `Generator::surface_biome`.
- `bench`: `--bench` also prints generation 1 and 2 throughput around the
  origin and in the nearest jagged peaks, on fresh generators.

## Checks

All on an Apple M5, release build, in this track's worktree. Other
v0.13 tracks were compiling on the same machine, so absolute times are
noisy. Ratios between runs taken back to back are more reliable.

- `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D warnings` and
  `cargo test --release` pass: 1,800 tests, 0 failed, 16 ignored. The
  device-independent configuration (`--no-default-features`) was not run.
- Generation 1 is unchanged: `generated_chunk_hashes_stay_identical`
  passes unmodified, and every generation 1 path is the old code behind a
  `density_terrain()` check.
- New tests in `world::terrain::density`:
  - `masks_match_point_samples`: the per-column bit masks and point
    queries agree, including the ring columns.
  - `neighbouring_chunk_columns_agree`: a chunk column's ring heights
    equal its neighbour's own heights.
  - `chunks_do_not_depend_on_generation_order`: 63 chunks around the peaks
    (seed 777), generated forward on one thread and backward on seven
    threads, hash identically.
  - `mountains_overhang`: more than 200 columns with solid rock over open
    air above the sea, in 3×3 chunk columns at the peaks (seed 12345).
  - `column_heights_are_the_generated_tops`: for 24 columns,
    `column().height` is the topmost terrain block of the generated
    chunks, and no ground lies above it.
  - `spawn_is_on_dry_land`: seeds 7, 12345 and 99 spawn on land above the
    sea, at `height + 1`.
  - `nothing_hangs_or_floods_into_open_air`: no water over air after
    caves, and no sand, gravel or grass over open air from the fill.
  - `little_rock_floats`: a flood fill over 5×5 chunk columns at the peaks
    counts solid blocks above the sea that don't connect to the ground.

  | Seed | Before caves | After caves |
  |---|---|---|
  | 12345 | 0 of 1,486,260 | 15 of 1,340,337 |
  | 42 | 105 of 2,323,929 | 200 of 2,154,454 |

- Overhangs (`print_mountains`, ignored): within 5×5 chunk columns around
  the nearest jagged peaks, 594 of 25,600 columns have rock over open air
  within 80 blocks of their top for seed 12345, and 2,111 for seed 42.
  The region also contains lower, flatter ground.

### Generation speed

`--bench --rd 8`, seed 12345, one thread, 7×7 chunk columns (588 chunks)
on fresh generators:

| Place | Generation 1 | Generation 2 | Ratio |
|---|---|---|---|
| Origin | 0.460 ms/chunk (2,173 chunks/s) | 0.534 ms/chunk (1,873 chunks/s) | 1.16× |
| Jagged peaks | 0.580 ms/chunk (1,724 chunks/s) | 0.706 ms/chunk (1,416 chunks/s) | 1.22× |

Both are inside the 1.5× budget. Earlier tuning runs of the same
comparison (`print_generation_speed`) gave 1.13–1.24×. The same `--bench`
run streamed the generation 2 world at `rd=8` on 9 workers: 3,516 chunks
loaded and 2,904 meshed in 0.84 s. No generation 1 streaming run was made
for comparison. The costs are the per-chunk-column density field (121
extra climate samples, plus noise nodes in the relief band only) and a
64-bit solid mask per column of each chunk.

## Unverified and gaps

- The owner's play. No save/reload run of a generation 2 world was made
  in this track. Saves don't store unmodified chunks, and the generation
  key is the shared item 1 change, but the integrated tree still needs the
  save/reload check (roadmap item 6).
- Floors under overhangs get soil and grass, but no plants, flowers or
  trees. Snow layers and ice only form on the column's top. Trees and
  structures stand on the topmost surface.
- Enclosed air pockets in the density field, which are rare, get surface
  rules like any other floor. Below the sea they fill with water.
- Some isolated rock can still float above high peaks, where the noise
  peaks before the squash cuts it off. It is smaller than before the
  squash was added (see the screenshots).
- The streaming benchmark (`--bench --rd 8`) was not rerun over the merged
  v0.13 tree. The numbers below are this track alone, on a shared machine.
