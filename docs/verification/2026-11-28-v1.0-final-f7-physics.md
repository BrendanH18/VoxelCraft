## Scope

The three [known limitations](../../packaging/RELEASE-NOTES.md) this track
closes:

1. "Mobs and dropped items treat scaffolding and powder snow as plain
   blocks": Java 26.3's `Entity.collide` hands every entity's block
   shapes `CollisionContext.of(entity)`, an `EntityCollisionContext`;
   VoxelCraft gave that only to players and used the empty context for
   the rest.
2. "Pistons don't push arrows, falling blocks, End crystals, item frames,
   paintings or armour stands."
3. "Walls built in older versions show their posts until a neighbouring
   block changes": a load-time fix for the posts.

## Java sources

The 26.3 server jar (`server-26.3.jar` inside the bundler, from Mojang's
version manifest) on a Boat sandbox (`voxelcraft-f7-java`, deleted
afterwards), classes decompiled one by one with Vineflower 1.11.1, the
tags read from the jar's data. Nothing decompiled is committed. Read:

- `CollisionContext.of(entity)`: an `EntityCollisionContext(entity, false,
  false)` for every entity (minecarts get `MinecartCollisionContext` only
  under the experimental minecart movement); `empty()` for queries with no
  entity.
- `EntityCollisionContext`: `descending` is `entity.isDescending()`, which
  is `isShiftKeyDown()` (only players set it); `entityBottom` is `getY()`;
  `isAbove(shape, pos, default)` is `entityBottom > pos.y + shape.maxY -
  1.0E-5F`.
- `ScaffoldingBlock.getCollisionShape`: `SHAPE_STABLE` when above the
  block and not descending; else `SHAPE_UNSTABLE_BOTTOM` (the 2-pixel rim)
  for `DISTANCE != 0 && BOTTOM` when above that rim; else empty.
- `PowderSnowBlock.getCollisionShape`: for an entity context,
  `FALLING_COLLISION_SHAPE` (0.9 high) when `fallDistance > 2.5`; the full
  block for a `FallingBlockEntity`, or for `canEntityWalkOnPowderSnow`
  above the block and not descending; else empty.
  `canEntityWalkOnPowderSnow`: `#powder_snow_walkable_mobs` (rabbit,
  endermite, silverfish, fox) or leather boots in the feet slot.
  (`#freeze_immune_entity_types`, stray, polar bear, snow golem and
  wither, decides freezing, not collision.)
- `ItemEntity.tick`: `noPhysics = !level.noCollision(this, box.deflate(1.0E-7))`
  (the item's own context) before `moveTowardsClosestSpace`;
  `ExperienceOrb.tick`'s own checks use the entity-less `noCollision(box)`.
- `Entity.getPistonPushReaction`: `PUSH_PULL`; overridden only by
  `ArmorStand` (`IGNORE_ENTITY` for markers), `Marker`, `Display`,
  `Interaction`, `AreaEffectCloud` and `OminousItemSpawner`. Arrows,
  falling blocks, End crystals, item frames and paintings don't override
  it.
- `PistonMovingBlockEntity.moveCollidedEntities`, `moveEntityByPiston`,
  `matchesStickyCritera` (`PUSH_PULL && onGround && ...`), and
  `Entity.move(MoverType.PISTON)` (`limitPistonMovement`, then the normal
  collision; `noPhysics` entities just move).
- `BlockAttachedEntity.move` and `push`: on a server, any non-zero
  movement `kill`s the entity and `dropItem(level, null)`s it; its `tick`
  checks `survives()` every 100 ticks.
- `EndCrystal.tick` (no `move` of its own), `FallingBlockEntity.tick`
  (`move(SELF, deltaMovement)` and `blockPosition()` when it lands),
  `AbstractArrow` (`shouldFall` once its block changes).
- `WallBlock.getStateForPlacement`, `updateShape`, `sideUpdate`,
  `shouldRaisePost`, `updateSides` and `makeWallState`, already ported by
  the [stored wall posts](2026-10-21-wall-posts-powered-doors.md) work.

## What changed

### 1. Collision contexts for mobs, items and the other entities

`physics::CollisionContext` gained `falling_block` and an
`entity(feet_y, fall_distance, walks_on_powder_snow)` constructor (not
descending), and `physics` gained `_context` variants of `move_box`,
`move_box_stepping`, `collide` and `overlaps_solid`, the old functions
keeping the empty context for queries no entity makes.
`cell_boxes_context` already held Java's scaffolding and powder-snow
rules; powder snow now also holds up a falling block.

- Mobs: `Mob::collision_context` (feet, the saved `fall_distance`,
  `walks_on_powder_snow`: the tag's four kinds or leather boots in the
  feet slot) for every mob movement call: the shared walking, stepping and
  falling path, drowned swimming, swimmers, lava striders' path, polar
  bears, frogs, sulfur cubes, allays, bees, bats and phantoms.
- Dropped items: `ItemEntity::collision_context`, with a fall distance the
  item now tracks (`Entity.checkFallDamage`: falling adds up, landing
  resets; not saved), used for the move and for the "inside a block"
  check, so an item inside scaffolding sinks instead of being popped out
  of the top.
- Experience orbs and primed TNT move with an entity context (fall
  distance zero); the orb's own "inside a block" check keeps the empty
  context, as Java's entity-less `noCollision` does.
- Armor stands fall with theirs (leather boots in their feet slot count).
- Falling blocks move by whole cells (`world::falling`): they already
  landed on top of powder snow and scaffolding, as Java's full and stable
  shapes give them.

### 2. Piston pushes

`Entities::apply_piston_pushes` now also runs `push_body` on arrows (their
0.5 box, blocks a second), tridents (the same box, blocks a tick; a
returning loyalty trident is `noPhysics`), End crystals (their 2 × 2 box;
a slime block's launch changes nothing, as `EndCrystal` never moves
itself) and armor stands (their fall speed takes a slime launch). An item
frame or painting a recorded push would move by more than nothing
(`world::piston::pushes_box`, the same sweep and `getMovement` as
`push_body`) is killed instead: `entity_die`, a frame's `block_change`,
then the frame or painting item and a frame's held item drop, under
`doEntityDrops` (`Entities::piston_break_decor`). Honey's sideways carry
needs `onGround`, which hanging entities never are.

Falling blocks live in the world, so `World::tick_moving_pistons` pushes
them itself right after the moving blocks advance
(`World::push_falling_blocks`), with Java's 0.98 box around the block's
centre. A sideways push moves a falling block off the grid; it now falls
and lands in the column under its centre (`blockPosition`), and a slime
block's upward launch becomes a negative fall speed that stops under
whatever it would rise into.

### 3. Old walls' posts

Every wall id from before stored posts means UP=true, so a world saved by
v0.8 or older loaded every wall with its post up. Now, when a chunk is
installed from `chunks.bin` (an old world's saved chunks, in a world with
region storage, so never in a LAN replica or a 1.0 world's records),
`World::migrate_legacy_wall_posts` gives each wall the post
`WallBlock.updateShape` gives it, computing a stack from its top down
(an upper wall in another chunk is recomputed too, not trusted). The
result is the chunk's data, written to its region record at the next
save; no id, state or format changes. Walls whose stored post is already
right (anything v0.9 to v0.18 wrote) come out the same, and the chunk's
data is returned untouched; walls a v0.9 to v0.18 build loaded from a
v0.8 world and saved again unchanged are fixed too, as every `chunks.bin`
wall is recomputed rather than only those of worlds that name no version. A wall whose neighbour or upper cell is in a
chunk nothing holds yet (a never-edited chunk not generated) keeps its
post and waits in `World::legacy_walls` (not saved);
`World::retry_legacy_wall_posts` settles it as an ordinary shape update
once the chunks around it load.

## Remaining differences

- A legacy wall still waiting for a neighbouring chunk when its own chunk
  is saved and unloaded keeps its raised post until a neighbour changes
  (the wait list isn't saved).
- Experience orbs, primed TNT and armor stands count no fall distance, so
  after a fall of more than 2.5 blocks onto powder snow they sink in at
  once where Java first rests them a tick on the 0.9 shape. Minecarts and
  boats keep the empty context.
- Thrown potions, pearls, snowballs and eggs, eyes of ender, fireballs,
  fireworks, wither skulls, shulker bullets, fishing bobbers and evoker
  fangs aren't pushed by pistons (Java pushes every one).
- A pushed falling block falls down the column under its centre instead
  of colliding with every cell its 0.98 box overlaps, and keeps no
  sideways speed from a slime block.
- Item frames and paintings a piston breaks make no break sound.
