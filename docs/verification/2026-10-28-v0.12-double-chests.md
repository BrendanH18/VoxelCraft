# v0.12 double chests and explosions destroying drops (2026-10-28)

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Roadmap v0.12](../roadmap.md#v012-scope-building-materials)

Two gaps v0.12 still had: chests, trapped chests and copper chests never
paired into double chests (the copper and utilities records list it as not
done), and explosions didn't destroy dropped items or experience orbs (the
[explosions record](2026-10-27-v0.12-explosions.md) lists it). Implemented,
unverified: no owner play yet.

- Requirements: BLD-05a (double chests), BLD-01c (copper chests), ENV-02
  (explosions).
- Reference: Java 26.3. Tested commit: `e0b9175` (PR #52).
- Automated checks on that commit: `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --release
  --all-targets -- -D warnings` and `cargo test --release` pass (1,779
  tests, 13 of them new).

## Double chests

`src/world/double_chest.rs` decodes every chest state (plain, trapped and
the four copper stages) into kind, facing, `type` and waterlogging, and
holds Java's pairing rules.

- **States.** The plain chest's left and right halves take 16 new ids,
  28952–28967 (dry and wet; [architecture](../architecture.md#v012-track-reservations)).
  The trapped chest's and copper chests' halves were reserved by their
  tracks and are now registered. Every half answers like its single chest
  for name, item, mining, drops, fuel and sounds, and turns keeping its type.
- **Placement** (`ChestBlock.getStateForPlacement`). A chest faces the
  placer and pairs with a single neighbour facing the same way, clockwise
  of its facing first (`left`) then counter-clockwise (`right`). Sneaking
  stops that, except that sneaking against the side of a chest joins it,
  taking its facing. Chests pair with their own block; copper chests pair
  across stages, and the pair takes the less oxidised stage (`getLeastOxidizedChestOfConnectedBlocks`).
- **Neighbour updates** (`updateShape`). A single chest beside a half that
  points at it becomes the other half; a half whose partner is gone is
  single again; a copper half takes its partner's stage, so scraping or
  aging one half changes both.
- **One container** (`DoubleBlockCombiner`, `CompoundContainer`). Each half
  keeps its own 27 slots in the chest table and the level file, so nothing
  saved changed. Together they are 54 slots, the right half's first:
  keyboard and controller screens show six rows titled "Large Chest",
  shift-clicks and hoppers fill the right half first, comparators by either
  half read all 54, and `chest take`/`put` reach slots 0–53. LAN snapshots
  carry the partner half's slots (`partner`, `partner_state`; a client
  without them sees one half). Breaking a half drops only its own slots.
- **Blocked chests** (`isChestBlockedAt`). A redstone conductor on top of
  either half keeps the chest shut for every input path and makes
  comparators read 0. Single chests had no such rule before. Sitting cats,
  the other blocker, don't sit on chests yet. No chest is a conductor
  (their shapes are inset): trapped and ender chests counted as one before,
  so a trapped chest conducted a lever's power and would have blocked a
  chest under it.
- **Viewers.** Each tick the game gathers every chest a keyboard,
  controller or LAN player has open and counts a viewer on both halves
  (`ContainerOpenersCounter.recheckOpeners`): both halves of a trapped chest
  signal, and a copper chest doesn't age while open. Controller and LAN
  players' open chests didn't count before, so their trapped chests now
  signal too.
- **Copper aging** (`WeatheringCopperChestBlock.randomTick`). Only the half
  that isn't `right` takes the random tick; the other follows by
  `updateShape`, so a pair ages together.
- **Shape.** A half reaches the side it shares with its partner (Java's
  `HALF_SHAPES`). Trapped chests now have the chest's inset shape too.
- **Textures.** Chests are drawn as cubes. A half's front, back and top are
  new layers 2686–2727 (`double_chest::tex`) that copy the single chest's
  texture with the frame at the joint replaced by interior and the joint
  column showing half the latch, so a pair looks like one wide chest. Faces
  are textured by world position, so each half needs a variant per joint
  side. The layers are drawn from the existing procedural textures; no new
  artwork.

### Tests

`src/world/double_chest/tests.rs` (12 tests): every state round-trips and is
registered, waterlogs and answers like its single chest; halves point at
each other and reach the shared side; placement pairs facing chests, skips
taken and turned ones and different kinds; sneaking stops pairing except
against a chest's side; breaking a half leaves the other single with its
items and drops only the broken half's; hoppers fill the right half then
the left; comparators read both halves and 0 when blocked; a screen moves
items across both halves; trapped halves both signal; copper pairs take the
younger stage, scrape and age together, only through the left half and not
while open; the half textures copy single ones; every state saves and
reloads, and contents reload by half.

## Explosions destroying drops

`Entities::blast` (`src/entity/mod.rs`) hurts dropped items and experience
orbs before anything else (Java gathers the entities first, so what the
blast breaks or kills drops afterwards and survives).

- Items and orbs have Java's 5 health. Each takes the blast's entity damage
  (`getEntityDamageAmount` with its own line of sight), keeps whole points as
  Java does, and is gone at 0; a merged orb goes with every orb in it. Both
  are pushed away from the blast.
- Items ignore a blast that breaks no blocks (`shouldAffectBlocklikeEntities`:
  a creeper's or ghast's without `mob_griefing`); orbs never do.
- Health saves: an item or orb that survived a blast appends its health to
  its level-file entry. Unhurt ones save exactly as before; an older build
  reading a hurt item ignores the field, but drops a hurt orb (its entry
  gains a seventh field).
- Every item is hurt: the nether star, the one item Java makes
  explosion-proof, comes with the Wither (v0.18) and needs the exception then.

Test: `explosions_destroy_dropped_items_and_orbs_near_them`.

## Frame time

`python3 tools/benchmark_scenes.py --scenes materials,building --repeats 3`
with this change's release build (Apple M5, AC power, 1920 x 1080). The
`materials` scene now includes the double halves, which its id range
covers. All six runs were invalid: the runner found the window hidden or
occluded (frames skipped, unpresented frames), with 1% lows of 53–72 FPS
that only show the window wasn't drawing, and the source wasn't committed
yet. The machine wasn't idle. These runs are no evidence either way; the
[owed runs](2026-10-27-v0.12-materials-frame-time.md) still are.
