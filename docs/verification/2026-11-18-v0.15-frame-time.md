# v0.15 frame-time run: the offscreen suite passes at `003cac4f` — 2026-11-18

[Roadmap §6.1](../roadmap.md#61-reference-configuration-and-frame-time-gate) · [v0.15 integration](2026-11-18-v0.15-integration.md) · [v0.14 tag gate run](2026-11-16-tag-gate-run.md)

This is the offscreen suite, the frame-time gate, run on the merged v0.15 tree with all eight tracks in. Every scene passes the [120 FPS gate](../roadmap.md#61-reference-configuration-and-frame-time-gate) in all three runs.

## Run

- **Build:** `t3/v0.15-villages-raids` at `003cac4f` (clean tree), built by the runner with `cargo build --release` after `cargo clean --release -p voxelcraft`.
- **Machine:** the owner's M5 (10-core GPU, 16 GB, macOS 27.2.1), on AC power, with the display and the user held awake by `caffeinate -d -u -i`.
- **Load:** remote checks ran on Namespace at the same time. They used the network, not local CPU or GPU work. A Codex review agent was also running locally.

```sh
python3 tools/benchmark_scenes.py --offscreen --output target/benchmarks/v0.15-offscreen
```

The runner judged all 24 runs valid and set `reference_gate_passed` to true. The summary and the per-frame samples are kept in [2026-11-18-003cac4-m5-offscreen](../../benchmarks/results/2026-11-18-003cac4-m5-offscreen/).

Every run meets both limits, a 1% low of at least 120 FPS and a p99 of at most 8.33 ms. The lowest 1% low is 225.1 FPS (streaming) and the highest p99 is 3.51 ms (materials).

Against the [v0.14 tag run](2026-11-16-tag-gate-run.md):
- Survival, entities and automation are within that run's spread or better.
- Materials and streaming are a few FPS below its lowest runs.
- The title screen's 1% low fell from about 3,700 to about 1,750 FPS, still far above the gate.

These are single sets of three runs on a machine that wasn't fully idle, so the differences aren't treated as regressions.

## Not covered

- **Code after the run.** The review fixes landed after `003cac4f`: hero credit for projectile kills, unresolved map offers, pot loot, legacy pillager crossbows, the raid village scan reading the claim indexes, the lectern pulse loader, and one price formula. None of them touches a per-frame path, and the raid scan change removes work.
- **The windowed suite,** which checks for stalls over 50 ms, wasn't run. The tag needs it.
- **New content in the scenes.** The scenes don't include a raid wave, a woodland mansion, a happy ghast flight or the Glowing outline. Frame time in those situations is unmeasured. A raid scene and a mansion-chunk streaming scene would cover the largest new costs.
