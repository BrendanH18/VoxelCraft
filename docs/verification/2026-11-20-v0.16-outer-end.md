## What is implemented

**Purpur and End rods.** The purpur block, pillar (axis from the clicked
face), stairs and slab with every stairs shape, half and water state through
the shared machinery, Java's hardness (1.5, the slab 2.0), resistance 6,
pickaxe needed, slab explosion decay; End rods in six facings (placed out of
the clicked face, or meeting a rod pointing at the face head on), light 14,
instant breaking, a rod-and-base model with Java's 4×4 outline, and
`animateTick`'s particle (one tick in five, drifting with 0.005 Gaussian
speed) as a new `EndRod` particle (`EndRodParticle`: 60–71 ticks, friction
0.91, gravity 0.0125). Recipes from the jar: purpur block (four popped
chorus fruit → 4), pillar (two slabs), slab (six), stairs (four), End rods
(blaze rod over popped chorus fruit → 4), and the stonecutter's pillar, two
slabs and stairs from a purpur block. Sounds are stone (purpur) and wood
(rods, chorus).

**Chorus.** Plants join chorus plants and flowers on every side and End
stone below (`PipeBlock(10)` core and arms), survive by `canSurvive`, and
break on a scheduled tick one game tick after losing support (dropping a
chorus fruit half the time; a flower dropping nothing, its table needing a
breaking entity). Flowers grow by `randomTick` (up, branching with
`nextInt(4)` tries plus one on a short stem over End stone, dying at age 4
or with no room, never above the world's top row), break when an arrow hits
them under `projectiles_can_break_blocks` (dropping themselves), and can be
placed only where they survive. Chorus fruit is food (4, 2.4, always
edible), teleports the eater with `TeleportRandomlyConsumeEffect` (16 tries
in a 16-block cube, `randomTeleport`'s scan down to a motion-blocking block,
room and no liquid), resets the fall and starts a one-second cooldown; it
smelts into popped chorus fruit (0.1 experience). The desktop, controller
and CLI-agent paths share `PlayerSession::chorus_teleport`.

**Generation 5 End.** `EndGen` now knows its generation; generation 4 is
untouched (`generation_4_chunk_hashes_stay_identical` passes unchanged).
In generation 5:

- Biomes follow `TheEndBiomeSource`: chunks within 64 of the centre are
  `the_end`, others are highlands above 0.25, midlands from -0.0625,
  small islands below -0.21875 and barrens between, sampled once per chunk at
  its middle (see the differences for the height value).
- Chorus plants grow in highlands chunks: `count` 0–4, `in_square`, the
  surface, air with End stone below, `generatePlant(…, 8)`.
- End cities: the `end_cities` triangular spread (spacing 20, separation 11,
  salt 10387313), the start at block (7, 7) needing a city biome, the
  rotation from `setLargeFeatureSeed`, the lowest of the four rotated
  corners at least y 60, and the start's own biome. Pieces are assembled by
  a port of `EndCityPieces` drawing from the same random sequence: the start
  house (base floor, second and third floor, third roof), towers with a
  random offset, one to three pieces and optional bridges in four
  directions, fat towers with up to two more middles and bridges, bridges
  of one to four pieces, steep or gentle stairs, ending in a new house tower
  or (once per city, with chance `1/(10 - depth)`) the ship 60–70 blocks
  out; depth limit 8; a child generation is kept only if the first piece it
  overlaps carries its parent's tag. Overwrite pieces store air; floors
  placed without overwrite leave what is there.
- Chest markers become chests filled from `chests/end_city_treasure`;
  `Sentry` markers spawn shulkers (once each, when a player comes within 96
  blocks, persistent); the ship's `Elytra` marker gives
  `City::elytra_frame` (cell and facing, Java's rotated `SOUTH`).
- `/locate structure end_city` finds the nearest start (console and agent
  completion).
- `generation_5_end_has_reachable_cities_with_loot_and_shulkers`: a city lies
  within 1200 blocks of where each of the first four gateways of seed 42
  leads.

**Templates (FE-02).** The 20 template names with Java's sizes, stored-cell
flag and markers (checked both ways against the fixture). The blocks are
original: storeys with End stone brick floors, purpur walls, magenta glass
windows and End rods, a ladder shaft that lines up through every storey and
roof of a house; towers with a central post and ladder clear of the four
bridge doorways; five-wide bridges whose deck meets the doorways bridge
ends cut; fat towers and the treasure loft with its two chests; and the
ship with an obsidian keel, a stern cabin holding the two loot chests either
side of the elytra frame's wall and a brewing stand, a mast and sail, a
raised forecastle and a lookout for its sentries, and the bow's stand for
the dragon head.

**Shulkers** (`entity::shulker`). 30 health, 5 experience, persistent,
never natural spawns. A shulker snaps to the bottom centre of its block,
clings to the first face it can (`canStayAt`: empty cell, sturdy face,
room to open), finds a new face or teleports when its support goes, and
never moves otherwise. The lid opens 0.05 a tick toward its raw peek; shut,
it has 20 armor and arrows bounce off. Goals: about once in ten ticks it
targets the nearest attackable player it can see in its 16-block box (4
along its attach axis); while it has a target it opens fully and fires a
bullet every 20–110 ticks while the target is within 20 blocks (dropping it
beyond); without one it peeks (raw 30) for one to three seconds now and
then. A player's hit makes it and nearby shulkers without targets turn on
them. An accepted hit below half health teleports it one time in four
(five tries within 8 blocks, not onto bedrock); otherwise a bullet hit on an
open shulker teleports it and leaves a new shulker of its colour where it
was unless crowded (failure chance `(n - 1) / 5` for the shulkers within
8). Drops: a shulker shell, chance 0.5 (0.5625 + 0.0625 per Looting level
above I). Colour variants (none or the 16 dyes) save, reload and replicate.
Voice: an original synth (ambient, hurt, death); the lid, shot, teleport and
bullet cues use the nearest shared sounds.

**Shulker bullets.** `selectNextMoveDirection`'s axis-at-a-time homing at
0.15 blocks a tick, turning when blocked, aligned or after 10–50 ticks,
accelerating toward the delta by 0.2 a tick, falling under 0.04 gravity
without a target; a hit deals 4 (`mob_projectile`) and 10 seconds of
Levitation to a player or mob (a shulker hit while open duplicates), a
block hit makes an explosion particle; an End rod particle trails each tick.
LAN clients draw them (`shulker_bullets` in the replica extras); Peaceful
removes them.

## Differences from Java

- **End islands (GEN-04b).** The outer islands stay VoxelCraft's original
  noise (blobs on a 128-block grid, tops about y 56–71) rather than
  `EndIslandFunction`'s simplex field over chunk-sized islands (sizes 9–21
  per chunk step, density `(doffs - 8) / 128`) and Java's noise router; no
  `end_island_decorated` small islands are placed. FE-01 covers only the
  Overworld and Nether noise: this record notes that the End's noise is
  original too, for the owner to rule on. The biome thresholds are Java's,
  but the height value they read is 100 × VoxelCraft's island mass, so End
  biomes follow the original islands. Seeds therefore can't reproduce
  Java's End cities' positions, though a given start lays out Java's pieces.
- Chorus feature draws use a seeded `JavaRandom` per 16-block chunk, not
  the decoration seed; plants ignore other chunks' plants when testing
  room, and cities are painted over plants (Java grows plants after
  structures, around them).
- The chorus plant stores one state: its connections are computed from the
  neighbours (as `updateShape` keeps them), so a Java world-gen plant whose
  connection Java left stale can look different.
- Chorus growth and death sounds use the nearest shared wood place and
  break sounds; the chorus fruit teleport uses the enderman teleport sound.
  Chorus plants don't block skylight like `PipeBlock` (`propagatesSkylightDown`
  is false in Java).
- Shulkers act once per game tick on the entity pass: a hit's teleport and
  duplication happen on the next tick, not inside `hurtServer`. The hit box
  is the shut 1×1×1 cube on every face (the opening lid doesn't grow it or
  push entities). The defense goal (only with a team) and mob targets are
  not modelled; hurt-by alerts reach shulkers within 16 blocks (10 up and
  down). Goal timings fold Java's every-other-tick goal updates into per-tick
  chances (peek one in 40, target search one in 10).
- Bullets aren't saved (a reload drops bullets in flight), can't be hit or
  punched by players, collide by sampling their path against block cells
  and entity boxes grown by 0.15, and are dropped after a minute of falling.
  Their knockback on a player is the fireball's estimate.
- Shulkers spawn from markers when a player first comes near, not as the
  chunk generates; they snap and cling on their first tick.
- The ship's item frame with its elytra and the bow's dragon head aren't
  placed: the elytra is Track B's item and the dragon head Track C's block.
  **TODO (integration):** place an item frame holding an elytra at
  `City::elytra_frame` (its cell, facing the piece's rotation of south, on
  the cabin's back wall) and a dragon head at `City::dragon_head` (the ship
  frame's `templates::DRAGON_HEAD`, on the bow's stand).
- Java's third floor holds an Ender chest beside the loot chest; ours does
  too, but other template contents (banners, the ship's dragon head) are
  original.
