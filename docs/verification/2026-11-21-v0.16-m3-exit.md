# v0.16: the M3 exit audit (GEN-01..07) — 2026-11-21

Status: **Implemented, unverified.** No owner play, and no agent played the
game. Branch `t3/v016-m3-exit-audit`, from the v0.16 integration branch at
`9c3abec` (wave 1 and wave 2 Tracks E, F and H merged; Track G, built
alongside, isn't on this branch), built by Claude Opus 5.5 as the
implementation agent for the M3 exit corpus that the
[v0.16 scope](../roadmap.md#v016-scope-the-end-and-the-wither)'s item 7
names. It audits every GEN row against the
[M3 exit](../roadmap.md#m3--java-terrain-and-exploration):

> A documented seed/coordinate corpus matches Java biome, terrain, feature
> and structure fixtures to the agreed fidelity contract. Generate
> neighboring chunks in different orders/threads and across cache
> evictions; compare outputs and inspect borders. Explore each core
> biome/structure family without cheats, obtain its rewards, and prove
> cleared containers/residents stay cleared after restart.

The agreed fidelity contract is [FE-01](../roadmap.md#14-what-can-follow-10)
(original Overworld and Nether noise, so Java seeds and coordinates don't
carry over) and [FE-02](../roadmap.md#14-what-can-follow-10) (original
templates held to Java's placement, pools, piece kinds and sizes, loot,
residents and spawners). Under FE-01 the corpus can't compare Java's own
seed/coordinate fixtures block for block; it pins VoxelCraft's coordinates
and checks every placement rule and container against the jar's data.

## The corpus

`world::terrain::m3_corpus::every_gen_05_family_matches_the_java_corpus_on_two_seeds`
locates the nearest instance of each of 26 families from the origin in a
generation 5 world of seeds 12345 and 42 (the `/locate` answer; dungeons,
fossils and wells by the discovery helpers the older tests use, a mesa
mineshaft from the nearest badlands), and pins the 52 coordinates. For each
instance it checks:

- **Placement.** The start chunk is the potential chunk Java's
  `RandomSpreadStructurePlacement` gives its region for the set's spacing,
  separation, salt and spread type (linear, or triangular for monuments,
  mansions and End cities), read from the fixture; the set's frequency
  reducer accepts it (`legacy_type_1` for outposts, `legacy_type_2` for
  buried treasure, `legacy_type_3` for mineshafts); and no villages
  potential chunk lies within the outposts' 10-chunk exclusion zone. Jigsaw
  structures whose start the corpus reads from a piece (ruined portals,
  trail ruins, ancient cities, trial chambers, Nether fossils) may sit one
  chunk off it; every one of them sat on it. `ring_and_feature_placements_match_java`
  checks all 128 strongholds of both seeds against the ring each falls in
  (`count` 128, `distance` 32, `spread` 3: rings of 3, 6, 10, 15, 21, 28, 36
  and 9), and the counts, height bands and rarities of dungeons (10 and 4 a
  chunk), fossils (1 in 64) and desert wells (1 in 1000) against the placed
  features.
- **Containers.** Every container the instance's chunks register
  (`Generator::structure_features`) names a loot table that is one of
  Java's (chest, barrel, dispenser or pot) and that VoxelCraft's
  `loot_tables.json` can roll, and the generated chunk holds a container at
  the first one.
- **Residents and spawners.** Spawner features stand on spawner blocks, and
  the family has the residents and spawners Java gives it (below).

## GEN-07: order, threads and cache eviction

`world::terrain::order_tests` gains three tests and a wider eviction:

- `generation_5_ignores_order_threads_and_caches` runs generation 4's
  corpus (origin borders, a whole village, every temple family within 1200
  blocks, outpost, ocean ruin, ruined portal, buried treasure, mineshaft,
  trail ruins, fossils and a well, a whole ancient city and trial chamber
  at negative coordinates) plus, whole, the nearest monument, shipwreck and
  abandoned camp, the nearest stronghold (−686..−534, −12..22,
  −1749..−1668: every piece of Track E's Java layout), a mesa mineshaft and
  a sulfur spring with their neighbours: 1,160 chunks, each generated four
  ways (row order on a fresh generator as the reference, shuffled on
  another, interleaved on six threads sharing one generator, and in row
  order with caches emptied on a five-chunk cycle) and compared block for
  block and feature for feature. It also checks the reference holds a
  silverfish spawner, stronghold and shipwreck chests, an abandoned camp
  chest, monument bricks and a sulfur spring's vents.
- `generation_5_nether_complexes_ignore_order_threads_and_caches`: every
  chunk of a whole generation 5 fortress and a whole bastion (80 chunks),
  reversed, on six threads, and on a fresh generator every third chunk.
- `generation_5_end_city_ignores_order_threads_and_caches`: every chunk of
  a generation 5 End city with its ship (36 chunks), the same three ways.
- The eviction step that empties structure caches now also replaces the
  strongholds, geodes and sulfur springs, for every generation.

Chunk borders are checked by comparing whole chunks: a structure clipped at
a border, or painted differently from either side, would differ between
runs. All three tests and the four older generations' passed.

## Cleared structures stay cleared after a restart

`entity::m3_restart_tests` loads a real headless world on generation 5
around generated structures, clears them as a player would, writes the
level with `Storage` in the game's save format (the dimension's `chests`
and `spawners`, the entities' properties, the edited chunks), and reads it
back on a fresh generator, so every unedited chunk and every structure
layout is generated again:

- `generation_5_overworld_structures_stay_cleared_after_a_restart`: a
  shipwreck chest, an abandoned camp chest and a stronghold chest emptied;
  the stronghold portal room's silverfish spawner broken; an ocean
  monument's three elder guardians spawned and killed. After the restart
  the chests are empty, the spawner is air and unregistered, and no elder
  guardian spawns.
- `generation_5_fortress_stays_cleared_after_a_restart`: a fortress chest
  emptied and a blaze spawner broken.
- `generation_5_end_city_stays_cleared_after_a_restart`: an End city chest
  emptied, the shulkers near the ship spawned and killed, and the ship's
  elytra taken from its frame; neither the shulkers nor the frame come back.

Older tests cover the rest: village chests of generations 2 to 4
(`world::village`), ruined portal and ocean ruin chests
(`world::ruined_portal`, `world::temples::ocean_ruins`), the outpost's
allays and the swamp hut's cat (`entity::allay`, `entity::cat_spawner`),
bastion residents (`entity::nether`), trial spawners and vaults
(`world::trial`), and the saved `structures_populated` markers
(`entity::aquatic`).

## Defects

Fixed here:

- **Monuments without elder guardians.** Structure residents were spawned
  only once every resident's box was free of blocks, which was meant to
  wait for the cells to load. Java spawns structure mobs where the piece
  puts them without a collision check, and an elder guardian's 2×2 box can
  reach into a monument's wall: seed 12345's nearest monument
  (−392 48 −360) has an elder at −415 45 −352 whose box meets six wall
  blocks, so none of its three elders ever spawned. Residents now wait only
  for every cell under their boxes to be loaded
  (`entity::aquatic::cells_loaded`). This applies to every generation's
  temple residents; a resident that would have stood in a block before now
  spawns there, as in Java.

Larger ones, each with the release the audit proposes to own it. None of
these assignments is approved yet: moving content between releases is the
owner's call, and the roadmap is unchanged until then.

| Defect | Rows | Proposed owner |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mineshaft chests are plain chests, not chest minecarts on rails (Track E) | GEN-05c | v0.17 (minecarts) |
| Strongholds skip Java's 112-block `#stronghold_biased_to` biome search at each ring position (Track E) | GEN-05c | v0.18 |
| Bastions use a compact original pool graph, not Java's full pool catalogue at the FE-02 contract | GEN-05g | v0.18 |
| Structure decoration randomness (wall stones, cobwebs, loot seeds) uses VoxelCraft streams rather than each chunk's decoration random, except where a track ported it (monuments, bridge ends) | GEN-05 | None: a recorded difference unless the owner wants it |
| Generation 5 has no golden chunk hashes; the corpus pins only locations | GEN-07 | Integration, before the tag |
| Nether biomes, features and structure placement aren't yet checked against Java beyond fortresses and bastions | GEN-04a | v0.16 integration or v0.18 |
