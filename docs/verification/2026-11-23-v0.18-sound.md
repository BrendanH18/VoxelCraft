## Fixture

`tools/extract_java_sound_fixtures.py server.jar client.jar sounds.json
--output tests/fixtures/java/26.3/sounds.json` checks all three SHA-1s and
writes:

- `subtitles`: the subtitle key of each of the 1,889 events that have one;
  `subtitle_text`: the English text of the 976 keys
  (`subtitles.entity.baby_chicken.hurt` has none in 26.3; Java then shows
  the key, and so does VoxelCraft); `silent_events`: the 102 events without
  one; `attenuation_distances`: the 44 events whose files reach farther than
  16 blocks.
- `sound_types`: every `SoundType` a block uses (129), with volume, pitch, the
  five events and whether hit and fall play the step event's files;
  `block_sound_types`: the type of every one of Java's 1,286 blocks, and
  `block_names`, their English names.
- `options` (labels, defaults and range of the three options), `overlay`
  (`DISPLAY_TIME` 3000 ms, `LOOPING_DISPLAY_FULL_BRIGHTNESS_TIME` 500 ms, the
  0.8 default backing, 35 pixels up) and `lids` (both lid speeds 0.1 a tick;
  every open and close sound at volume 0.5, pitch 0.9 to 1.0).

The extractor fails rather than guess when a constant, constructor or
override changes form.

## What was built

### Subtitles (UX-03d)

- `src/audio/events.rs`: every `Sound` names its Java event
  (`Sound::java_event`). Only the synthesized wind and cave room tone have
  none.
- `src/audio/subtitles.rs`: `SubtitleOverlay`'s logic. A played sound adds
  its caption or refreshes the line with the same text (one entry per
  position, newest last); entries older than 3 s go, and a line with none
  left goes. A line shows while the listener is within its range
  (attenuation distance, 16 unless the fixture says otherwise, times the
  volume when that is above 1; sounds with no position are heard anywhere).
  Brightness falls from 255 to 75 over 3 s, after 0.5 s at full for looping
  sounds. The arrow follows `getBestSubtitleCandidate`: none when the sound
  is within 60° of the view (`forward · delta > 0.5`) or at the ear, else
  `>` or `<` by the side.
- `Audio::play_in` records each sound while Closed Captions is on and the
  sound isn't muted; the rain loop refreshes "Rain falls" twice a second
  while rain is audible, standing in for Java's repeated `weather.rain`.
- `src/app/hud.rs` `captions_ui`: Java's layout. Lines stack up from
  `guiHeight - 35`, 10 pixels apart, centred on `guiWidth - width / 2 - 2`,
  where width is the widest caption plus the widths of `<`, ` `, `>` and ` `;
  each has a backing one pixel beyond the half-width and four pixels above
  and below its middle in Text Background's colour; text has a shadow and
  goes through `Ui::text`. Captions draw under the pause menu and options
  screens and over everything else, as `Hud` defers them for in-game
  screens.
- Options: Closed Captions (`showSubtitles`, default OFF; Java 26.3's label
  for the old Show Subtitles) in Music & Sound Options (after the sliders,
  where Java has it after the device, its row partner Directional Audio left
  out) and in Accessibility Settings (Java's second item); Text Background
  Opacity (`textBackgroundOpacity`, 0–100%, default 50%) and Text Background
  (`backgroundForChatOnly`: Chat (default) or Everywhere) after it.
  `Settings::background_opacity(default)` is `getBackgroundOpacity`.

### Blocks' own sounds (UX-02)

- `src/audio/block_sounds.rs` reads the fixture once at startup. Each state
  finds its Java block through `java_registry::state_java_ids` (ID, English
  name and the state aliases the mining test used, now shared), then its
  `SoundType`. States with no Java block keep the shared `Material` sounds.
- 126 Java event families (`block.<family>.*`), each with its own original
  recipe: granular crunch at the family's pitch and grain, plus a wooden
  knock, metal ring, crystal chime, glass ping, wet squelch, chain clinks or
  the honey squelch where the material calls for one. Stone, deepslate and
  its bricks, tiles and polished form, tuff and its forms, mud, mud bricks,
  packed mud, copper (block, bulb, grate, statue), the cherry, bamboo and
  nether woods and their hanging signs, amethyst and its buds, and the
  v0.12 materials (candles, sculk, froglights, resin, sulfur, cinnabar…)
  all differ from each other and from the old shared set.
- A family's hit and fall reuse its step buffers (no extra memory), as
  Java's hit and fall events play the step files.
- Playback follows Java: break and place `(v + 1) / 2`, pitch `p × 0.8`;
  step `v × 0.15`, pitch `p`; mining hit every fourth tick (was every 0.22 s)
  `(v + 1) / 8`, pitch `p × 0.5`; fall `v × 0.5`, pitch `p × 0.75`.
  `block_sounds::GAIN` scales each to the old loudness for a volume-1 type
  (break 1.0, place 0.9, step 0.55, hit 0.45, fall 0.8), so only Java's
  per-type differences change loudness. Pitches are now Java's exact values
  where they used to be random ranges; variety comes from the variants.
- Footsteps (the player's, other players', landing falls), breaking,
  placing, mining hits and the sniffer's digging all use them.
- New `Sound` variants appended after `Sound::Crafter`:
  `Block(Family, BlockCue)` (only the family/cue pairs some type plays are
  synthesized) and `Container(ContainerCue)`. LAN carries them by key like
  every sound; guests hear their own steps locally as before.

### Container lids

- `src/world/lids.rs`: `ChestLidController` for chests, trapped chests,
  copper chests and ender chests (0.1 a tick each way, `getOpenness`
  interpolated by the partial tick), barrels' open top, and the open/close
  cues. `World::set_container_viewers` takes the same open list as the
  shulker boxes (keyboard, controllers, LAN guests) plus copper golems'
  chests; ender chests now join that list from controllers and LAN guests.
  The first viewer's arrival and the last one's leaving play the sound:
  a single chest at its centre, a double chest once from its right half,
  shifted half a block toward the left (`ChestBlockEntity.playSound`), a
  barrel at its open face. Volume 0.5, pitch 0.9–1.0, `BLOCKS`; copper chests
  use the weathered and oxidized events at those stages.
- Rendering: a container in use or with its lid still moving is left out of
  its chunk's mesh (`MeshInput::models`) and drawn by the block-model pass:
  the chest's body and a lid turned up about its back edge 9 pixels up by
  `π/2 × (1 − (1 − t)³)`, with the block's own textures; a barrel with its
  open end on texture layer 1290 (new original pixels: the closed top's rim
  around a dark hollow). Entering or leaving remeshes that one chunk in the
  same tick, as a block edit does. Closed containers cost nothing per tick
  or frame.
- LAN: the host sends lids within 160 blocks (`lids` in the snapshot, like
  `shulker_lids`); a guest applies them and remeshes the chunks that change.
  The sounds reach guests through the sound tap.
- The old stand-in chest sound (`Place(Wood)` at 0.65 or 0.8 pitch when a
  screen opened or closed) is gone.

## Differences from Java

- Sounds are original procedural recipes, not Java's recordings; each
  family's character is a design choice no one has listened to yet.
- Stand-ins: a sound used for several Java events captions as its main one
  (`Sound::Door` is "Door creaks/Door closes" also for fence gates and
  trapdoors; `Sound::Teleport` stands in for anchors and portals;
  `Sound::Bow` for throws and crossbows; legacy `Material` sounds keep their
  call sites in screens, beds and buckets). Crop planting plays
  "Block placed" where Java says "Crop planted", since `item.crop.plant`
  and `item.nether_wart.plant` are item events outside the block families.
- Notification Time (`notificationDisplayTime`) isn't an option yet: the
  caption time is Java's default 1×, 3 s. Captions follow the main view in
  split-screen.
- With F1 (no HUD) Java still shows captions over an open inventory; here
  the UI isn't built without the HUD, so they hide.
- The barrel's `open` is drawn from its viewers rather than saved as a
  block state: no state IDs were reserved for this track. Java resets it on
  load anyway (`recheckOpeners`).
- The decorated pot's cracked sound type isn't used: VoxelCraft's pots
  don't crack.
- `Entity.playStepSound`'s use of a carpet or snow layer on top of the block
  below, and the step cadence, are unchanged.
- Captions measure text with today's font (`Ui::text_width`); Track 1's font
  changes the widths.
