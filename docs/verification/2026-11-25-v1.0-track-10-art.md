## What changed

1. **197 tiles redrawn in place, in v0.18's style** (a new generator,
   [`src/render/final_art_textures.rs`](../../src/render/final_art_textures.rs),
   a child of the material generator so its tiles share the material
   cache, the pinned-checksum test and the memory budget). Integer maths
   only, so every platform draws the same texels; tiles are built once,
   on first use. No layer was added and none of the reserved layers
   4066–4079 was used.
   - **Wooden doors and trapdoors, all thirteen woods** (oak, spruce,
     birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, mangrove, cherry, pale oak, bamboo,
     crimson, warped and poplar): 39 tiles, both door halves and the
     trapdoor of each. Every face is built from parts (raised frame,
     recessed panel, flat boards with one-texel seams, openings, iron
     fittings) and shaded by one rule set: light from the top left, the
     frame lit at its outer top and left edges and on the lower and right
     walls of every opening and recess, shaded on the upper and left
     walls, recesses shaded under the frame, short wood grain on the
     boards. Each wood has its own original design: oak four panes over
     two fielded panels; spruce boards with a peephole and a Z brace;
     birch a grid of nine panes over one deep panel; jungle a porthole
     in an iron ring over studded boards; acacia a diamond light over
     two panels with diamonds; dark oak heavy boards with iron straps
     and a barred grille; mangrove two tall lights over plank courses;
     cherry an arched light over a carved blossom; pale oak two narrow
     lights over three panels; bamboo canes with nodes, open between them
     at the top; crimson and warped rows of slots over a doubly framed
     panel; poplar three slits over chevron boards. Door halves carry
     iron hinges on the left stile. Upper halves and trapdoors have
     see-through openings; lower halves have none, as before.
   - **Chests:** top, side, front and the trapped chest's front (the
     double chests' halves are drawn from these, so they follow). Plank
     boards in a dark rim, the lid's seam lit below and shaded above, a
     silver latch on the front and a red one on the trapped chest. The
     game has no Christmas chest, so there is none to draw.
   - **Dirt path:** a packed, smoothed top in the natural cluster style;
     sides of the redrawn dirt under a lip of the path; the bottom is the
     dirt tile.
   - **Hay bale:** straw strands bound by two red-brown bands lit along
     their top, and the bale's cut ends.
   - **Bell:** polished gold lit down its left like a cylinder, a bevelled
     top, and the rim bands on the face the model uses for them.
   - **Anvil:** hammered dark iron for the body; the striking face worn
     bright down the middle, with more cracks and chipped corners at each
     damage stage (chipped, damaged).
   - **TNT:** red paper sticks lit on their left, a white band with a
     stencilled name, the stick ends on top and bottom, and the fuse.
   - **Plants, 139 tiles**, drawn as sprites with a lit upper left and a
     shaded lower right on every leaf, petal and stalk:
     - the small flowers (dandelion, golden dandelion, poppy, blue orchid,
       cornflower, lily of the valley, oxeye daisy, azure bluet, allium,
       the four tulips, wither rose, torchflower, open and closed
       eyeblossom, cactus flower) and the tall flowers (sunflower, lilac,
       rose bush, peony, pitcher plant, both halves);
     - the saplings (oak, spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, dark oak, cherry,
       pale oak, poplar), the mangrove propagule (also the hanging one)
       and the bamboo shoot, each a small tree of its own shape;
     - short and tall grass, fern and large fern (still green in every
       texel, so the biome tints recolour them as before), the dead bush,
       bush, firefly bush and the dry grasses;
     - the crops at every age: wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroots, melon
       and pumpkin stems and their attached stems, torchflower crop,
       pitcher crop (the upper half stays empty until age 3, as the
       models expect), nether wart and the sweet berry bush;
     - mushrooms (kept at their small size), crimson and warped fungi,
       sugar cane (canes running the full height, so stacks join), the
       Nether roots and sprouts, weeping and twisting vines (tips and
       plants), kelp, seagrass and tall seagrass, cave vines with and
       without glow berries, spore blossom, hanging roots, big dripleaf
       stem, small dripleaf, pale hanging moss and the red shrub.

   Potted plants draw their plant from these tiles, so they follow. The
   old drawings stay in `textures.rs`, `plant_textures.rs`,
   `crop_textures.rs`, `sniffer_plant_textures.rs`, `gen5_textures.rs`,
   `nether_textures.rs`, `overworld_textures.rs`, `cave_textures.rs` and
   `utility_textures.rs`, unreached for these layers: the material lookup
   comes first in `textures::pixel`. Removing them is left to the
   integrator or a later cleanup, so this branch doesn't touch files other
   tracks may edit.
2. **Mob detail (MOB-06).** The [v0.18 mobs record](2026-11-23-v0.18-mobs.md)
   ends with every mob carrying hand detail (fourth round), so no mob has
   only a pattern left. Checking each `MobKind` against the model code,
   the two with the least were given a small pass, original cuboids on
   the existing models: the **ravager** (slit pupils with a glint, a heavy
   brow over each eye, nostrils, a dark mane along its hump and a scar on
   its flank) and the **zombified piglin** (tusks beside the snout and a
   ragged mouth under it). No new texture layers.
3. **Asset manifest:** `procedural_resources.material_art` records the
   round in its provenance, 669 tiles and a 768 KiB cache budget (669 ×
   1 KiB = 669 KiB).
4. **Capture scenes**, appended to `tools/capture_scenes.json`
   (`materials` group, 1920 × 1080): `material-wood-doors` (the thirteen
   doors, both halves, face on in front of a calcite wall),
   `material-wood-trapdoors-chests` (the trapdoors as 2 × 2 groups, a
   chest, a trapped chest and a double of each), `material-farm-blocks`
   (dirt path beside grass, hay bales both ways, TNT, the three anvils,
   four bells), `material-plants` (four rows of flowers, mushrooms,
   grasses, saplings and tall plants) and `material-crops` (every crop
   age on farmland, nether wart on soul sand, berry bushes and the
   others). The plant and crop scenes turn random ticks off
   (`random_tick_speed=0`): in the first before-run a mangrove propagule
   grew into a tree during the capture. Door states are placed by raw
   state id (lower half, then the upper half eight ids on), so
   `tests/capture_scenes.rs` now accepts registered raw ids as the game's
   `--place` does.
5. **A review sheet:** the ignored test
   `render::textures::tests::final_art_sheet` writes every layer of this
   round, 8× and as a 2 × 2 repeat, to `$VC_FINAL_ART_SHEET`. It reads
   through `textures::pixel`, so the same test drew the before sheet on
   the scene commit and the after sheet on the art commit.

## Sources

No Minecraft texture was opened, copied or traced. Every design, palette
and shape is original and follows [the art and UI guide](../art-and-ui.md).
Block and state layouts (which faces a door, chest or bell uses, the
pitcher crop's halves, the attached stems' bend) are VoxelCraft's own and
unchanged. No Java behaviour changed, so no Java fixture was read.

## Tests added

In `src/render/final_art_textures.rs`:

- `the_round_replaces_existing_layers_only`: the 197 layers are distinct,
  all below the block array's existing 4,066, which hasn't grown (so none
  of the reserved 4066–4079 is used), and each is drawn.
- `doors_and_trapdoors_keep_their_openings`: every door half and trapdoor
  has solid stiles on both sides; upper halves and trapdoors have
  see-through openings and lower halves none; alpha is cut-out only; no
  wood repeats another's face.
- `grasses_and_ferns_keep_taking_the_biome_tints`: every texel of tall
  grass and the fern stays green (`g > r`, `g >= b`), the condition
  `tint_foliage` recolours.
- `plants_are_shaded_cut_out_sprites`: every plant is a cut-out sprite
  with at least three shades, except the young pitcher's upper half,
  which must stay empty.
- `the_trapped_chest_differs_only_at_its_latch`.

Extended: `render::textures::material::tests::redrawn_tiles_are_deterministic_and_pinned`
pins all 669 tiles (the 472 earlier ones unchanged) and the stone asset;
`texture_memory_stays_within_the_recorded_budget` reads the new budget;
`tests/capture_scenes.rs` checks raw state ids.

## Gaps and open decisions

- **The owner's approval of the look** is required before this merges,
  and the owner's ordinary play.
- **Not redrawn**, as outside this round's list: the flat ground plants
  (lily pad, pink petals, wildflowers, leaf litter), vines and glow
  lichen (flat, not cross-model), corals, coral fans and sea pickles
  (animals, not plants), the bamboo stalk and its leaves (a shaped
  model), the potted torchflower's own layer (3443; the other potted
  plants follow their plant), the ender chest, and amethyst buds.
  Glazed terracotta is kept on purpose. Open decision for the owner:
  whether any of these belong in a later round.
- **Old drawings left in place.** The replaced layers' previous drawings
  are still in nine generator files, now unreached. Deleting them touches
  files other tracks may change, so it is left for after integration.
- **Mob art:** no mob had only a pattern left; the two given more detail
  were chosen as the least detailed. The motion items and frame-time run
  in the v0.18 mobs record are unchanged.
- **Frame time:** the round adds no layers and no per-frame work: 197 more
  tiles are built once at startup (about 0.2 MiB more in the tile cache).
  Startup cost and the frame-time gate stay to be measured on the
  reference Mac.
- Captures are software-rendered and appearance only. The hanging
  plants placed on the ground in `material-crops` (cave vines, spore
  blossom, hanging roots, pale hanging moss) don't show in either run,
  and the water plants aren't in any scene, so the review sheet is their
  only evidence.
