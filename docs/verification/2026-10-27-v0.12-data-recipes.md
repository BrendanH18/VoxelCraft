[Roadmap](../roadmap.md#v012-scope-building-materials) · [Requirement inventory](../requirements.md) · [Fixture notes](../../tests/fixtures/java/26.3/README.md#recipes)

Scope: v0.12 work item 6, the recipe slice. Every Java 26.3 recipe in
`data/minecraft/recipe/` compared with VoxelCraft's crafting, furnace,
smoker, blast furnace, campfire, stonecutter and smithing data, both ways,
and the game's data fixed to match. Integrated and checked at `a463b50` (PR #50, with the other slices); "before" counts were measured on `70be72a`.
Nothing here is Verified: the owner has not played it.

## What was compared

`tools/extract_java_recipe_fixtures.py` writes
`tests/fixtures/java/26.3/recipes.json` (255 KB, one recipe per line).
`tests/java_recipes.rs` (comparison code in `tests/java_recipes/compare.rs`,
shared with `examples/recipe_audit.rs`) checks:

- **Crafting** (shaped, shapeless, transmute, decorated pot): a reference
  recipe is compared when its result and at least one alternative of every
  slot are registered; alternatives VoxelCraft lacks (poplar planks in
  `#planks`) are left out of both sides. Shape, mirror image, every slot's
  alternatives and the result count must equal one VoxelCraft recipe, and
  every grid built from the recipe (each slot switched to each alternative)
  must craft that result through `Grid::result`, so no other recipe shadows
  it. Every VoxelCraft recipe must equal a reference recipe.
- **Smelting, blasting, smoking, campfire cooking**: each registered input
  gives the result in the right furnace kind, the experience matches
  (`smelt_xp`), and the cook time equals Java's effective time (200 ticks,
  100 in smokers and blast furnaces through the fast speed multiplier, 600
  on campfires). Every item VoxelCraft can hold is checked for cooking
  recipes Java doesn't have.
- **Stonecutting**: every registered input/result pair with its count is
  in `crafting::stonecutting::all()`, and nothing else is.
- **Smithing**: every registered transform upgrades through
  `smithing::upgrade`; no other base upgrades.

## Allow-list

In `tests/java_recipes/compare.rs`, each with its reason:

- `crafting_dye/leather_{helmet,chestplate,leggings,boots,horse_armor}_dyed`
  (5): dyed leather colours are a data component VoxelCraft doesn't have yet.
- `crafting_special_repairitem/repair_item`: combining two damaged items in
  the grid isn't implemented; anvils and grindstones repair.
- `crafting_special_bannerduplicate/*` (16): copying a banner's pattern
  layers; banners are plain until the loom (owner decision, v0.17).

There are no allow-listed VoxelCraft-only recipes: every invented recipe
was removed or corrected.

## Changes a player will notice

Crafting (`src/crafting.rs`, `src/crafting/colour.rs`):

- A melon block no longer crafts into nine slices (Java has no such
  recipe); nine slices now craft a melon.
- Granite is one diorite and one quartz, anywhere in the grid (it was two
  of each); andesite is one cobblestone and one diorite for two (it was two
  of each).
- New recipes: coarse dirt, dripstone block, name tag (any metal nugget
  over paper), stone brick stairs and slab, muddy mangrove roots, red dye
  from beetroot, and re-dyeing wool slabs and wool stairs (32 recipes).
- Wider ingredients: the brewing stand takes any `#stone_crafting_materials`
  (cobbled deepslate, blackstone); TNT takes red sand; the daylight detector
  takes any wooden slab; the smoker takes crimson and warped stems and
  hyphae (`#logs`); mushroom stew takes any two mushrooms.
- Narrower: bamboo mosaic slabs no longer count as wooden slabs (composter,
  barrel, lectern, chiseled bookshelf, daylight detector).
- Shape: the jack o'lantern needs the carved pumpkin above the torch.
  Hay bales, packed ice, blue ice, nether wart blocks, prismarine bricks and
  bamboo blocks are shapeless nines, and the pale oak and bamboo buttons and
  rabbit stew follow Java's split; a full grid crafts the same as before.

Furnaces (`src/world/furnace.rs`):

- Emerald ore and redstone ore (and their deepslate forms) smelt and blast
  into an emerald (1.0 experience) and redstone (0.7).
- Red sand smelts into glass (`#smelts_to_glass`).
- Smokers dry kelp.
- A sponge gives 0.15 experience (was 0.1).

Stonecutter data (`src/crafting/stonecutting.rs`): the 105 recipes of the
families from before v0.12 (stone, cobblestone, bricks, sandstone, stone
bricks, granite, diorite, andesite and polished forms, mossy cobblestone,
tuff, deepslate forms, blackstone forms, basalt, mud bricks, prismarine
forms) now sit with the v0.12 tracks' tables behind
`crafting::stonecutting::all()`. The stonecutter screen is still v0.17, so
players see no change.

Campfire cooking and smithing already matched.
