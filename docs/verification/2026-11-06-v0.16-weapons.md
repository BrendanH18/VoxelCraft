## Implemented behavior

- **Items.** Trident (250 uses, 9 attack damage, 1.1 attack speed,
  enchantability 1, rare), mace (500 uses, 6 damage, 0.6 speed,
  enchantability 15), spears of all seven tiers (tier durability, `1 +
  material bonus` damage, `1 / attackDuration` speed, minimum attack charge
  1, the tier's enchantability; netherite fire resistant). Trident and mace
  carry Java's tool component (2 wear per block, can't break blocks in
  Creative); every weapon wears 1 per hit. Wooden spears are 200-tick fuel.
  Java names resolve, so the existing loot tables now produce the bastion
  `diamond_spear` entries, the ocean-ruin `stone_spear` and buried-treasure
  `iron_spear` entries (the fixture tables were filled in to match).
- **Crafting.** The six spear recipes, the netherite spear smithing upgrade
  (keeping wear and enchantments), and the mace recipe, which appears once a
  `breeze_rod` item is registered (Track C). Anvil repair: spears with their
  tier material, the mace with breeze rods; tridents have no repair item.
- **Enchanting.** Spears join the sword's `melee_weapon` sets (sharpness,
  smite, bane, knockback, fire aspect, looting) and Lunge; the mace takes
  smite, bane, fire aspect, Density, Breach and Wind Burst; tridents take
  Loyalty, Impaling, Riptide and Channeling. Java's exclusive sets apply
  (damage set includes Impaling/Density/Breach; Riptide excludes Loyalty and
  Channeling). Wind Burst is treasure, never offered by the table or random
  loot and not tradeable; villager book picks keep their previous RNG.
- **Heavy core.** A waterloggable 8×8×8 block: hardness 10, blast resistance
  1200, pickaxe-mineable but drops with any tool, drops itself (the
  explosion-survival loot policy), creative palette, original texture.
- **Mace smash** (every input path through `PlayerSession::attack`). Falls
  over 1.5 blocks smash: Java's piecewise bonus plus Density per block,
  added before the critical multiplier; the attacker's motion becomes 0.01
  blocks a tick upward; nearby living mobs within 3.5 blocks of the target
  (not the attacker's tamed animals or its allay) are thrown out and up,
  doubled after 5 blocks; the fall distance resets and later fall damage is
  measured from the impact height for Java's 40-tick grace (the impulse
  context in `Vitals`); air/ground/heavy smash sounds and block dust. Breach
  lowers armor effectiveness by 0.15 a level where mob armor applies (magma
  cubes, horse armor). **Wind Burst** (fall ≥ 1.5, not flying) launches the
  attacker by 1.2/1.75/2.2 blocks a tick times exposure and pushes nearby
  mobs, ending the impulse grace like Java's non-wind-charge explosion.
- **Tridents.** Charge with use; release after ≥ 10 ticks throws a saved
  `ThrownTrident` (2.5 blocks a tick plus the thrower's motion, Java's
  spread), worn by 1 without ever breaking (`hurtWithoutBreaking`); Creative
  keeps its trident and the throw is Creative-only pickup. The entity runs
  Java's 20 Hz arrow physics (0.05 gravity, 0.99 drag in air and water,
  stepping block clip, sticking, falling when its block goes, 1200-tick
  despawn unless loyal and recoverable), deals 8 plus Impaling (2.5 a level
  against Java's `#aquatic` kinds), reverses at `(-0.01, -0.1, -0.01)` after
  a hit, never hits twice, and Endermen dodge it. **Loyalty** returns it
  through blocks after it hits or rests five ticks (Java's acceleration and
  vertical pull, the return sound once), dropping it if the owner is gone.
  **Riptide** needs water or rain and no vehicle: it pushes 1.5/2.25/3 blocks
  a tick along the view, lifts a grounded player 1.2 blocks and runs the
  20-tick spin attack (8 damage to the first mob touched, bounce back, stop
  on a wall). **Channeling** in a thunderstorm calls the v0.15 lightning on a
  hit mob or player under open sky, or on a sky-lit lightning rod.
  **Pointed dripstone** breaks with its drops when a trident faster than 0.6
  blocks a tick hits it, gated by `projectiles_can_break_blocks` and, for a
  drowned's trident, `mob_griefing` (ENV-04). Pickup follows Java: only the
  owner (or anyone once ownerless), after the shake, Creative-only for
  Creative throws, always for the owner of a returning trident.
- **Drowned.** 10% of spawns hold an item: 10 in 16 a trident, else a
  fishing rod (`populateDefaultEquipmentSlots`); saved as `main_hand`. A
  drowned with a trident stands and throws every 40 ticks within 10 blocks
  in sight (1.6 blocks a tick, difficulty uncertainty `14 - 4 × id`), else
  closes in; hits on players use the armored mob-damage path. On a player
  kill its hand item drops 8.5% of the time with Java's rerolled wear.
- **Spears.** The attack key always jabs (`PiercingWeapon.attack`) and never
  mines; it is refused until fully charged. A jab stabs every mob between 2
  and 4.5 blocks (6.5 in Creative) along the look plus the forward motion,
  boxes grown by 0.125, stopping at the first block: charge-scaled damage
  plus the enchantment bonus, `hurtServer`'s push, 0.4 extra knockback plus
  the Knockback enchantment, wear and exhaustion per mob, the attacker slowed
  and unsprinting. **Lunge** (not riding, not in water, food ≥ 7 unless
  Creative) costs a durability and 4 exhaustion a level and pushes 0.458
  blocks a tick a level along the horizontal look. Holding use charges
  (`KineticWeapon`): after the tier's delay, each mob the reach passes is
  stabbed at most every 10 ticks with dismount/knockback/damage windows and
  thresholds per tier, `1 + floor(relative speed × multiplier)` damage,
  using the vehicle's motion when riding. A spear charge keeps full speed.
- **Input paths.** Everything runs in `PlayerSession`: `attack` (mace,
  jab), `start_draw`/`tick_draw`/`release_draw` (trident and spear charges,
  throws, Riptide) and `tick_weapons` (the spin attack), so the desktop,
  controller seats, LAN peers (through `hands::Actor`) and the CLI agent's
  `draw` command share them. Thrown tridents reach LAN clients through the
  replica extras; their sounds stay host-local like other entity cues.
- **Saves.** Thrown tridents save in the dimension's `tridents` property and
  in per-chunk entity storage; a drowned's hand, item wear and every
  enchantment (Wind Burst at bit 41) round-trip.

## Integration hooks

- `weapons::breeze_rod()` resolves Track C's `breeze_rod` by name: the mace
  recipe and the mace's anvil repair appear when it is registered.
- `weapons::mace::wind_burst_blocks(world, center)` is empty: route it to
  Track C's wind-charge explosion (`block_interaction: trigger`, the
  `#blocks_wind_charge_explosions` immunity, gust particles). The launch and
  mob knockback are done here. `combat::Outcome::Hit.smash.wind_burst`

- The heavy core is obtainable only from Track C's ominous vaults: its
  `heavy_core` Java name resolves for their reward tables.
- Item icons, `Sound::Weapon`/`MobSound::Weapon` and `Enchantment` bit 40
  (Swift Sneak) need the integrator's A–D ordering.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Mob spear use is not ported.** Java 26.3 zombies (1%/5% Hard: iron
  sword, iron spear or iron shovel), husks and piglins (golden spear for a
  tenth of their gold-sword rolls) carry spears and use `SpearUseGoal` /
  `SpearApproach`/`SpearAttack`/`SpearRetreat`. This game's zombies don't
  hold weapons yet and piglin rolls weren't changed (the RNG order would
  shift existing worlds); both stay open.
- No held-item or third-person poses of Java's own: trident and spear
  charges reuse the bow's draw pose; no Riptide spin pose; no trident
  in-hand model in the drowned's hand. The thrown trident is an original
  cuboid model; enchanted tridents brighten instead of a glint.
- The rarity colour of epic items (mace, heavy core) isn't shown (the HUD
  only has the rare colour, used for the trident).
- Wind Burst's block interaction and particles are the Track C hook; the
  attacker's exposure uses the shared explosion ray test. The mace's smash
  knockback and Wind Burst don't push other players (LAN), and maces/spears
  don't hit players through LAN PvP.
- Mob armor still doesn't reduce player hits in this engine (existing gap),
  so Breach only matters against magma cubes and horse armor.
- Looting's `equipment_drop_chance` (+1% a level) isn't applied to the
  drowned's trident drop; drops happen when the death animation ends.
- The impulse context (smash height and grace) isn't saved; a save/reload
  mid-fall measures the fall from its peak.
- The drowned's first throw comes as soon as it engages (Java waits 40
  ticks); its trident attack uses the shared hostile chase rather than
  Java's goal selector, and drowned keep the shared zombie armor roll that
  Java's `Drowned` override skips. Tridents can't be shot by dispensers.
- The heavy core uses the shared stone/metal sound set, not `HEAVY_CORE`'s,
  and isn't washed away by flowing lava (`#washed_away_by_fluids`; the
  copper golem statue has the same gap).
- A player's own trident never hits its thrower, and player tridents hit
  other players only with PvP on (the arrows' rule); Java lets a trident
  hit its owner once it has left the owner's box.
- The owner's eye for a Loyalty return uses 0.9 × the player box height.
