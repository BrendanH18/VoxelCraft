## Scope

The [F7 record](2026-11-28-v1.0-final-f7-physics.md#remaining-differences)'s
remaining differences:

1. A legacy wall waiting for an unloaded neighbour lost its place in the
   wait list when the game saved or its chunk unloaded.
2. Experience orbs, primed TNT and armor stands counted no fall distance,
   so they sank into powder snow at once after a long fall.
3. Minecarts and boats used the empty collision context.
4. Thrown potions, pearls, snowballs, eggs, eyes of ender, fireballs,
   fireworks, wither skulls, shulker bullets, fishing bobbers and evoker
   fangs weren't pushed by pistons.
5. A pushed falling block fell down the column under its centre and kept
   no sideways speed from a slime block.
6. Frames and paintings a piston broke made no sound.

## What changed

### 1. The legacy wall wait list is saved

`World::legacy_walls` is now a chunk-record system (`legacy_walls`, in
`chunk_records::systems` and `CHUNK_KEYS`): a waiting wall whose chunk
unloads goes into that chunk's region record and back into the list when
the chunk loads. The level file holds the loaded chunks' waiting walls
under a new dimension key `legacy_walls` (`x,y,z` joined by `;`, sorted),
written **only while some wall waits**, so every other world's level file
is byte for byte as before (the v0.8 fixture has no walls). When a chunk
loads, `retry_legacy_wall_posts` settles the walls near it as before,
including ones the list read back.

### 2. Fall distance for orbs, TNT and armor stands

`XpOrb`, `PrimedTnt` and armor stands (`Decor`) track
`Entity.fallDistance` (not saved, as for items): falling adds up, landing
resets it (`entity::item::fallen`, now shared with items). Their
collision contexts carry it, so after a fall of more than 2.5 blocks
powder snow's 0.9 shape holds them for the tick they land; landing resets
the count and they sink in on the next tick, as in Java.

### 3. Minecarts and boats

`Minecart::collision_context` (feet, the cart's saved `fall_distance`,
not a walker) for a cart's on-rail and off-rail moves, a boat's
`move_boat` and its water-landing `noCollision`: scaffolding holds them
only from above, powder snow only after a fall of more than 2.5 blocks.
`getGroundFriction` keeps the context-free shape, as Java's
`getCollisionShape(level, pos)` does.

### 4. Piston pushes on the other projectiles

`Entities::push_projectiles` runs `push_body` on pearls, thrown potions,
snowballs, eggs, llama spit, wind charges and bottles o' enchanting,
fishing bobbers, eyes of ender, blaze and ghast fireballs, dragon
fireballs (a fight's and wild dragons'), fireworks, wither skulls,
shulker bullets (`noPhysics`: no collision) and evoker fangs, with each
`EntityType`'s box (0.25; wind charges, small fireballs, skulls and
bullets 0.3125; large and dragon fireballs 1; fangs 0.5 × 0.8) and each
one's velocity units for a slime block's launch. Fangs never move
themselves, so a launch changes nothing.

### 5. Pushed falling blocks

`FallingBlock` gained a sideways speed (not saved) that a slime block's
horizontal push sets. A falling block off the grid, or moving sideways,
now moves as `FallingBlockEntity.tick` does: its 0.98 box through
`physics::move_box_context` with the falling-block context (powder snow
holds it), 0.98 drag a tick on its sideways speed, and on landing the
cell its bottom centre is in. If that cell's floor is free (it landed on
a ledge's edge), it drops as its block's item. A block on the grid with
no sideways speed falls cell by cell as before.

### 6. Break sounds

`Entities::piston_break_decor` queues `MobSound::DecorBroken(kind)`: a
frame's always, a painting's under `doEntityDrops`. The game plays it
through `decor_feedback`'s `Broken` sound, the one a player's break plays.

## Remaining differences

- The sideways speed of a pushed falling block and the fall distance of
  orbs, TNT, armor stands and items aren't saved (Java saves
  `Motion` and `fall_distance`); a save mid-flight loses them.
- A falling block that lands over a free floor drops its item whatever
  `doEntityDrops` says (the world has only `doTileDrops`), and a falling
  block whose landing cell it can't replace is lost, as before, rather
  than dropped.
- Powder snow's `makeStuckInBlock` slowing (0.9, 1.5, 0.9) isn't applied
  to orbs, TNT, carts, boats or armor stands.
- Projectiles are pushed with their Java boxes, but VoxelCraft's own
  flight code still treats most of them as points.
