## Scope

[Track 8g's remaining differences](2026-11-28-v1.0-gap-track-08g-death-messages.md#remaining-differences)
in damage causes, the combat tracker and death messages:

1. Fall locations as Java's `CombatTracker`/`FallLocation` keep them
   (ladders, vines, weeping and twisting vines, scaffolding, other
   climbables, water) and their messages.
2. A removed attacker's custom name kept for the message, as Java's
   `DamageSource` holds its entity (a creeper's own blast), and a tamed
   wolf's owner taking the kill credit.
3. Goat rams, the Ender Dragon's bite and wing knockback, evoker fangs,
   guardian beams and spikes and a ghast fireball's direct hit carrying
   their mob, as Java's damage sources do; a witch's potion keeping its
   thrower when saved mid-flight.

All three are done; what is left is [listed below](#remaining-differences).

## Java sources

The official 26.3 server jar (bundler SHA-1

and client jar, from Mojang's version manifest onto a Boat sandbox

  `assist`/`finish`/`killer`).
- `LivingEntity`: `lastClimbablePos`, set by `onClimbable` (a

- `RamTarget.tick`: the ram is `noAggroMobAttack(goat)` (its knockback's

- `EnderDragon`: `knockBack` (`mobAttack(dragon)`, 5) and `hurt` (the

- `EvokerFangs.dealDamageTo`: `magic()` with no owner, else
  `indirectMagic(fangs, evoker)` (none for an evoker's ally).
- `Guardian.hurtServer`: `thorns(guardian)`; its attack goal's

## What changed

### Fall locations (item 1)

The player keeps Java's `lastClimbablePos` (`Player::last_climbable`): the
cell `onClimbable` last found, forgotten on the step after standing on the
ground (the landing's fall damage, recorded after the move, still sees
it). Each tick the survival inputs read Java's
`getCurrentFallLocation` from it (`Env::fall_location`: the block now in
that cell, as `FallLocation::of_block` names it, else `Water` in water),
and every hit the combat tracker records keeps it with the health the hit
took.

`CombatTracker::death_message` now names where a fall began: a fall on its
own from a ladder or trapdoor is "fell off a ladder", then "fell off some
vines", "fell off some weeping vines", "fell off some twisting vines",
"fell off scaffolding" and "fell while climbing" (cave vines, or a ladder
broken since); a fall away from any is still "fell from a high place".
The hardest hit over five taken while climbing or in water now stands in
for a short fall (`getMostSignificantFall`'s alternative): a zombie's
6-damage hit on a ladder followed by a four-block fall is "was doomed to
fall by Zombie", where it was "hit the ground too hard".

### Departed attackers and the wolf's owner (item 2)

The game remembers each mob leaving the level (killed, blown up, despawned)
for 20 seconds, at most 64 of them (`Entities::depart`), by its custom
name and named weapon; `Entities::attacker` falls back on it. A named
creeper's own blast now reads "was blown up by Boom" after the creeper is
gone; a zombie that hit the player and died since still gets its custom
name in "was doomed to fall by Grumbles". A departed mob is no longer
alive, so it gives no kill credit (`baseTick` forgets a dead
`lastHurtByMob`), as before.

A tamed wolf's bite carries its owner (`Cause::credit`), whom the player's
kill credit remembers (`lastHurtByPlayer`): a player bitten by Alex's wolf
who then drowns "drowned while trying to escape Alex".

### Mobs behind their hits (item 3)

These hits now carry their mob (`Cause::by`), so the message names it by
its custom name, a fall it started names it ("was doomed to fall by Goat")
and, except the ram, it gives the kill credit:

- the goat's ram, now typed `mob_attack_no_aggro` as Java's
  `noAggroMobAttack` (no kill credit: `#no_anger`);
- the Ender Dragon's bite and wing knockback, and its breath
  (`indirectMagic(cloud, dragon)`): the dragon isn't a mob, so a reserved
  uid (`dragon::DRAGON_UID`) names it "Ender Dragon", alive while a dragon
  is;
- evoker fangs, while their evoker is in the level; ownerless fangs are
  Java's plain `magic` ("was killed by magic", which takes the kill credit
  variant);
- the guardian's beam (both hits, an elder's too) and its spikes;
- a ghast fireball's direct hit, which VoxelCraft lacked: 6 damage,
  `fireball(fireball, ghast)` ("was fireballed by Ghast", or the player
  who punched it back), before the blast, which the hurt cooldown then
  lets in only by its excess; with no owner, `unattributed_fireball`
  ("burned to death"). The hit is the ghast's (`OwnerHurt`), as the
  blaze's already was.

A thrown witch's potion saves its witch (`thrower`, written only when there
is one) with the level file's other projectiles, so after a reload it
still names her ("was killed by Witch using magic").

### Old saves

`Cause::saved` looks a text up in `Cause::RETIRED` first: the ghast
fireball's direct hit uses the words its blast had before Track 8g ("was
fireballed by a ghast"), and an old save's raw text still reads back as
the blast ("was blown up by Ghast"). New deaths are saved in Java's words
("was fireballed by Ghast"). No other saved text changes; the v0.8 fixture
resaves byte for byte.

## Remaining differences

- A `death.fell.accident.water` message (a fall's damage taken in water,
  which in practice never happens: water breaks the fall) shows Java's
  bare key after the player's name; Java's chat shows the key alone.
- The fall location is read from the survival tick before the hit, not at
  the hit: a hit landing in the tick after the player stood on the ground
  (before the next movement step clears the cell) still has the climbable
  location, where Java's has none; Java's `onClimbable` also runs before
  the move, VoxelCraft's after it.
- A departed mob is remembered for 20 seconds (at most 64); Java's damage
  source holds it as long as the combat tracker keeps the hit (at most 15
  seconds after the last). Mobs unloaded with their chunk aren't
  remembered (Java's can't be looked up either).
- Several dragons in one dimension share the reserved uid: any living one
  keeps the kill credit alive. Dragons can't be given custom names in
  VoxelCraft, so all are "Ender Dragon".
- From Track 8g, still open: Java's attacker-first `.player` variants of
  `sulfurCubeHot`; a pearl in another dimension waits for the host there;
  the credits' chunks stay loaded; the villager's "no" is original audio
  nobody has listened to.
