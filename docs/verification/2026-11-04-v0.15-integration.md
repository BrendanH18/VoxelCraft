## Integrator checks of the agents' work

Each track's key constants were compared against the pinned 26.3 bytecode
and data before merging. These matched: CropBlock growth and crowding,
beetroot's 2/3 tick gate and divided bone meal, stem bone meal and the
`supports_stem_fruit` tag, melon loot, LightningBolt copper cleaning and
rod powering, thunder strike odds and targeting, skeleton trap chance,
arrow `POTION_DURATION_SCALE`, PhantomSpawner, bat spawning, bee nest
chances, CatSpawner (1200 ticks, 8+rand(24) offsets, five occupied beds,
fewer than five cats in 48×8×48, none in 16×8×16 at huts), FrogspawnBlock
(3600–11999 ticks, 2–5 tadpoles), armadillo scutes (6000 + rand(6000)),
ocelot trust (1/3) and spawning (2/3, sea level, grass or leaves) and panda
gene weights and 1/32 mutation.

Fixed while merging:

- Struck waxed lightning rods kept their oxidation but lost their wax, and
  lightning's cleaning walk de-oxidised waxed copper. Both now match Java.
- Phantoms used full skylight for `canSeeSky`, so they spawned under glass;
  they now use the WORLD_SURFACE heightmap.
- Lightning swaps a mooshroom's colour once per bolt with no damage
  (`MushroomCow.thunderHit`, an unsaved last-bolt id).
- Item icons follow the item list order, so track 3's icons come before the
  five end icons of tracks 2 and 4.

## Council review, round 1

Three reviewers from different providers reviewed the merged branch at
0ab37cd: Claude Opus 5.5 (8/10), GPT-6.1 Sol (7/10) and DeepSeek Flash
(7/10). Fixed in 2c9bade, e8f3757 and 935dc80, each with a test:

- Lingering clouds were dropped on reload (`potion_clouds` was not a
  dimension key). A test now checks every entity save key.
- Instant Damage from clouds and tipped arrows was absorbed by wolf armor;
  magic bypasses it in Java.
- Ocelots took fall damage; they are in `fall_damage_immune`.
- Player arrows didn't anger bees.
- Skeleton→stray and mooshroom→cow conversions kept damaged health; Java
  makes a new entity at full health.
- Natural nest bees stored a full mob record each, in memory and in every
  save, for every nest ever loaded. They are now a null record until they
  leave.
- Every skeleton was saved; only freezing ones are now.
- A zombie riding a zombie horse could despawn.
- Doc corrections: the waxed mirror is 840 states; the kind order note.

Declined: adding wither skeletons to `burns_in_sun` (they are fire-immune,
so Java shows no sun damage or flames), and refactoring bounded per-tick
index rebuilds in the new mob upkeep without a measured regression.

  `cargo test --release`: 2132 passed, 0 failed, 19 ignored.
- `--no-default-features` clippy and tests: 1930 passed, 0 failed, 18 ignored.

  0ab37cd, three alternating runs each: single-thread meshing 76.5–79.6 ms
  (main) and 76.9–80.0 ms (merge), within noise; rd 8 streaming 0.93–0.99 s
  and 0.74–0.92 s. The merged world has 9 more quads (426908).

  through chunk unload, conversions with identity and equipment, all waxed
  states, every crop state, phantom size and membranes, clouds). A
  GPT-6.1 Terra hands-on run relaunched a scripted world with a bee nest,
  copper and a lightning rod, froglights, a panda, armadillo, frog,
  mooshroom and skeleton horse; all were present after the restart.
