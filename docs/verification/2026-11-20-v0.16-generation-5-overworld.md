## What is built

**Poplar wood family** (`world::wood::poplar`, states 19584–19983). Log,
wood, stripped log and stripped wood on three axes; planks; orange, red and
yellow leaves (waterloggable); the sapling at stage 0 and 1 and the potted
sapling; fence (waterloggable), fence gate, door (left and right hinge,
upper half); stairs and slab as a `slab_stair_states` family of their own
(every Java state); trapdoor, button and pressure plate as `wood::switches`
family 8 (switch wood 12, mirroring oak like the other woods); standing,
wall, hanging and wall hanging signs and the shelf as `wood::Kind::Poplar`
(same layouts as the other kinds, in poplar's range); the boat and chest
boat (items 1705 and 1706, entity codes 40 and 41). Hardness, tools, fire
and lava odds, drops, fuel, compost, explosion resistance and decay,
recipes (planks from `#poplar_logs`, wood, stripped wood, stairs, slab,
fence, gate, door, trapdoor, button, plate, signs, hanging signs, shelf,
boats) and the `#planks`, `#wooden_slabs` and `#logs_that_burn` tags follow
Java. Axes strip poplar logs and wood. Leaves drop saplings (1/20 with
Fortune steps) and sticks; the generic leaf decay applies.

**Poplar trees** (`world::poplar_trees`). `PoplarTrunkPlacer(7, 4, 0)` with
four blocks of trunk above one to four sideways branches,
`PoplarFoliagePlacer` (radius 5/6/7/8 weighted 5:5:1:1, height 5..=6,
side-hole chance 0.15, the rhombus rows and the arm logs of the widest
row), `ShelfMushroomDecorator(0.4)`, the `_leaf_litter` variants' two
ground decorators, and `fallen_poplar_tree` (stump, a log of two to five
lying two or three blocks away, brown mushrooms on 10% of the logs and
`ShelfMushroomDecorator(0.8)` on its sides). A poplar sapling grows a red,
orange or yellow poplar with equal odds (`TreeGrower.POPLAR`).

**Dappled forest** (generation 5). `biome::pick_in` gives Java's
`MIDDLE_BIOMES_VARIANT[1][0]` (dappled forest) where generations 1–4 fell
back to plains; every plains column of temperature band 1, humidity band 0
and non-negative weirdness comes from that slot, so the swap is exact and
older generations are untouched. Trees: `trees_dappled_forest`'s selector
(red 200, orange 240, yellow 90 leaf-litter poplars, spruce 27, fallen
poplar 120) at six attempts a chunk; red shrub patches, brown mushrooms
and forest grass. Spawns: Java's creature, ambient and glow squid lists;
monsters are the default list (as Java's).

**Cinnabar and sulfur** (`world::gen5_blocks`, states 16000–16099 and
stairs 20000–20479). Cinnabar, polished cinnabar, cinnabar bricks and
chiseled cinnabar, the same four of sulfur, stairs, slabs and walls of the
six non-chiseled blocks (every Java state), potent sulfur (its five
states), sulfur spikes (thickness, direction and water: 20 states),
nether gold ore. Crafting (polished, bricks, chiseled, shapes, potent
sulfur from nine sulfur, sulfur from four spikes) and every stonecutting
recipe Java has for them. Spikes place pointing away from the nearest
vertical look direction (or the other way when only that one holds), stand
on a sturdy face or a spike and break when their support goes.

**Sulfur caves** (generation 5, `terrain::sulfur_caves`). `biome::cave_in`
adds Java's sulfur caves box (continentalness −0.19..0.55, erosion from
0.45, weirdness −1.1..−0.85, 20+ blocks down); lush caves keep precedence.
The stone there turns to cinnabar and sulfur strata by
`sulfur_cave_bands`' thresholds on an original 3D noise; spikes grow up
from floors and down from ceilings on a sulfur layer (one to four long,
base/middle/frustum/tip); small sulfur pools of water over a sulfur bed
with wet potent sulfur under their middle. Monster spawns follow Java's
list (the sulfur cube entry is skipped while the mob is missing).

**Plants.** Red shrub (replaceable, burns 60/100, compost 30%), golden
dandelion and its pot (crafted from a dandelion and eight gold nuggets;
yellow dye and suspicious stew of Saturation; burns 60/100; piglins love
it). Used on a baby animal outside `#cannot_be_age_locked` it toggles
Java's age lock (`AgeableMob.mobInteract`): the baby goes back to its
youngest age, stops growing while locked, is kept from despawning, and the
flower is used up; the lock is saved with the mob. Shelf mushroom (four
facings, two ages; placed on a sturdy side face, breaks with its support,
a grown one drops two; entities bounce off it with Java's 0.75
restitution; compost 65%; rabbit stew recipe). The wandering trader's poplar log and
sapling, golden dandelion, shelf mushroom and sulfur spike offers now
resolve.

**Nether gold ore** (generation 5). Placed in Nether netherrack at heights
10..=117, scaled from quartz's density to Java's ten veins of ten a chunk
(twenty in basalt deltas); drops two to six gold nuggets with Java's ore
bonus (silk touch keeps the ore); smelts and blasts to a gold ingot;
breaking it angers nearby piglins (`#guarded_by_piglins`).

**The sulfur cube** (`entity::sulfur_cube`, `entity::mob::sulfur_cube`;
`MobKind::SulfurCube`). A monster-category ageable cube of 0.49 × size
(eye 0.175), not fire immune and allowed in Peaceful. Adults are size 2
with 8 health, babies size 1 with 4; natural spawns are adults, and after
a pack's first member one in twenty is a baby (`AgeableMobGroupData`). It
spawns from the sulfur caves' monster entry (weight 100, packs of 2–4) on
the ground with no light check (`checkSulfurCubeSpawnRules`). Without a
block it hops like a slime at `0.2 + 0.1 × size` and re-picks a heading
every 40–99 ticks; babies follow players holding slime balls and adults
players holding a swallowable block within 8 blocks, and adults whose
pickup timer is out hop toward swallowable items within 8 blocks, both
with a third of the hop delay. It never attacks.

An adult takes a swallowable block from the hand (or the ground, under
`mobGriefing`) into its body slot (a guaranteed drop), spitting out a
different block it held; the same block is refused. The block's
`sulfur_cube_archetype` (regular, bouncy, slow bouncy, slow and fast flat,
light, fast and slow sliding, high resistance, sticky, explosive, hot;
every item list and modifier from the data) then sets the cube's
knockback and explosion knockback resistance, bounciness, friction and
air drag, and its goals stop: it moves one Java tick at a time under
`travelInAir`/`travelInFluid` with the modified friction and drag, bounces
off walls, floors and ceilings with `restituteMovementAfterCollisions`,
never steps up, and buoyant blocks bob up in water and lava. A player's
hit is no damage but Java's angled knockback (the archetype's powers turned
by the attacker's aim, tipped by the hit's height and the height
difference, times √damage and `1 − knockback resistance`); mob hits,
arrows, explosions and the rest of `#sulfur_cube_with_block_immune_to`
give knockback only. Players walking into it push it along their speed
(`playerPush`: within 1.3 blocks, `2 × speed × 0.3`, at most 0.5, lifted
on the ground), with the archetype's push sound and cooldown. A hot cube
(magma) burns players it touches and mobs it bumps for 1
(`sulfur_cube_hot`, the floor-is-lava death message). An explosive cube
(TNT) is lit by flint and steel or a fire charge, by burning, by redstone
power where it stands, or by an explosion (a short `nextInt(30) + 15`
fuse); lit, it can't be hurt and after 120 ticks explodes with power 3
(under `tnt_explodes`; with that rule off, lighting it fails) and leaves
no babies. Shears take the block out (100 ticks before it takes another).
Babies eat slime balls (`ageUp` by a tenth of what is left), grow up into
adults of size 2 and can be age locked with a golden dandelion. A dead
adult splits into two babies; it drops its block and 1–2 experience
(babies none); its loot table is empty.

**Sulfur cube bucket** (item 1707, `sulfur_cube_bucket`, stacks to 1). An
empty bucket used on an adult scoops it up with its block, health and age
lock; used on a block face, or from a dispenser, it lets the cube out
(`from_bucket`, persistent) and leaves an empty bucket. Natural sulfur
cubes are saved with the level (size, block, fuse, pickup timer,
`from_bucket`). The LAN look carries the block (equipment), fuse and size.
The model is an original translucent sulfur-yellow shell around a
mustard core with a face, or around the held block drawn with its
texture; a lit cube flashes white. Sounds are original synth stand-ins
(hurt, death, squish, bounce, jump, absorb, eject, eat, hit, push, bucket
fill and empty) and landing throws yellow goo.

**Abandoned camps** (generation 5, `temples::camp_templates`,
`temples::Kind::AbandonedCamp`). Java's `abandoned_camp` set (random
spread 37/8, salt 91231127) of eighteen structures, one per biome: a start
whose biome takes a camp builds that biome's camp from
`abandoned_camp/tent/<biome>` at the start chunk's corner, projected to the
surface, size 2, 80 blocks out, and keeps it only where the start piece's
centre is in that biome. Each tent's `connector` takes a campsite from
`abandoned_camp/camp/<biome>` (the default chest, barrel and special
campsites weighted 15 each against the biome's own campsite's 4, as
Java's 15 + 15 + 15 shared and 4 own elements). The camps add
`beard_thin` terms to generation 5 terrain like outposts. The catalogue is
original and generated (FE-02): per biome three tents (8 or 6 wide, wool
stairs on fence poles of the biome's wood, a straw bed, a barrel or a
common chest) and a fourth beside a tree where Java's tents have trees; the
default campsites (10 × 3 × 10) ring a campfire with a common chest, a
barrel, or the special campsite's oxidized copper chest of
`chests/abandoned_camp_secret_chest` beside a common chest and a lantern;
each biome campsite (10 × 10 × 10) has the biome's ground, a log seat, a
barrel and trees. Trees stand where templates mark one of the 21
`abandoned_camp/trees/*` pools and grow an original tree of that species
(oak, fancy oak, birch, super birch, spruce, pine, mega pine, thick spruce,
jungle, acacia, cherry, pale oak, three poplars, bamboo). Containers fill
from Java's three tables. `/locate structure abandoned_camp` (and any
`abandoned_camp_<biome>` name) finds them.

**Abandoned camp map** (item 1708, `abandoned_camp_map`, layers 796–797).
Loot rolls now see the container's biome: `location_check` (and
`inverted`) entry conditions are kept when they hold, so a common camp
chest's map pool offers the seven camp kinds other than the one it stands
in. The map is named by its `filled_map.*` key (e.g. "Swamp Camp Map"),
searches 50 rings of camp regions for a generated camp of its biome with
`skip_existing_chunks` (the references are saved with the maps), and marks
it with an original tent decoration. The secret chest's five v0.15
explorer maps and the trial chambers map now have their source.

**Shelf mushrooms** (second slice): landing on one counts half the fall
(`fallDistanceReduction(0.5)`), and bone meal grows a young one to age 1.

**Sulfur stalactites** (second slice): a downward sulfur spike run that
loses its support falls like pointed dripstone (`SpeleothemBlock`), its
tip hurting what it lands on, and breaks into spikes where it lands.

## Differences from Java

- Random draws, the band noise and feature counts per 16³ chunk are the
  engine's (FE-01); features stay inside their chunk cube.
- Dappled forest grass and leaves use the dry foliage tint (the engine has
  five fixed foliage groups); Java's are orange.
- Sulfur pools are 3×3 basins, not Java's lake blob; rooted sulfur springs
  (`rooted_sulfur_spring`, Mojang spring templates) are not generated.
- Spikes stand only on a sturdy face or a spike; they don't grow or hurt
  what lands on them, and thickness is set at generation, not updated by
  neighbours (unsupported stalactites fall, second slice).
- Potent sulfur keeps the state it was generated or placed in: no nausea
  gas, geysers, countdowns or bubbles yet.
- Only players get the shelf mushroom's fall reduction (mobs take no
  fall damage here except mounts).
- The golden dandelion's age lock has no 40-tick cooldown, sound or
  particles, and covers the mobs with animal state (tadpoles, which Java
  also locks, are not covered).
- Poplar leaves make no falling-leaf particles or ambient sound.
- Nether gold ore uses hashed cells scaled from quartz, not Java's ore
  veins.

Sulfur cube:

- Its goals are evaluated each update from the same inputs, not Java's
  goal selector; the hop itself is the engine's shared cube hop (jump
  0.42 a tick, 10–29 ticks between hops). The float goal jumps on 80% of
  updates in liquid.
- Only a player's melee hit uses Java's angled knockback; a mob's hit, a
  projectile and a sweep push it the ordinary way (Java angles those by
  the attacker's aim too), and the player's extra knockback (Knockback,
  sprinting) is not added on top.
- One hit and one push sound stand in for each archetype's own; a few
  sound events (small-cube variants) share the adult's. Archetypes don't
  change the cube's look apart from the block inside.
- Player pushes read the player's speed from the last update's positions
  (`getKnownSpeed`); vehicles use the passenger scale but carts and boats
  aren't pushed themselves.
- Contact damage to mobs needs box overlap (Java's `doPush`) and skips
  mobs still in their hurt flash.
- The `suppresses_bounce` block tag and sneaking players' bounce
  suppression are not modelled; block speed factors (soul sand, honey)
  don't slow the ball.
- `SulfurCubeBlockDispenseItemBehavior` (a dispenser putting a block into
  a cube) is not built; dispensers do let cubes out of buckets.
- The bucket's payload packs health to hundredths and the block's item id
  into `entity_data`; a custom name rides in the stack name. Shift-click
  and creative-only data components are not modelled.
- Leads attach as for any mob (Java only leashes a cube holding a block).
- The release plays the shared bucket splash as well as the cube's own sound.

Abandoned camps and the map:

- The catalogue is reduced (FE-02): 3–4 tents per biome instead of 10, and
  one template for each of Java's default campsite kinds and biome
  campsites, with the kinds' weights kept. Tree pools are markers in the
  templates (not connectors) and grow original shapes; bee-nest variants
  grow the same tree without a nest.
- Java places cushion entities at campsites; none are placed.
- The camp kind is chosen by the biome at an unturned tent's centre at the
  start chunk corner, then checked at the placed start piece's centre (Java
  tries each of the eighteen structures in a seeded order).
- The map's name is a custom stack name; Java sets `item_name`.
- A camp map rolled by a worker thread is not re-searched against existing
  references when it is numbered (the other explorer maps are).
