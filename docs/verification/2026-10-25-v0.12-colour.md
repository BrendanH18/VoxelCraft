## Design

`src/world/colour_blocks.rs` owns the range, with `banner.rs`,
`candle.rs`, `straw_bed.rs`, `cushion.rs` and `world.rs` beside it; shared
files carry only hook lines.

- **Stairs and slabs** join `slab_stair_states::FAMILIES` as families
  57–88 after stone's 39–52 and copper's 53–56, so placement, corner shapes, slab merging,
  waterlogging, light faces and neighbour reshaping come from the existing
  machinery. 85 families no longer fit `PACKED`'s six family bits, so the
  in-memory packing widened to seven (properties moved up a bit); no saved
  ID changed. Wool stairs and slabs burn like wool; concrete ones mine like
  concrete. Three wool now make six wool slabs, and carpet takes two wool,
  as in 26.3. Concrete's 32 stonecutter recipes are data
  (`crafting::colour::stonecutting`) until the stonecutter screen (v0.17).
- **Candles** hold one to four candles, place onto one of their own kind,
  light with flint and steel, a fire charge or a Flame arrow, go out by a
  bare-handed click or on taking water, give 3 light per lit candle, and
  drop one candle per candle. Dyed candles come from a plain candle and a
  dye. A candle on an uneaten cake makes a candle cake: lit the same way,
  put out by clicking its candle, eaten like a cake (dropping its candle),
  and picked as a cake.
- **Banners** stand in 16 rotations facing the player or hang on walls
  (`StandingAndWallBlockItem` order), need a solid support, have no
  collision, Java's selection box, mine fastest with an axe, and draw as
  models outside the chunk meshes (Java's `BannerRenderer` at two-thirds
  scale, within 64 blocks) with original procedural cloth.
- **The straw bed** places both halves, breaks as a pair, drops from the
  head, and sleeps with `straw_bed_rule`: no respawn point, and it falls
  apart once its sleeper leaves; outside the Overworld it falls apart
  instead of exploding.
- **Cushions** are a `CartKind` on the existing vehicle system (saved with
  carts as kind 24 + colour). The item goes only on a top face, centred,
  at the click height (`CushionItem.useOn`), facing the snapped look
  direction, if `canBePlacedAt` holds and no other cushion is there; a
  cushion placed into fire or lava burns at once. Players sit with their
  feet 0.35 below its top; sneaking passes over it. It never moves and
  can't be pushed; any hit breaks it (players who can't build can't), as
  does an explosion; every 101st tick it checks its anchor
  and burns in fire or lava. Creative breaking drops nothing. It sits,
  gets up and is placed with the wool sounds Java's cushion sounds share.
  The model is Java's one 16 × 4 × 16 box in the dye colour.

## Differences from Java and not done

- **Candles have no survival source yet.** The plain candle's recipe is
  string over honeycomb, and honeycomb arrives with bees in v0.15; dyed
  candles and candle cakes need a plain candle. They are complete and in
  the creative palette; the recipe is left out until honeycomb exists.
- Banner patterns, the loom and shields with banners wait for v0.17.
- A splash water bottle doesn't put out candles, and explosions don't
  either (`AbstractCandleBlock.onExplosionHit`); fire charges from
  dispensers and arrows set alight by passing through fire or lava don't
  light them (only Flame arrows do).
- Cushions: no breaking particles; lightning doesn't strike yet (ENV-03,
  v0.15), so `thunderHit` has nothing to do. `CushionItem`'s re-aim
  against the collision shape of cauldrons, hoppers and composters needs
  no code: the composter's selection shape is already its hollow
  collision shape, and cauldrons don't exist yet. Our hopper's shape has
  no inner floor at y 10–11, so a cushion clicked into a hopper sits on
  its funnel (an existing hopper-shape gap, not this track's).
- Cushions draw with the fixed light every vehicle model uses
  (`entity::model::build_minecarts`), not the light where they sit, so
  they don't darken at night.
- The straw bed's loose straw strands are drawn into its texture, not
  modelled.
- The sounds reuse VoxelCraft's original wool, cake and fuse sounds; there
  are no candle- or cushion-specific sound files.
- Arrows pass through cushions, as they pass through boats and minecarts:
  no projectile hits any vehicle yet (a shared entity gap, not this
  track's). Java's arrows break cushions and hurt boats and minecarts.
- Review fixes (2026-10-26): LAN clients index and draw banners from
  snapshots and the host's edits; host, CLI and controller players stand
  up beside a straw bed when they wake (before it falls apart); Flame
  arrows hit candles by Java's collision box, so two candles light when
  shot between their bodies; each split-screen view draws the banners
  near its own camera; cushions and boats no longer power detector rails.
- Not played by hand: owner play is the acceptance evidence.
