# v0.13 giant trees and biome features (GEN-03, 2026-10-30)

[Documentation index](../README.md) · [Roadmap v0.13](../roadmap.md#v013-scope-terrain) · [Overworld terrain](../overworld-terrain.md#surface-and-features)

Track 4 of v0.13. Implemented and covered by automated tests; not played by
the owner. Everything that changes generated output applies to
`Generation::V2` only: the generation-1 tree shapes and the golden hash test
are unchanged.

- Requirement: GEN-03.
- Reference: the behaviour of Java 26.3 as publicly described, plus the
  official-server fixtures already in `tests/fixtures/java/26.3`
  (`block_loot.json`, `block_mining.json`) for drops and hardness. No Mojang
  code, data or assets were used: shapes, textures and placement rules are
  original. **Placement densities and which biome gets which plant are tuned
  by hand from public descriptions, not read from Java's placed-feature data**;
  they are the main unverified item.

## What changed

**Giant trees (V2).** `trees::mega_spruce_v2` and `trees::mega_jungle_v2`
replace `mega_spruce` and `mega_jungle` for generation-2 worlds and for 2x2
saplings.

- Giant spruce and pine (old growth taigas): a 2x2 trunk 13–26 blocks tall
  under a cone of leaf rings that starts as the trunk's own 2x2 tip, widens
  to about 10 blocks and pulls in on every third ring, with a ragged rim; the
  trunk is bare near the ground; a pine keeps only a tuft at the top. The
  ground within about 3.5 blocks of the trunk centre turns to podzol with an
  irregular edge and one block in eight of coarse dirt
  (`trees::podzol_disc`). Mossy cobblestone boulders (`trees::mossy_boulder`)
  of radius 1–2 dot old growth taigas, rarely plain taigas.
- Giant jungle tree: a 2x2 trunk from the first block over the ground (the
  generation-1 shape started its logs one block inside the soil), a dome of
  jungle leaves with two leafy side branches, vines climbing runs of the
  trunk's eight outside faces and cocoa pods of random age on the lower trunk.
  The existing hanging vines under leaves still apply.
- 2x2 saplings (`World::grow_tree`): four spruce saplings grow a giant spruce
  or pine (one in two) with its podzol, four jungle saplings a giant jungle
  tree; dark oak and pale oak already needed a 2x2. Grass under any 2x2 trunk
  turns to dirt. Three saplings, or one, grow the ordinary tree. Growth is
  gameplay, so it is not generation-gated.

**New blocks** (`world::biome_plants`, ids 9984–10009 of the reserved
9984–10111, texture layers 2832–2845 of 2832–2863, no new item ids):

| Block | Ids | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| leaf litter | 9984–9987 | 1–4 segments; one-sixteenth carpet like moss carpet; breaking drops one item per segment; stacks by placing more on it |
| wildflowers | 10000–10003 | 1–4 flowers; same carpet rules; bone meal adds a flower, or drops one when full |
| bush | 10004 | cross plant on dirt-like soil |
| firefly bush | 10005 | as a bush; light level 2 |
| short dry grass | 10006 | on sand, terracotta and soil; shears or silk touch only; bone meal makes it tall |
| tall dry grass | 10007, 10008 | lower and upper half through the shared double-plant rules; shears or silk touch only |
| cactus flower | 10009 | on a cactus or any opaque block |

Hardness is 0 for all of them and they burn (Java odds 60/100). Drops and
hardness match the Java 26.3 fixtures (leaf litter and wildflowers: `n` items
for `n` segments; short and tall dry grass: nothing without shears or silk
touch; the others drop themselves). Java's `facing` property of leaf litter
and wildflowers is not stored: both look the same from every side in the
carpet model, so it would only spend ids. Creative palette, item forms
(`Item::from_block`), names, textures and save/reload are covered.

**Biome features (V2)** (`world::biome_features`, a child module of
`terrain`): mossy boulders and fallen logs from hashed cells; floor plants
column by column after the existing plants. The fallen log is three to five
logs of the forest's wood lying along X or Z, placed only on level ground
(every covered column has the origin's height and biome). Cacti get a flower
on top one time in four (`trees::cactus_v2`).

## Tests

- `world::trees`: giant spruce and jungle trunks are 2x2 columns from the
  first block above the ground to their top, with no log below ground and
  inside the shape bounds; the spruce crown is a cone over a bare trunk; the
  jungle tree carries vines against its logs and cocoa on logs; podzol disc
  covers the trunk ground and holds podzol and coarse dirt; boulders, fallen
  logs (horizontal axis) and cactus flowers (always on the top cactus block).
- `world::biome_features`: boulders and logs are deterministic and equal in
  overlapping windows cut through a border (so continuous across chunks);
  generation 1 never produces the new blocks; an old growth taiga holds
  giant 2x2 spruces, podzol and boulders; desert, dark forest, meadow and
  swamp hold their plants.
- `world::growth`: four spruce, jungle or dark oak saplings grow 2x2 trunks;
  three saplings grow a plain tree; podzol around a sapling-grown giant
  spruce.
- `world::biome_plants`: registry, names, stacking, drop counts and shears
  rules, placement rules, bone meal, double-plant halves, and every state
  through the chunk save and reload.
- Unchanged and passing: `generated_chunk_hashes_stay_identical`.

## Shared tables that now include the new blocks

The Java 26.3 fixtures cover these blocks and caught three mismatches that
were fixed: the bush drops nothing without shears or silk touch (as the dry
grasses do), silk touch keeps the dry grasses, and leaf litter and wildflowers
decay per item in explosions. Also registered: fuel (leaf litter and both dry
grasses burn 100 ticks), composting (all seven items, 30%), the smelting of
any leaves into leaf litter, and the dyes (cactus flower makes pink, wildflowers
yellow). The block-loot and mining fixture tests map the tall dry grass top to
`tall_dry_grass`, and the loot test reads `segment_amount` and
`flower_amount` from the state.

## Checks

On the finished tree: `cargo fmt`, `cargo clippy --release --all-targets -- -D
warnings` and `cargo test --release --no-fail-fast` pass (1,809 tests, 13
ignored; the ignored count includes the timing test below).
`generated_chunk_hashes_stay_identical` passes unmodified.

Chunk generation time, 2,304 surface chunks of one seed
(`cargo test --release --lib -- --ignored --nocapture
generation_two_features_cost`), after a warm-up pass: generation 1 1.082 ms
per chunk, generation 2 1.063 ms (noise-level difference; the new passes do
a few hashed-cell lookups and one hash per surface column).

## Not verified

- Densities and the biome assignment of the new plants against Java's data
  (see Reference above); the owner's play will show whether they look right.
- Features on the density terrain (another v0.13 track): trees and my
  features anchor to `Generator::column(..).height`, as trees do. If the new
  terrain makes the surface differ from that height, the integrator must
  anchor to the real surface in `place_trees`, `place_ground_features` and
  `place_biome_plants`.
- Hands-on play: placing and stacking leaf litter and wildflowers in the
  game's UI, the look of the textures and of a giant tree, in a screenshot.
- The floor plants whose ground is the top cell of the chunk below (surface
  at y = 31 mod 32, such as every sea-level shore) take the column's untouched
  surface as the soil without reading it, so a cave or a tree's podzol disc
  exactly there can leave one plant on other soil. The older `place_plants`
  skips those columns altogether.
- `cargo clippy` and `cargo test` with `--no-default-features`, and the
  headless `--bench` run.
