## What changed

### Every mob's head and body (`src/entity/mob/head.rs`)

- **Java's `yHeadRot` and `xRot` for every mob** (`Mob::look`, Track 2b's
  spear-only look made general and never saved). The game's eased
  `head_target` and its random idle head turns are gone; every writer of
  them now asks the look control as its Java goal does: attack goals and
  the iron golem's, polar bear's, ocelot's, illagers', witch's and
  llama's `setLookAt(target, 30, 30)`, the blaze's (10, 10), the
  guardian's (90, 90), `BreedGoal` and `FollowOwnerGoal` (10, head pitch
  limit), `TemptGoal` (head yaw limit + 20, pitch limit), the Wither's
  main head at its target (30, 30), and the brain sinks at the mob's own
  speeds. Goals that don't look in Java no longer do (the piglin fetching
  an item, the strider's lava goal, a caravan llama, a leashed mob).
- **`LookControl.tick`** every tick after the move control, by kind: a
  request turns the head by its speed for two ticks, the pitch from level
  (`resetXRotOnTick`; not for a frog with a tongue target) by its limit;
  otherwise the head turns back to the body by 10°; while a path runs it
  stays within `getMaxHeadYRot` of the body. Java's per-kind limits:
  `getHeadRotSpeed` breeze 25, frog 35; `getMaxHeadXRot` shulker and
  guardians 180, slimes, magma and sulfur cubes 0, a sitting wolf 20,
  axolotl and dolphin 1; `getMaxHeadYRot` shulker 180, ravager 45, breeze
  and camels 30, goat 15, frog 5, axolotl and dolphin 1, sniffer 50,
  armadillo 32 (0 rolled up). `SmoothSwimmingLookControl` (dolphins,
  tadpoles, nautiluses 10°; axolotls 20°, not while playing dead) aims 20°
  and 10° past the point, holds the head with the last request's speed,
  levels the pitch 5° a tick when navigation is done, and swings the body
  4° a tick toward a head more than its limit off. The fox's look control
  rests while it sleeps, the bee's while angry, the creaking's while it
  can't move; a mount a player steers has head and body on the rider's
  heading (`tickRidden`). Shulkers, bats, phantoms, ghasts, happy ghasts
  and wardens keep their own head code.
- **`BodyRotationControl`** after the move: the drawn body is now
  `Mob::body_yaw` (Java's `yBodyRot`), apart from `Mob::yaw` (Java's
  `yRot`, the heading every movement code turns and follows), and
  `pose_values` hands the body to the models (field names unchanged for
  Track 7b's models). Moving, the body takes the heading and the head
  stays within the limit; still, a head that moved more than 15° since it
  settled pulls the body within the limit of it, and after ten still ticks
  the body turns to face it over ten more. A mob carrying a mob passenger
  doesn't turn while still. The armadillo's body holds while rolled up,
  the creaking's while it can't move; camels keep Track 3b's
  `CamelBodyRotationControl`, now on Java's head angle. The move control
  turns the heading 90° a tick (`rotlerp(yRot, wanted, 90)`) where the
  game used 8 radians a second for travel without a path, which also turns
  a charging spear holder at Java's rate. LAN hosts send the drawn body.
- **Idle look goals** in each kind's `registerGoals` priorities, started on
  the selector's full update (every other tick): `LookAtPlayerGoal`
  (`nextFloat() < 0.02`, or 1 for the illagers', vex's and pillager's
  close ones; the nearest player within the range scaled by
  `getVisibilityPercent`, in sight and not riding the mob; `Mob.class`,
  `Strider.class` and `Guardian.class` from the sensed mobs within the
  range and 3 blocks up or down; held for `adjustedTickDelay(40 +
  nextInt(40))` full updates while the target lives and stays in range),
  `InteractGoal` and `LookAtTradingPlayerGoal` for the wandering trader,
  and `RandomLookAroundGoal` (2% a full update, a random direction for 20
  to 39 ticks, ticked every tick). Flags as Java's: the random look and
  `InteractGoal` need MOVE free, so they wait while the mob strolls,
  panics, chases or a table goal moves it; attack, breed, tempt and
  follow-owner goals and any behaviour that asked the look control this
  tick hold LOOK, stopping a running idle look; a mob steered by a mob
  passenger loses MOVE and LOOK (`updateControlFlags`), so a strider
  jockey's strider stops its look goals while its rider keeps its own.
  The dolphin's flagged selector (Track 3) already held its look goals;
  they now ask the look control too.
- **Brain looks**: `LookAtTargetSink(45, 90)` starts on the tick after
  `LOOK_TARGET` appears, turns the head toward it at the mob's speeds
  while it stays visible, for 46 to 91 ticks, then erases it. Writers by
  activity: villagers' full look behaviour (idle, meet, play: a cat at
  weight 8, a villager or player 2, a creature, water creature, axolotl,
  underground water creature, water ambient or monster 1, `DoNothing(30,
  60)` 2) and minimal (work, rest, panic, hiding, raids: villager or
  player 2, `DoNothing` 8), shuffled as `ShufflingList` does, and
  `LookAndFollowTradingPlayerSink` while trading within 4 blocks; piglins'
  idle (a player holding a loved item within 14, then the `RunOne` of a
  player, piglin or anything within 8 or `DoNothing(30, 60)`), avoid,
  celebrate and ride activities (the riding baby piglin: the loved-item
  player within 8, then `triggerOneShuffled` over a player, piglin or
  anything within 8, or nothing, each tick); the brute's `RunOne`;
  hoglins', zoglins' and creakings' `SetEntityLookTargetSometimes(8,
  30..60)`, the allay's (6), the copper golem's (player 6, 40..80), and
  the player-only ones of armadillos, goats, frogs, axolotls and tadpoles
  (6, 30..60); `RandomLookAround(150..250, 30, 0, 0)` for armadillos and
  camels; the sniffer's `RunOne` look option. `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES`
  for the kinds whose looks pick mobs is sensed every 20 ticks (staggered
  by uid; the 8 nearest living mobs within 16 blocks in sight), their eyes
  refreshed every tick while looked at; players are read live.
- `Mob.playHurtSound` resets the ambient sound time: no idle call follows
  a hurt cry within 80 ticks (an existing test found the gap once the
  random stream moved).

### Spear leftovers (`src/entity/mob/spear.rs`, `src/weapons/spear.rs`)

- **The margin's block check** (`getManyEntityHitResult`): a box the ray
  only crosses within the 0.125 margin is hit only if, from that crossing
  toward the box's centre, the clip against block collision boxes still
  reaches the box; a ray starting inside the margin but outside the box
  misses (`AABB.clip`). Shared by mob stabs and players' jabs and charges.
- **Stabs on entities that aren't living** (`canHitEntity`: pickable and
  alive): minecarts and boats (`VehicleEntity.hurtServer`: ten times the
  damage, breaking past 40, with `entity_damage`; a cushion refuses a mob
  without mobGriefing), item frames, paintings and armor stands (a framed
  item pops out first; otherwise nothing without mobGriefing; with it a
  stand wobbles and breaks on a second hit within 5 ticks, since
  `DamageTypes.SPEAR` is in `#can_break_armor_stand`, and a frame or
  painting breaks), and End crystals (set off). Only damage applies
  (`stabAttack`'s knockback, its slowdown and `hurtEnemy` are for living
  targets); each is remembered for the contact cooldown; the mob's own
  cart isn't stabbed. Contacts use the cart's motion.
- **Body turn**: the move control's 90° a tick (above).

### Mounts and animals

- **Forced age for every animal**: `feed_growth` (the animals' feeding,
  pandas, ghastlings, baby nautiluses) ages up with `ageUp(seconds, true)`,
  keeping `forcedAge` (saved, as Track 3b made it) and its 40 ticks of
  `HAPPY_VILLAGER` stand-in particles, which the animal tick now counts
  and the animal pass draws; a fed baby no longer shows love hearts
  (Java's hearts are `setInLove`'s).
- **Players pushed by Nether lava** at Java's `FAST_LAVA` 0.007: the block
  source reports the dimension (`BlockSource::fast_lava`, true for a
  Nether `World`).
- **Horse jump sound**: `handleStartJump` plays `playJumpSound`
  (`entity.horse.jump`, the donkey's and mule's, a skeleton horse's
  `jump_water` in water) as new mount cues with original procedural
  stand-ins at Java's 0.4 volume.
- **Grazing**: `AbstractHorse.aiStep`'s `setEating` (1 in 300 a tick on a
  grass block, nobody riding; 50 ticks), `isImmobile` while it grazes,
  rearing or a lead's pull ending it, `eatAnim` and the mouth (`openMouth` when fed,
  `mouthAnim`), saved as `eating` only while set and sent to LAN clients
  beside `stand` (`eat`, `mouth`) for Track 7b's animation.
- **Textured mount preview**: the mount screen's model is drawn a model
  pixel at a time, with the entity shader's per-texel noise (`hash2`) or
  the box's material pattern, lit by face, as the player preview draws its
  skin.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **One goal selector for looks**: VoxelCraft has no general goal
  selector, so which goals hold MOVE and LOOK is read from the game's own
  state (chasing, panic, a stroll's path, a running table goal, any path,
  a look request this tick). For every kind but two the idle look goals
  rank below those goals, so this matches Java; the parrot's
  `LookAtPlayerGoal` (1) and the witch's (3) outrank goals the game models
  apart and here yield to them.
- **Eyes** are taken at 0.85 of a mob's height (Java's default; some
  kinds' `getEyeHeight` differ), a hunted mob's 1 block up, a player's by
  pose (1.62, 1.27 crouching, 0.4 swimming or crawling).
- **Sensing**: `NEAREST_VISIBLE_LIVING_ENTITIES` keeps the 8 nearest mobs
  in sight from a scan every 20 ticks; `LookAtPlayerGoal(Mob)` reads that
  scan rather than searching at `canUse`. The wandering trader's and
  villagers' trading player is the nearest one (the game keeps a trading
  flag, not the customer).
- **Brain writers not ported**: villagers' `SetLookAndInteract`,
  `ShowTradesToPlayer`, `InteractWith` and `WorkAtPoi` looks; the piglin's
  `SetLookAndInteract`; the celebrate `RunOne`'s stroll counts as doing
  nothing for 10 to 20 ticks; the sniffer's other `RunOne` options count
  as `DoNothing(5, 20)`; the camel's other writers (as Track 3b).
  `FollowTemptation`, `AnimalMakeLove` and `BabyFollowAdult` don't write
  `LOOK_TARGET`. Attack behaviours ask the look control directly each tick
  rather than through the sink.
- **Kinds' own conditions** not modelled: the fox's faceplant and interest
  (only sleep stops its looks) and its `resetXRotOnTick` exceptions, the
  bee's pollinating pitch, the panda's `setTarget`, armor stands'
  invisibility and markers, interaction entities (none exist).
- **Own heads kept**: the warden's `SetWardenLookTarget`, the shulker's,
  bat's, phantom's, ghast's and happy ghast's controls stay the game's.
- **Llama spit** asks the look control on the spit's tick only.
- **Piglin admiring**: the head is held 34° down through the look
  control, a stand-in for `PiglinModel`'s `ADMIRING_ITEM` pose (Track 7b's
  models could draw it).
- **Spear stabs** don't reach the dragon's parts.
- **Turtles** keep their own growth clock, so feeding a baby turtle keeps
  no forced age; horses, donkeys, mules and llamas grow by
  `handleEating` (unforced, as Java's `fedFood`).
- **Sounds**: the jump sounds are original procedural stand-ins.
- **Mount preview**: no world light, hurt tint, translucent or glowing
  parts (as before).
- The eating and mouth animations themselves are Track 7b's (the state is
  exposed as `mounts::State::eat_anim` and the LAN look's `eat` and
  `mouth`).

## What remains unverified

No one played any of this: every mob's head turning toward players and
around, bodies following heads, villagers looking at cats and each other,
piglins on hoglins and strider jockeys looking around, mobs stabbing carts,
stands and crystals, fed babies' particles, players in Nether lava, the
horse's jump sound and grazing, and the textured mount preview. No
screenshots were taken. Performance: each mob runs at most one idle look
search per full update (a 2% roll first), brain mobs one selector pass a
tick over the players and their sensed list, and the kinds that look at
mobs one sensor scan every 20 ticks (a mob-index visit within 16 blocks
and up to 8 sight rays); the preview draws about one quad per model pixel
of the visible faces, only while the mount screen is open.
