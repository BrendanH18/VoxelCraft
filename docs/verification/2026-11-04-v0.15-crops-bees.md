`world::crops` contains bounded growth, fruit support, neighbour detachment,
loot and shape helpers. The existing crop API appends `Crop::Beetroot` and
exposes each crop's maximum age. The shared player placement and pick-block
paths recognise all seeds. Farmland retains stems as well as crops when dry.

All four ordinary crops and the stems now use `CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed`:
base 1, centre dry/wet farmland 1/3, neighbouring farmland 1/4 of those
values, half speed for same-species plants on both axes or any diagonal.
Growth requires raw brightness 9 and succeeds with probability
`1 / (floor(25 / speed) + 1)`. Attached stems do not count as unattached
stems for crowding. Beetroot additionally gates normal growth to 2/3 of
ticks, with maximum age 3.

Unattached stems grow to age 7, then choose one horizontal neighbour on a
successful growth tick. Fruit needs air and the pinned support tag's
existing soil blocks. It places fruit and attaches to that facing.
Removing/replacing that fruit returns the attached stem to age 7.
Bone meal advances a young stem 2–5 ages, and reaching 7 calls one full
random tick (with its light and probability checks); age 7 and attached
stems reject bone meal. Stems require farmland and break on support loss.

Stem loot is three independent seed trials at `(age + 1) / 15`, attached
stems using age 7, unaffected by Fortune. Young beetroot drops one seed;
ripe beetroot drops one root plus `1 + Binomial(3 + Fortune, 4/7)` seeds.
Melons drop 3–7 slices plus 0–Fortune, capped at 9; Silk Touch keeps the
melon. Existing grass/fern drops remain wheat seeds only. Melon slice
crafts one melon seed; the existing pumpkin recipe gives four seeds;
six beetroots plus a bowl craft soup shapelessly.

The existing modern village crop palette now includes beetroot alongside
wheat, carrot and potato. Farmers recognise beetroot at age 3; the existing
harvest event handler replants it at age 0. Java farmers do not harvest
melon/pumpkin stems, so stems are intentionally excluded.

## Bee continuation after 901dcea

Bee slice implemented, unverified; focused headless checks have passed.

New modules `entity::bee`, `world::bees`, `entity::model::bee`,
`render::textures::bees`, and bee acquisition recipes. Allocations:
10844–10867 nests, 10868–10891 hives (honey level × 4 + S/N/E/W),
10892 honeycomb block, item 1281 honeycomb, texture layers 3008–3016,
MobKind::Bee appended at index 54 after Camel. Honey bottle 1073 reused.

Additional pinned sources: javap `Bee` and its pollinate/grow/enter goals,
`FlyingMoveControl`, `BeehiveBlock`, `BeehiveBlockEntity`,
`BeehiveDecorator`, `TreeFeatures`, `TreeGrower`, `FireBlock`; client jar blockstates,
bee_food/bee_attractive/bee_growables tags and hive/comb recipes.

Nests/hives each have 24 states, facing and honey 0–5. Original procedural
wood/fibre/honeycomb textures, original striped box bee with animated
wings and pollen, original synthesized harmonic buzzing. Bee attributes:
health 10, box 0.7 × 0.6, attack 2, movement 0.3/flying 0.6. Uses existing
flying navigation, common breeding/tempt/age pipeline and new hive goals.

Hives keep three occupants, persisted with their block through dimension
properties. Payloads use the existing mob serialization, including names,
effects and breeding state. Ages and love time advance by time in hive
on release. Occupants wait more than 600 ticks (empty) or 2400 (nectar);
night, rain and blocked exits prevent ordinary release. Nectar normally
adds one honey level, 1% two, capped at 5. Fire causes emergency release.
Only loaded/ticking hives advance; an active index excludes unloaded saves.
Pending emergency releases also persist if a save intervenes before the
next entity tick. Entering drops a lead and clears the stored leash.
Immutable Silk Touch components are deduplicated in a process-lifetime
interner to preserve the existing Copy stack API; unique payloads remain
allocated until exit.

Bee nectar follows flower visits; bees boost young crops/stems, sweet
berries and cave vines below them, limited to ten boosts per pollination.
Hives are found in a bounded radius of twenty. Successful stings apply
Poison I for 200 ticks on Normal, 360 on Hard, none on Easy; stinger loss
is acknowledged only when the player's damage transaction succeeds,
including shared shield interception and hurt immunity.
After stinging, every fifth tick checks Java's declining death denominator,
dealing its current health as generic damage, certain to trigger by tick
1200. Shared resistance/absorption effects still apply. Anger lasts 400–780 ticks and alerts nearby
bees. Flower feeding and babies use the shared animal pipeline.

Full hives yield three honeycomb with shears (one durability) or exchange
one bottle for honey bottle. Smoke prevents harvest anger; unsmoked use
releases and angers occupants. Silk Touch preserves hive occupants and
honey on the dropped stack; ordinary nest mining drops no nest, crafted
hives drop themselves. Honeycomb block uses four combs, hive uses six
planks and three combs. Comparators read honey level. Fire values:
nest (30,20), crafted hive (5,20).

Natural generation decorates eligible existing trees: forest/birch 0.2%,
flower forest 2%, plains 5%, meadow 100%. The brief omitted cherry and
mangrove decorators: the pinned TreeFeatures also supplies cherry 5%
and mangrove 1%, included here. Nests face south beside a log near the
first leaf layer, with a clear exit, containing 2–3 bees initially inside
for a random 0–598 ticks. The underlying tree layouts/RNG remain the
existing generator's, not Java's tree feature layouts/seed stream.
