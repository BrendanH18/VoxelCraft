`world::crops` contains bounded growth, fruit support, neighbour detachment,
loot and shape helpers. The existing crop API appends `Crop::Beetroot` and
exposes each crop's maximum age. The shared player placement and pick-block
paths recognise all seeds. Farmland retains stems as well as crops when dry.

All four ordinary crops and the stems now use `CropBlock.getGrowthSpeed`:
base 1, centre dry/wet farmland 1/3, neighbouring farmland 1/4 of those
values, half speed for same-species plants on both axes or any diagonal.
Growth requires raw brightness 9 and succeeds with probability
`1 / (floor(25 / speed) + 1)`. Attached stems do not count as unattached
stems for crowding. Beetroot additionally gates normal growth to 2/3 of
ticks, with maximum age 3.

Unattached stems grow to age 7, then choose one horizontal neighbour on a
successful growth tick. Fruit needs air and the pinned support tag's
existing soil blocks. It places fruit and attaches to that facing.
Removing/replacing that fruit returns the attached stem to age 7.
Bone meal advances a young stem 2–5 ages, and reaching 7 calls one full
random tick (with its light and probability checks); age 7 and attached
stems reject bone meal. Stems require farmland and break on support loss.

Stem loot is three independent seed trials at `(age + 1) / 15`, attached
stems using age 7, unaffected by Fortune. Young beetroot drops one seed;
ripe beetroot drops one root plus `1 + Binomial(3 + Fortune, 4/7)` seeds.
Melons drop 3–7 slices plus 0–Fortune, capped at 9; Silk Touch keeps the
melon. Existing grass/fern drops remain wheat seeds only. Melon slice
crafts one melon seed; the existing pumpkin recipe gives four seeds;
six beetroots plus a bowl craft soup shapelessly.

The existing modern village crop palette now includes beetroot alongside
wheat, carrot and potato. Farmers recognise beetroot at age 3; the existing
harvest event handler replants it at age 0. Java farmers do not harvest
melon/pumpkin stems, so stems are intentionally excluded.

## Differences, pending scope and unverified behaviour

- **Not implemented:** bee MobKind/model/sound/AI, nests/hives and their
  generation/occupants, honey collection, honeycomb/block, pollination and
  boosts, stinging/anger/breeding, Silk Touch occupants, all 56 waxed copper
  blocks, waxing/axe un-waxing/recipes, sign waxing, survival candles.
  Honey bottle 1073 and honey block remain existing content.
- Original crops use the engine's crossed-plant renderer, rather than
  Java's crop-row models. Attached art has directional silhouettes but
  does not reproduce Java's single bent plane orientation.
- Existing farmland only stores dry and wet, not moisture 0–7. The
  growth formula treats wet as Java moisture > 0; intermediate drying
  stages and the associated time to dry remain absent.
- Existing crop survival checks soil on support updates; Java's general
  low-light survival/removal rules are not fully represented. Growth
  itself checks the correct raw brightness.
- Village structures/palettes are existing approximations: this slice
  adds beetroot to their existing uniform palette, without porting Java's
  biome-specific processors or structure layouts. Legacy generation's
  historical farm palette is retained unchanged. Existing farmers replant
  freely and spill loot; inventory-based seed use/collection and planting
  empty farmland remain broader villager work.
- World random draws still use the engine's SplitMix stream, not Java's
  per-table/per-world RNG sequence. Drop distributions match the inspected
  tables; exact seeded sequences are not claimed. New crops use the
  shared per-item explosion-decay path.
- Crop-heavy frame time, LAN round-trip gameplay, unloaded-border growth,
  owner ordinary play and full bee/wax gameplay remain unverified.
