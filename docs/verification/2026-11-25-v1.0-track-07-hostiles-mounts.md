## What changed

### The Ender Dragon spawn egg (owner decision, 2026-11-25)

- `ender_dragon_spawn_egg` is item **1937**
  (`content::spawn_egg::ENDER_DRAGON`), the item after the Wither's egg,
  where v0.18 Track 4 kept it. Its colours are the 26.3 fixture's
  (`#1c1b1c`, `#9642a8`); it stacks to 64, is allowed in Peaceful (Java's
  `isAllowedInPeaceful` is true for the dragon) and has no recipe.
- **Creative placement:** Java 26.3's `CreativeModeTabs` lists the egg in
  no tab (`in_tab: false` in `spawn_eggs.json`), exactly like the Wither's
  egg, so it is in none here either; the Spawn Eggs tab keeps its 86 eggs.
  It is still in `Item::all_items` (the LAN palette), appended last.
- Its icon is the shared two-colour egg sprite, one more icon after the
  Wither egg's (the item icon ordering grows by one at its end).
- Using it (on a block face, a water or lava source, or from a dispenser)
  spawns a dragon no fight owns, through `Entities::spawn_egg_spawns`:
  dropped onto the floor by `EntityType.getYOffset` with the dragon's
  16 × 8 box, facing `wrapDegrees(random × 360)`, then its growl. On a
  spawner it doesn't set the spawner (spawners hold `MobKind`s); it spawns
  a dragon in front instead (difference below).
- `registry_gaps.json` loses its `ender_dragon_spawn_egg` entry.

### A dragon with no fight (`entity::dragon::wild`)

Java gives an egg-spawned or summoned dragon no `EnderDragonFight` (its
UUID isn't the fight's), so:

- It starts **`HOVERING`** (`EnderDragonPhaseManager`'s first phase): it
  holds where it appeared. Hovering counts as sitting, so its wings shove
  without hurting and knockback is ignored, but arrows still hurt it
  (`DragonHoverPhase` keeps the default `onHurt`). After more than 50
  damage (a quarter of 200) while sitting it **takes off**.
- Its fight origin is `BlockPos.ZERO`: it perches on the top of the
  `MOTION_BLOCKING_NO_LEAVES` column at (0, 0), and its 24 path nodes are
  Java's absolute rings around (0, 0) at `max(73, ground + 5/15/5)`, with
  `Mth.cos`/`Mth.sin` table values. With no crystals counted, closest-node
  searches and Java's A* (`findPath` over `nodeAdjacency`) use only nodes
  12–23: the holding pattern circles the radius-40 ring, lands one time in
  three, and strafes the player nearest the egg one time in two
  (`NEW_TARGET_TARGETING` and `NEAR_EGG_TARGETING` have no range).
  Takeoff and landing approach follow their phases' node rules.
- No boss bar, no exit portal, no gateway, no `DragonKilled`: `tickDeath`
  pays 500 experience (`floor(500 × 0.08)` every fifth tick past 150 and
  `floor(500 × 0.2)` at 200) and removes it. `checkDespawn` is empty, so it
  never despawns. Crystals within 32 still heal it.
- It can be hit by melee, arrows and blasts (`Hit::Wild`), draws with the
  End dragon's model and shadow, breathes fire and spits fireballs whose
  breath bottles collect, and replicates to LAN clients (`ender_dragons`).
- It saves in the dimension's properties as `ender_dragons` (JSON: position,
  yaw, health, phase, death time, sitting damage, as
  `EnderDragon.addAdditionalSaveData` saves its phase, death time and
  sitting damage). A strafe or charge loses its target on reload and goes
  back to circling, as Java's does.

**The End fight is unchanged.** Its dragon now runs through a shared
`Arena` (fight or no fight) built from the same fight fields; every phase
branch for a fight-owned dragon is the code it was. The only shared-code
edit is `checkWalls`: the dragon now skips breaking blocks only while
perched on the portal (as before, since it never hovers), and hovering
dragons break blocks as Java's do. All 20 existing `entity::dragon` tests
pass unchanged.

### FOO-04a: boats tick by tick (`entity::minecart::boat`)

Boats, chest boats, rafts and chest rafts now run `AbstractBoat.tick`:

- **Status** (`getStatus`): under water (flowing or still) when water rises
  past the box's top; in water when the bottom is below a surface (the
  highest becomes the water level); on land when blocks' collision meets a
  0.001-thin slab under the box (lily pads left out), with their mean
  `getFriction`; else in the air.
- **`floatBoat`**: gravity 0.04, friction 0.9 in water, 0.45 under still
  water, 0.9 under flowing water with a 7.0E-4 sink, the land friction
  (stone 0.6, ice and packed ice 0.98, blue ice 0.989, slime 0.8), 0.9 in
  the air; buoyancy `(level − y) / 0.5625 × 0.04 / 0.65`, then × 0.75. The
  tick a boat falls into water it snaps onto `getWaterLevelAbove() −
  0.5625 + 0.101` if there's room.
- **Paddling** (`controlBoat`) from the rider's **keys**, not a world
  direction: left/right add ∓1° to `deltaRotation` (which decays by the
  friction), forward adds 0.04, back takes 0.005, turning alone adds 0.005;
  the paddles row as Java's do (π/8 a tick, a splash or scrape at π/4). Top
  speeds are therefore Java's `0.04 / (1 − friction)`: 0.4 blocks a tick
  (8 m/s) on water, 2 on ice, about 3.64 (72.7 m/s) on blue ice.
- The rider's heading turns with the boat and stays within 105° of it
  (`positionRider`, `clampRotation`).
- **Water currents** push it (`Entity.baseTick`: the normalised flow ×
  0.014, as for any non-player entity).
- **Moving**: no step height, Java's axis order (`collideWithShapes`), slime
  and bed bounces at 0.8 of their restitution, the block speed factor (soul
  sand, honey), and `checkFallDamage`, which only resets the fall on landing.
- **Bubble columns** through `applyEffectsFromBlocks`, which a boat runs
  twice a tick: a column cell under air marks the boat above it, and after
  60 ticks there it is thrown up (vertical speed 0.6) or dragged down (−0.7)
  and emptied; a submerged column cell pushes it as any entity (±0.06 /
  −0.03 capped at 0.7 / −0.3).
- **Out of control**: 60 ticks under water eject the passengers.
- **Passengers** sit at Java's attachment points: `rideHeight` up (a third
  of 0.5625; 0.8888889 of it on a raft), less the passenger's own vehicle
  attachment (a player's 0.6; a mob's `ridingOffset`, halved for babies),
  0.2 ahead and 0.6 behind when two ride (animals 0.2 further forward and
  turned sideways when the boat is full), a lone passenger in the middle
  (0.15 forward in a chest boat or chest raft). The player's camera is
  therefore 0.3125 lower in a boat than before.
- No new passengers board while a player steers (`addNewPassengers`).
- **Breaking and drops**: a hit adds ten times its damage
  (`VehicleEntity.hurtServer`), so a fist takes five hits and a diamond
  sword one, for boats and minecarts alike; a broken boat drops its own item
  and a chest boat its contents (the old planks-and-sticks break is gone —
  26.3 has none). Storage is unchanged (27 slots, saved as before).
- Nothing new is saved: Java saves none of a boat's state beyond position,
  motion and rotation.

### FOO-04c: mounts

- **Exact equine travel** for ridden horses, donkeys, mules, skeleton
  horses and zombie horses (`entity::mob::equine`), on land: whole Java
  ticks of `travelRidden` — `getRiddenInput` (half the strafe, a quarter
  backwards), the rider's yaw, `executeRidersJump` (vertical speed =
  `JUMP_STRENGTH × scale × block jump factor + Jump Boost`, plus `0.4 ×
  scale` forward when moving forward; scale 1 for a 90%+ charge else
  `0.4 + 0.4 × charge / 90`), `travelInAir` with the `MOVEMENT_SPEED`
  attribute, `STEP_HEIGHT` 1, gravity 0.08 and drag 0.98. This replaces the
  continuous ride and its fitted launch speed.
- **Fall damage** follows `calculateFallDamage`, which floors:
  `floor((fall − 6) × 0.5)` (it used to round up, so a 7-block fall no
  longer hurts a horse). Only a fall that hurts the horse passes on, as each
  rider's own fall damage (`floor(fall − 3)` for a player), not the horse's.
  Slime cancels it and hay bales scale it by 0.2.
- **Zombie horsemen** (`ZombieHorse.finalizeSpawn`): the natural zombie
  rider faces the horse's way and holds an unenchanted **iron spear**.
- **Trap enchantments** were already in place (`SkeletonTrapGoal`'s iron
  helmet and fresh `mob_spawn_equipment` rolls on the bow and helmet); this
  track adds the test that checks them and their save.

### MOB-04c: Nether mobs

- **Strider jockeys** (`Strider.finalizeSpawn` for an adult natural spawn):
  one time in 30, while monsters spawn, a zombified piglin holding a warped
  fungus on a stick rides it, and the strider wears a saddle that always
  drops; else one time in 10 a baby strider rides it. Riders sit on the
  strider's 1.7-block attachment (the piglin 0.7 lower, its `ridingOffset`)
  and save with their link.

### MOB-04a: hostiles

- **Line of sight for targets** (`NearestAttackableTargetGoal` with
  `mustSee`): zombies, skeletons, creepers, spiders, slimes, silverfish and
  witches now take a player only in sight (eye to eye through collision
  shapes) and let go after 60 ticks out of sight. Reinforcements and mobs
  provoked by a hit keep chasing, as `setTarget` and `HurtByTargetGoal` set
  their targets outside that goal.
- Mob `travelInAir` friction now includes slime blocks (0.8).

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **Dragon:** the fight-less dragon uses the existing VoxelCraft flight
  code and its death rises in place (Java's `DragonDeathPhase` first flies
  toward the origin's perch). A spawner given the dragon egg spawns a dragon
  in front rather than turning into a dragon spawner. The egg's spawn growl
  is the growl sound, not `ENDER_DRAGON_AMBIENT`. Node heights come from the
  loaded world; until every node column is loaded, unloaded ones sit at
  y = 73 and are recomputed. A dragon in an unloaded chunk doesn't tick.
- **End fight finding (not changed, for the owner):** the End fight's nodes
  use `max(10, ground + lift)`, while 26.3's `findClosestNode` uses
  `max(73, …)`; its holding pattern is VoxelCraft's simplified ring walk,
  not Java's A*. Both are left as they were because this track was asked
  not to change the End fight; changing them is a later decision.
- **Boats:** `applyEffectsFromBlocks` uses the final box rather than the
  swept path; entity collisions (boats against boats) don't block the
  water-landing snap; the 105° clamp is applied once a tick, not on every
  mouse move; `Animal` is approximated by "breedable"; leashes on boats are
  not implemented; the minecart seat still ignores the player's 0.6
  attachment (a minecart, not a boat, gap).
- **Horses:** horses in water or lava keep the continuous ride; Speed and
  Slowness don't yet scale the ridden speed; the jump isn't refused while
  the horse rears (`isStanding`), which VoxelCraft doesn't model; camels
  are unchanged; a player's Jump Boost doesn't raise their safe fall as a
  rider.
- **Spears:** horsemen hold iron spears but no mob uses a spear's charge
  (`SpearUseGoal`); they melee as before.
- **Strider jockeys:** the zombified piglin doesn't steer the strider; the
  pack's later striders aren't rolled as babies (`AgeableMobGroupData(0.5)`).
- Local difficulty for trap enchantments still uses zero inhabited time.
