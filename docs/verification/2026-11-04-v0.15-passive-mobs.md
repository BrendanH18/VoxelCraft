**Task/reference discrepancy:** the supplied 26.3 `Bat.checkBatSpawnRules`
has no Halloween/date branch and no fixed sea-level cutoff. It requires

## Delivered behavior and allocation

- `MobKind::Mooshroom` (`mooshroom`), then `MobKind::Bat` (`bat`), appended
  after Camel in the enum and `ALL`. Existing names/indices are unchanged.
  The integrator must combine appended track variants in track order.
- Item **1300**, suspicious stew: one per stack, 6 hunger/7.2 saturation,
  always edible, 32 consumption ticks, bowl remainder. Crafted shapelessly
  from any two mushrooms, a bowl and a supported flower,
  or milked from a flower-fed brown mooshroom. It deliberately shares the
  existing mushroom-stew icon, as the Java items are visually identical.
- The mooshroom/bat slice used no block states or block texture layers.
  Original entity cuboids and shader noise provide both variants, calf
  mushroom suppression and folded/flapping bat wings. Saturation appends a
  dynamic HUD effect icon. Frog-family allocations are listed below.
- Stew effect component codes 1–11 are stable, saved in `Stack::entity_data`
  for this item only. Bucket items retain their existing payload meaning.
  The effect survives stacks, crafting results, drops, inventory persistence
  and existing serialized LAN inventory transport. `mooshroom` state saves
  the variant and stored flower code. Bat saves its resting flag, including
  natural bats that do not require persistence; flight targets are transient,
  as in Java. Existing chunk storage freezes unloaded entity timers.
- Wheat temptation/courtship, baby growth, panic, following parents and
  navigation reuse the cow goal table and attributes. Mooshrooms prefer
  mycelium for stroll targets. Adult loot is 0–2 leather and 1–3 beef,
  including existing fire cooking and looting paths; babies drop nothing.
- Adult shearing preserves health, name, age, persistence and leash, converts
  to a cow and scatters five mushrooms of the old variant. Shears wear one
  point through the authoritative player adapter.
- `Mob::lightning_swap(&mut self)` toggles red/brown on a living mooshroom.
  **Integrator hook:** call once per lightning bolt, deduplicating repeated
  flashes with the bolt identity as Java does. This track does not implement
  the lightning caller or its sound.

## Frog family continuation

Implementation reads `animal.frog.{Frog,Tadpole,FrogAi,TadpoleAi,
ShootTongue,FrogAttackablesSensor,Croak}`, `FrogspawnBlock`,
`TryLaySpawnOnFluidNearLand`, `LongJumpToRandomPos`,
`LongJumpToPreferredBlock`, `LongJumpUtil`, `LongJumpMidJump`,
`ConversionType`, `ConversionParams` and `LivingEntity.travelInAir`.
[frogs.json](../../tests/fixtures/java/26.3/frogs.json) pins selected tags,
variant selectors, loot, attributes and timers from the local 26.3 artifacts.

Allocations: `Frog` (`frog`) and `Tadpole` (`tadpole`) append after Bat.
State 11008 is frogspawn; 11009–11011 ochre, 11012–11014 verdant and
11015–11017 pearlescent froglight (Y/X/Z each). Item 1301 is the tadpole
bucket. Layers 3136–3145 are original procedural egg and froglight textures.
The original frog cuboids have three colors, eyes, legs, croaking throat
and animated tongue; tadpoles have a swimming tail. Their voice bank is
original synthesis; action sounds reuse original engine effects.

**Brief/reference discrepancy:** 26.3 variant selectors use biome tags,
not numeric temperature. Deep dark and deep frozen ocean select cold;
mangrove swamp selects warm. Selection happens at tadpole growth, including
3D cave biomes and dimension selectors. Tadpoles belong to CREATURE, have
six health, are not baby frogs and grow at 24,000 loaded ticks. Adult frogs
have ten health, ten attack damage, movement speed one and step height one.
Swamp/mangrove spawn entries are weight 10, group 2–5. Slimeballs tempt both
species, accelerate tadpole growth and breed adults. Pregnancy replaces
ordinary birth; both adults receive the ordinary breeding cooldown.

Spawn lays on water adjacent to land and hatches after a saved scheduled
3,600–11,999 ticks into 2–5 persistent tadpoles. Removing water, falling
blocks and pistons destroy it without loot, including Silk Touch. Exact
1.5/16 outline, ray picking and mesh height use reserved detail coordinate
31 without expanding the existing packed quad. Froglights have all nine
axis states, emission 15, hardness 0.3, hoe preference and ordinary drops.

Frogs accept leads through the shared player actions. Tadpoles require
persistence, including explicit summons. Tadpole bucket payloads retain
age, health, name and age lock through
inventory serialization and release. Growth creates a healthy persistent
frog, retains UID/name/leash/effects and selects the destination variant.
Frog state, tadpole age and pregnancy use existing entity persistence and
chunk-unload rules; transient tongue/croak/jump poses are replicated but
restart at rest. Hatch ticks reuse the existing saved block tick queue.

Tongue sensing rejects larger cubes, scans visible prey within ten blocks
on a twenty-tick interval, approaches with modifier two, catches within
1.75 blocks, pulls at 0.75 blocks/tick, eats on catch tick seven and releases
the animation on tick eleven. Killed prey disappear immediately. Small
slimes eaten by frogs drop exactly one slimeball (a separate Java loot
entry from the ordinary 0–2 drop); magma cubes drop the matching froglight,
with mob-loot and recent-player XP gates. A five-entry, 100-tick unreachable
memory and 100-tick hunt timeout bound repeated attempts.

Long jumps use weighted positions within 4 horizontal/2 vertical blocks,
shuffled 65/70/75/80-degree angles, Java's shortened target, ballistic arc
sweeps and float constants, forty-tick preparation, Jump Boost and the
100–140 tick cooldown. Mid-jump motion discards all friction and retains
gravity. Frog falls subtract five from ordinary fall damage. Shared
amphibious navigation applies the frog's shallow preference and refuses
water-border corner shortcuts; swimming uses the shared Java controls.

Differences still open: the engine schedules a bounded activity/goal table
instead of the full Java memory/sensor Brain scheduler. Shore selection
chooses a nearby valid laying cell with a bounded scan, rather than Java's
random land-search behavior. Jump candidate selection tries at most twenty
positions in one activity update, rather than distributing tries across
Brain ticks. Size-change clearance uses a bounded local collision search
rather than Java's voxel-shape free-position solver. Existing navigation
and entity-collision gaps apply. Golden dandelion age-lock interaction is
not registered (plant absent in this tree); a saved/bucket age-lock flag is
retained. Original art/audio intentionally differ from Mojang assets.
Owner play, full Brain equivalence and mob-heavy frame performance remain
unverified. Checks for this continuation are recorded below when complete.

## Known differences and unverified behavior

- Original geometry, procedural colours and audio deliberately differ from
  Java assets. Cow voices serve mooshrooms; bats have original synthesized
  chirps in Java's Neutral sound category (`AmbientCreature` inherits the
  default source). Specialized mooshroom milking/eating/shearing/conversion
  sounds and the bat takeoff level-event sound are not yet implemented.
  Ambient voice scheduling retains the engine's existing schedule.
- All reference flower-effect mappings exist, but wither roses, torchflowers,
  open/closed eyeblossoms and golden dandelions are not registered blocks in
  this checkout; their input acquisition therefore remains unavailable. This
  track does not add those plants or the deferred sniffer. The other twelve flower
  inputs work through crafting and brown-mooshroom feeding.
- Registered suspicious-stew recipes carry their flower's result component
  through the recipe book. Java 26.3 uses `#mushrooms` twice, so matching
  mushroom colours are valid. Creative gives an effect-free default stew.
- Bat navigation mirrors sign-based target steering, ceiling tests and Java
  air travel. Water/lava movement retains a compact drag/gravity adaptation;
  fluid-edge movement, slime/honey callbacks, block-trigger immunity and
  exact animation poses and sound timing have not been compared tick by tick.
  Nearby player sensing uses the existing shared target/line-of-sight representation.
- Existing shared age/breeding/navigation and loot RNG streams retain their
  engine differences from Java. Matching constants and tests do not prove
  seed-identical Java behavior.
- Owner play, a real LAN session, controller hardware, and the 1080p 240 FPS
  acceptance gate have not been performed. Shared session tests cover the
  authoritative actions used by desktop, CLI, controller and LAN adapters;
  they do not stand in for the owner's ordinary play.
