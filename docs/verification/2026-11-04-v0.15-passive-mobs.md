## Mooshrooms

Red/brown mooshrooms use cow attributes, navigation, panic, wheat temptation,
courtship and baby growth. Mushroom-fields spawning uses Java's mycelium/light
predicate and weight 8, groups 4–8. Matching parents mutate with probability
1/1024; differing parents choose one variant. Adults give milk or stew, brown
adults store one flower effect, and shearing produces a cow and five matching
mushrooms while preserving health, identity and age. Babies give no loot/XP.
The public `Mob::lightning_swap` hook toggles the variant; callers must deduplicate
repeated flashes of the same lightning bolt.

Item 1300 is suspicious stew, one per stack, 6 hunger/7.2 saturation, always
edible, 32 ticks, bowl remainder. It crafts from red/brown mushrooms, a bowl
and a supported flower. Effect codes 1–11 save in the item payload and in
the mooshroom's state. No block states or block texture layers are allocated.
The model is original procedural geometry; the item shares the existing
mushroom-stew icon. Saturation adds one food/two saturation per active tick.
