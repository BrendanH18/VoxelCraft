| Fixture | Extractor | Test | Entries |

| `fuel_integrations.json` (regenerated) | `extract_java_fuel_fixtures.py` | `tests/full_fuel_integrations.rs`, `examples/fuel_inventory.rs` | 347 fuels |

`java_block_properties.py` evaluates `Blocks.<clinit>` symbolically and also
resolves each `Items` field to its registry ID (`item_registry_ids`). Only
three fields differ from their IDs: `DRY_SHORT_GRASS` (`short_dry_grass`),
`DRY_TALL_GRASS` (`tall_dry_grass`) and Mojang's misspelt
`CUT_STANDSTONE_SLAB` (`cut_sandstone_slab`). The old fuel extractor named
items by field, so the fuel fixture listed the two dry grasses under the wrong
IDs; it was regenerated and those two entries are the whole diff. Every
extractor regenerates byte-identical output, checked again under a different
`PYTHONHASHSEED`.

## What was compared, and counts

| Area | Java entries | Compared (registered) | Matched before | Fixed | Allow-listed | Unregistered |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Mining (hardness, harvest with 38 held items, dig progress) | 1,286 blocks | 1,046 blocks over 13,592 states | 1,014 | 29 | 3 | 240 |
| Fuels | 347 | 323 | 322 | 1 (block of coal) | 0 | 24 |
| Compost | 122 | 101 | 100 | 1 (straw bed) | 0 | 21 |
| Repair | 75 | 60 | 55 | 5 (stone tools) | 0 | 15 |

Mining compares each state's `Block::hardness`, `mining::can_harvest` and
`mining::dig_progress` (on the ground, dry) for a hand, a stick, shears and all
35 tools against Java's rules: requires-correct-tool, then
`incorrect_for_<tier>_tool` denies and `mineable/<kind>` allows; swords and
shears allow only cobweb; speeds from the tier, the sword and shears tags.
Every registered VoxelCraft name maps to a Java block except the 11 retired
invented blocks and the `unknown` placeholder. The 240 unregistered Java
blocks are exactly `registry_audit`'s missing blocks, apart from 7 names the
audit can't match but the test resolves through state aliases (carrots,
potatoes, iron chain, grass block, short grass, snow block, moving piston).

Fuels, compost and repair also check the reverse: no VoxelCraft item burns,
composts or takes a repair material unless Java's data says so.

Allow-list (mining): air, water and lava. Java never mines them (their outline
shape is empty, so the crosshair can't target them); VoxelCraft keeps them
unbreakable rather than carrying Java's unused hardness of 0 and 100.

The 24 unregistered fuels (poplar wood, dry grass, leaf litter, jukebox,
wooden spear), 21 compostables (poplar, dry grass, eyeblossoms, torchflower,
pitcher, wither rose, glow lichen, bush, firefly bush, cactus flower, leaf
litter, wildflowers, red shrub, shelf mushroom) and 15 repairables (chainmail
armor, spears, elytra, mace, shield, wolf armor) belong to later releases, so
the reserved item IDs 1190–1194 were not used.

## Behaviour a player will notice

- Java 26.3 removed `requiresCorrectToolForDrops` from the ender chest, bell,
  brewing stand, iron door, light and heavy weighted pressure plates and
  pointed dripstone: they now drop when broken by hand. Their loot tables
  agree (no tool condition).
- Dead coral, dead coral fans and dead coral wall fans need a pickaxe to drop.
- Snow blocks need a shovel to drop snowballs.
- Stone, stone brick, blackstone and polished blackstone brick slabs take 2.0
  to break (Java's `registerSlab(id, base, 2.0F)`), not their base's 1.5.
- Turtle eggs take 0.5 to break and bamboo shoots 1.0 (both were instant).
- A block of coal burns for 16,000 ticks (80 items).
- Straw beds compost at 85%.
- Stone tools repair with blackstone and cobbled deepslate as well as
  cobblestone.
