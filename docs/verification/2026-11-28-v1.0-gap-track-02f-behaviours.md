## What changed

### 1. Villagers and brutes (`src/entity/mob/brain_walk.rs`, `src/entity/mob/head.rs`, `src/entity/villager.rs`)

- **The work package.** A working villager no longer walks onto its job
  site and stands there (the game's `v.goal` for work is gone):
  - `SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory(JOB_SITE, 0.5, 9, 100, 1200)` at
    priority 2: with no walk target, more than 9 blocks (`distManhattan`)
    from the job site it walks back (0.5, 9 short); more than 100 it
    heads for a spot 15 blocks toward it (`DefaultRandomPos.getPosTowards`,
    up to 1000 tries, else it gives the job site up); a job site it hasn't
    reached for 1200 ticks (`CANT_REACH_WALK_TARGET_SINCE`, which the walk
    sink now sets from the first path that falls short and clears on
    reaching a target) is given up.
  - The `RunOne` at priority 5, after the look `RunOne`, with Java's
    weights (farmers: `HarvestFarmland` 2 and `UseBonemeal` 4; others 5
    and 7): `WorkAtPoi` 7 (within 1.73 of the job site's centre, every
    300 ticks on a coin flip; it looks at the job site and holds the
    `RunOne` for its 60 ticks while within 1.73; the village pass restocks
    when `shouldRestock` allows, as before), `StrollAroundPoi(JOB_SITE,
    0.4, 4)` 2 (a land spot within 8 and 6, every 180 ticks),
    `StrollToPoi(JOB_SITE, 0.4, 1, 10)` 5 (every 80 ticks),
    `StrollToPoiList(SECONDARY_JOB_SITE, 0.5, 1, 6, JOB_SITE)` 5 (a random
    farmland cell from `SecondaryPoiSensor`, every 40 ticks: within 4
    across and 2 up or down of a farmer; every 100 ticks), `HarvestFarmland`
    (a farmer with `mobGriefing` and farmland near: a ripe crop or empty
    farmland within a block of it, walked to at 0.5, 1 short; a ripe crop
    within 1 of its centre breaks; then the next cell 20 ticks later; 60
    ticks a run, 40 between runs) and `UseBonemeal` (which never starts:
    see the differences). The one-shot strolls start (and end the
    `RunOne`'s pick) even while on their cooldown, as Java's return true.
  - A `RunOne`'s running option stops when another activity's `RunOne`
    runs (`HarvestFarmland.stop` erases its look and walk targets).
- **`PlayTagWithOtherKids`** (play package, priority 5, before its
  `RunOne`, which now needs no babies in sight, `VISIBLE_VILLAGER_BABIES`
  absent): with babies in sight (`Seen.baby`) and no walk target, one tick
  in ten a baby another baby chases (`Seen.chasing`, that baby's
  `INTERACTION_TARGET`, refreshed every tick) runs off to a land spot within
  20 and 8 that is in a village (ten tries, 0.6); otherwise it chases the
  baby with the fewest chasers among those one to five chase, or else the
  closest baby, as its `INTERACTION_TARGET`, looking at it and walking to
  it (0.6, 1 short). The village test reads the village sections within
  three of the baby, noted by the sense pass every 20 ticks.
- **`TradeWithVillager`** (idle and meet packages, priority 3): the
  villager `INTERACTION_TARGET` is now a mob (`Walking::interaction`), set
  by `InteractWith` (villagers and cats), `SocializeAtBell` and the chase.
  The gate runs `TradeWithVillager` while it is a villager in sight: when
  it starts and each tick they are within `sqrt(5)` it locks both
  villagers' looks and walks on each other (0.5, 2 short; the partner's
  through the next sense pass, `Walking::lock_with`); after its 60 ticks
  (it ticks 61 times) or once the target isn't a visible villager, the
  gate erases the target. `SetLookAndInteract` and `SocializeAtBell` wait
  while a villager target is held.
- **Piglin brutes' idle** (`PiglinBruteAi.createIdleMovementBehaviors`,
  after the look `RunOne`): `RandomStroll.stroll(0.6)` 2, `InteractWith` a
  piglin 2 or a brute 2 (within 8, 0.6, 2 short), `StrollToPoi(HOME, 0.6,
  2, 100)` 2, `StrollAroundPoi(HOME, 0.6, 5)` 2 and `DoNothing(30, 60)` 1,
  walked by `MoveToTargetSink`; `HOME` is the spot a bastion resident spawned
  at (the saved `home`). The game's walk back beyond 12 blocks no longer
  applies to brutes (piglins and hoglins keep it).

### 2. The fox (`src/entity/fox.rs`, `src/entity/mob/fox.rs`, `src/entity/animals.rs`, `src/audio/fox.rs`)

- **`DefendTrustedTargetGoal`** (target priority 3, before the prey goals
  at 4 and 6, one full update in `reducedTickDelay(10)`): the first player
  the fox trusts who is in the world, hurt by a mob within the last 100
  ticks (`lastHurtByMob`, from the melee hits that already tell wolves,
  `EntityEvent::OwnerHurt`) in a hurt it hasn't answered, gives it that mob
  as its target if within 32 blocks: the fox calls `FOX_AGGRO`, sets
  `defending` and wakes. Its melee and leap goals act on that
  target (stalking and pouncing are for chickens and rabbits only). Losing the target ends the defence
  (`setTarget(null)`), as does `clearStates`. While defending it calls
  again one tick in 20, and doesn't avoid players or wolves, panic
  (`FoxPanicGoal`) or follow its parent (`FoxFollowParentGoal`).
- **`FoxStrollThroughVillageGoal`** (9): at night, on its full updates,
  one time in `reducedTickDelay(200)` within six sections of a village, the
  entity pass picks the spot closest to a village of ten land spots within
  15 and 7; the goal starts with it unless the fox sleeps, sits, defends,
  has a target or is ridden, clears its states and walks (speed 1) to the
  surface 10 blocks toward a point 60% of the way there (or a random
  surface spot within 8), unless the spot is within 10.
- **Thunder** (`level.isThundering()`, already on `Entities`): it wakes the
  fox (`Fox.tick`), and `SeekShelterGoal` under open sky in a storm looks for
  cover at once, without its 100-update interval, daylight or village
  conditions.
- **Its own sounds** (`fox::Cue`, original procedural stand-ins in the
  established voice style, `src/audio/fox.rs`): `entity.fox.aggro`,
  `entity.fox.bite` (`FoxMeleeAttackGoal` biting), `entity.fox.sniff`
  (one tick in 20 on the way to berries), `entity.fox.spit`,
  `entity.fox.eat`, `entity.fox.sleep` (its ambient call asleep) and
  `entity.fox.screech` (at night, one ambient call in ten with no player
  within 16 blocks of its box, volume 2); `NEUTRAL`, subtitled through
  their Java events.
- **Mouth items**: Java's `canHoldItem`: food replaces a held non-food item
  once the fox has held it a tick, the old item spat out a block up at its
  look direction (40 ticks before pickup, `FOX_SPIT`); a held food is eaten
  only while it has no target, is on the ground and is awake, chewing
  (`FOX_EAT`, one tick in ten) from 28 seconds.
- **Faceplant particles**: a faceplanted fox kicks up the block it is in one
  tick in five (level event 2001, `EntityEvent::DestroyEffect`: the
  block's break particles and sound, the block staying).

### 3. The goat (`src/entity/mob.rs`, `src/entity/animals/lifecycle.rs`)

- **`Pose.LONG_JUMPING`**: from its jump's launch until it lands (or
  enters water) a goat is long jumping (`animals::State::long_jumping`,
  `Mob::goat_long_jumping`), and its box (collision, hits, navigation) is
  0.7 of its standing size, adult or baby (`Goat.getDefaultDimensions`).
  LAN clients get `"goat_long_jumping": true` in the mob's look.

## Differences and remaining gaps

- **`UseBonemeal` never starts**: it needs bone meal in the villager's
  inventory, and the game's villagers pick up only food (bread, carrots,
  potatoes, beetroot), not bone meal, wheat or seeds (Java's
  `Villager.WANTED_ITEMS` and farmer's `requestedItems`). For the same
  reason `WorkAtComposter` makes no bread and composts no seeds,
  `HarvestFarmland` doesn't plant seeds on empty farmland (a farmer still
  walks to it, as a Java farmer without seeds does), and the game's own
  harvest event replants a broken ripe crop at once in place of Java's
  planting from the farmer's seeds; `TradeWithVillager` throws no food,
  wheat or requested items (the game's own sharing pass shares food) and
  doesn't gossip (the game's own gossip pass does). `playWorkSound` (the
  professions' `entity.villager.work_*`) isn't played.
- **Trading lock**: the partner turns to the villager on the next tick (the
  sense pass), not the same tick.
- **Play tag**: `findSomeoneBeingChased` sorts a hash map, so Java's pick
  among equally chased babies has no fixed order; the game takes the first
  in sight order. A chased baby's position comes from what the chaser
  sees, else the next sense pass.
- **Brutes**: a brute without a saved `home` (spawn eggs, commands, old
  saves) has no `HOME`, so its home strolls never start; Java's
  `finalizeSpawn` sets it wherever a brute spawns. Setting it would change
  saves, which this track leaves alone.
- **`SetWalkTargetFromBlockMemory`'s give-up** clears the job site in the
  brain; the village pass's claims follow on its next pass. The meet and
  rest packages' block-memory walks stay the game's own walks.
- **Fox defence**: only mob attackers are tracked (`OwnerHurt` comes from
  mob melee hits; a projectile or another player hurting a trusted player
  isn't recorded), so a fox doesn't target players or skeletons that shot
  its player. `TRUSTED_TARGET_SELECTOR` (the attacker has hurt something)
  always holds for the recorded attackers.
- **Village stroll**: each of the ten candidate spots is itself the best of
  `RandomPos`'s ten by walk value before the village score picks among
  them; the goal holds while its path lasts, where Java's ends at its next
  update and leaves the path walking (so the fox's priority 10 to 13 goals
  can't start meanwhile here).
- **Eating**: the eat event's item particles (entity event 45) aren't
  drawn (the game has no item particles); a chorus fruit's
  `entity.fox.teleport` isn't played (the game's fox eats without
  consuming effects). `ClimbOnTopOfPowderSnowGoal` stays unported.
- **The goat's long jump** is still the game's stand-in jump (straight up,
  every 30 to 60 seconds): only its pose and box are Java's. Java's
  `LongJumpToRandomPos` (a target up to 5 across and 5 up, the ballistic
  arc, the long-jump and landing sounds) isn't ported.
- 2e's other remaining differences stand.
