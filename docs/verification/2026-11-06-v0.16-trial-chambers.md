## Implemented behaviour

**Structure.** `world/trial_chambers.rs` places generation 3 chambers with
Java's random spread (an independent Python `java.util.Random` oracle checks
four regions), a start at uniform -40..=-20 with the floor one below,
depth 20 and distance 116 limits, and the biome check at the start's 3D
biome (every overworld biome but the deep dark). Pools and connector names
follow Java: the start pool `trial_chambers/chamber/end` (corridor ends),
then `trial_chambers/corridor` (second plate, intersections, first plate,
atrium, entrance), hallways (`trial_chambers/hallway`, weight 150 for
chambers, fallback rubble) and chambers capped with `entrance_cap`. The 22
original templates use tuff bricks, polished and chiseled tuff, waxed
copper grates, cut and chiseled copper and waxed copper bulbs. Processors:
every bulb is lit and weathered by Java's rule order and position random
(0.1 oxidized, 0.333 weathered, 0.5 exposed); containers become barrels or
dispensers (ceiling dispensers face down) by their table. Pool aliases are
drawn once per structure. Chests, barrels and dispensers use the Java
tables; spawners get configurations; vaults register their block entities.
Natural mob spawning is empty inside any piece (`spawn_overrides`).
`/locate structure trial_chambers` and the CLI's `locate` find them.

**Trial spawner.** `world/trial/spawner.rs` holds Java's 14 configurations
(normal and ominous, with builder defaults) and the saved state;
`entity/trial.rs` runs `tickAndGetNext` each tick for loaded spawners: the
six states, scans every 20 ticks phased by `BlockPos.asLong`, line of sight
for the first player and range-only for later ones (range 14, no Creative
or Spectator), the 40-tick detection buffer, target total and simultaneous
mobs scaled by additional players, untracking past 47 blocks or on death or
unload, spawn position and checks, 40-tick shutter delay, one reward
ejection every 30 ticks per detected player from the weighted
`loot_tables_to_eject`, the 36000-tick cooldown, peaceful,
`spawner_blocks_work` and `spawn_monsters`. Spawned mobs are persistent;
`finalizeSpawn` runs only for id-only entries (baby zombie and slime
entries skip it). Ominous: Trial Omen, or Bad Omen converted to Trial Omen
for 18000 ticks per level, turns a spawner ominous, discards its mobs and
uses the ominous configuration; ominous item spawners appear every 160
ticks above a random mob or player and fire projectiles down or drop items.
Cooldown ends by clearing the ominous flag. Lights follow the states.

**Vault.** `world/trial/vault.rs` and `entity/trial.rs`: activation 4 and
deactivation 4.5 blocks, refreshed every 20 ticks, excluding rewarded
players; key insertion only while active (normal key / ominous key), the
15-tick fail-sound buffer, the rewarded player list (oldest dropped past
128), unlocking for 14 ticks, ejection one stack per 20 ticks with the
display item following, display cycling every 20 ticks, and the light
levels. Rewards use `chests/trial_chambers/reward*` with their persistent
random sequences. The shared `PlayerSession::interact` path serves
keyboard, controller, CLI and LAN players; Creative keeps the key.

**Items and effects.** Trial keys, ominous trial keys, breeze rods, wind
charges (recipe: one rod to four), ominous bottles 0–4 (32-tick drink,
Bad Omen for 120000 ticks at the amplifier), the trial explorer map sold by
journeyman cartographers (12 emeralds and a compass, 12 uses, 10 xp,
radius 100 chunks). Bad Omen and Trial Omen are appended effects.

**Breeze.** 30 health, 0.6 × 1.77 box, hostile, spawner-only, 10 xp,
`entities/breeze` (1–2 rods for a player kill plus round(Looting × U(1,2))).
The brain follows the fight activity order: Shoot (15-tick charge, shot at
0.7 blocks a tick with inaccuracy 5 − 4 × difficulty, 4-tick recovery,
10-tick cooldown, 16-block range, needs `BREEZE_SHOOT`), LongJump (on the
ground or in water, target in 24 blocks and farther than 4, four air blocks
above, not on honey, a point behind the target snapped to the surface with
sight, 10-tick inhale, Java's five angles and 1.4 blocks/tick limit via
`LongJumpUtil`, landing sets jump cooldown 10 or 2 after a hurt and
`BREEZE_SHOOT` for 100 ticks), ShootWhenStuck (in water), Slide (away when
inside 4 blocks, else behind the target or into the middle circle, 20–40
ticks, then `BREEZE_SHOOT` for 60). Snowballs and eggs that hit a breeze
turn back at half speed (`ProjectileDeflection.REVERSE`); wind charges do
not; breeze charges cannot hurt breezes. Saved: pose and every memory.

**Wind charges.** Players throw at 1.5 blocks a tick (inaccuracy 1) with a
10-tick cooldown shown on the hotbar; breezes shoot theirs. Both fly
straight, deal 1 damage to what they hit and burst; LAN clients see them
as thrown kinds 3 and 4. `entity::wind::wind_burst` is the shared burst:
radius 1.2 and knockback × 1.22 (player's) or 3.0 and × 1.0 (breeze's),
explosion exposure and distance falloff, explosion knockback resistance for
players, no damage, no block breaking, items unaffected; it toggles wooden
and copper doors, trapdoors and fence gates, presses buttons, flips levers,
rings bells and puts out candles found by the blast rays. A player's own
charge sets the fall impulse: the next fall counts from the hit height,
with Java's 40-tick grace and reset rules.

## Differences and gaps

- Template catalogue is original and reduced (22 templates): Java's
  slices, addons, decor, reliefs and its multi-level chamber assemblies are
  folded into fewer pieces. Decorated pots are deferred: their positions are
  plain chiseled tuff. Beds are red rather than random colours.
  `terrain_adaptation: encapsulate` is approximated by solid template
  shells; caves outside a shell can still meet it.
- Swords from `equipment/trial_chamber_melee` are rolled but zombies do not
  hold weapons in this engine; chainmail has no item, so the armor set is
  carried on the mob (`ArmorKind::Chain`) without an item form. Trims are
  not drawn.
- Slime `Size: 2` (size 3) clamps to size 2 in this engine.
- Spawn checks use the engine's collision, fluid and line tests; Java's
  `SpawnPlacements` rules are permissive for trial spawners and this
  matches them except for custom rules (none in the configs).
- The breeze runs on the shared mob physics (gravity, step-up), not a
  Java brain scheduler; `getPosAway` and the slide walk use simplified
  sampling and the shared navigator. No idle stroll yet.
- Wind charges are not fired by dispensers; minecarts, boats and
  decorations ignore bursts; `onExplosionHit` for beehives and creaking
  hearts is not applied (they have no wind effect in Java either).
- Presentation: no spinning display mob inside spawners, no display item
  inside vaults, simplified flame particles, original placeholder sounds.
  The fall-impulse state is not saved across restarts.
- Raid Omen and raid captains are v0.17.
