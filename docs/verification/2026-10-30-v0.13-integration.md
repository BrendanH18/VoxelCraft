## Integration changes

- Shared registry lines (texture count, `Block::base`, palette,
  replaceable, emission, hardness, fire odds, bone meal, silk-touch
  drops) combined by hand. Texture layers: underground 2768–2784, plants
  2832–2845, bubble columns 2864–2865; `tex::COUNT` ends at 2866.
- `nearest_biome` decides surface and lush or dripstone biomes from
  climate alone (the density track's change) and reads a column height
  only for the deep dark, which needs it.
- Options files from before the off-hand (no `key_key.swapOffhand` line)
  list flying on `F`, now the swap-hands key. `Bindings::migrate` moves
  flying to its new default `R` so one press doesn't do both
  (`options_from_before_the_off_hand_move_flying_off_f`).
- The fire sweep test now lists glow lichen (15/100) and the new plants
  (60/100), which the tracks added in parallel.

### Chunk generation speed

`--bench --rd 8 --seed 12345`, merged tree, one thread, 588 chunks on fresh
generators:

| Place | Generation 1 | Generation 2 | Ratio |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Origin | 0.410 ms/chunk (2,441 chunks/s) | 0.542 ms/chunk (1,843 chunks/s) | 1.32× |
| Mountains | 0.509 ms/chunk (1,964 chunks/s) | 0.745 ms/chunk (1,342 chunks/s) | 1.46× |

Streaming a generation 2 world at `rd=8` on 9 workers: 3,516 chunks
loaded and 2,879 meshed in 0.70 s. Both ratios are inside the track's
1.5× target. They are higher than the density track measured alone
(1.16–1.22×) because the underground and feature passes run on top. The
frame-time gate stays with v0.20.

## Review

Three rounds with Codex GPT-6.1-Sol (approved). Fixed, each with a test:
controller and LAN shields lowered every tick (shared hands now own the
tick; the agent ticks only when they didn't); fresh lava springs never got
lava ticks (the chunk is installed before its fluids wake); totems refused
magic deaths (only `/kill` and the void bypass them); Wither deaths
skipped the totem; off-hand food took the click from a main-hand shield or
bow, also after an interrupted meal; off-hand fishing casts lost their
bobber; off-hand buckets, snowballs and eggs repeated while use was held.
`generated_jungle_fluids_settle_after_neighbor_updates` now runs on
generation 1, since generation 2's lava springs keep flowing past its
1,200 ticks.
