# v0.7.0 play-test checklist

Automated checks pass on `work/v0.7-assessment` (release 1111 and headless 1024
tests, desktop LAN harness 13/13), but none of this has been played. Work
through this list in a release build (`cargo run --release`) before tagging
v0.7.0. Note anything that looks, sounds or feels wrong, with steps, in the PR.
A new world exercises fresh generation; also open one 0.6.0 world to check that
it loads.

## Combat and survival

- [ ] Swing a sword repeatedly: early hits are weak, the crosshair bar fills,
      and full-charge hits sweep nearby mobs. Fall onto a mob for a critical.
- [ ] Bow draw and release, crossbow load and fire, and Multishot/Piercing/Quick
      Charge from an enchanting table or anvil. Check the arm poses in third
      person (F5).
- [ ] Craft copper tools and armor, wear the armor (worn look), smelt an iron
      or copper tool into a nugget.
- [ ] Eat, drink a potion, fish (the hook pulls a mob), sleep in a bed and set
      the respawn point; die and respawn.
- [ ] Right-click doors, chests, furnaces and a villager; walk away from an open
      chest until it closes.

## Building and mining

- [ ] Mine with each tool type and with the wrong tool; break blocks with shears.
- [ ] Place snow layers on snow, barrels while looking up and down, hay bales
      (jump onto them), saplings with bone meal (two stages).
- [ ] In Adventure mode, use a door, button, lever and composter.

## Light, mobs and the world

- [ ] Explore a dark cave by day: monsters spawn; place torches and they stop.
- [ ] Watch villagers at night (they lie in beds, close doors) and farmers
      harvesting; trade until a villager levels up.
- [ ] Visit the Nether: piglins, biome edges, fog per split-screen view, basalt
      deltas. Visit the End: terrain is lit by its ambient light, not too dark
      or too bright.
- [ ] Find a jungle temple: the lever puzzle and both tripwire traps work.
- [ ] Ride a horse, a boat (break it with a hard landing) and a minecart through
      water.

## Controller and LAN

- [ ] With a controller: mine, place, attack, open the inventory and a chest,
      sleep, die and respawn, ride a cart.
- [ ] Open to LAN with a second computer if available (or a second window):
      join, trade items through a chest, PvP on and off, follow through a portal.
      Reopen the world to LAN: cheats and PvP settings are remembered.

## Performance and saves

- [ ] Play for 15 minutes with the F3 screen open: no stutters while walking,
      flying or loading new chunks.
- [ ] Save and quit, reopen, and check inventory, position and placed blocks.
- [ ] Open a world saved by 0.6.0.

The remaining known issues are listed in `packaging/RELEASE-NOTES.md` and in the
[defect inventory](2026-10-13-v0.7-defect-inventory.md); they are not
regressions to report.
